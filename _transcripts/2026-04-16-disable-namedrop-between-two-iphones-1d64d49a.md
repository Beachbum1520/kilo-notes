### CLAUDE (2026-04-16T17:40)
 This is **NameDrop** — Apple's feature that shares contact info when iPhones are held close together. Easy fix:

On **each iPhone**:

1. Open **Settings**
2. Tap **General**
3. Tap **AirDrop**
4. Toggle off **Bringing Devices Together**

Do this on both phones and they'll stop trying to swap contacts.
