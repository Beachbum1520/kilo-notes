**Tidal Wave is the better of the two, but for a Cadillac the better call is a hand wash, specifically Shine Again Auto Detailing.**

**Tidal Wave (530 Bullsboro Dr)**
- 4.5★ on 1,466 reviews, the strongest track record in town
- Pre-wash prep by staff, and a towel bay for your own wipe-down
- Recent complaints: dryer roller barely working, cars coming out half-wet, and one long-time member seeing new scratches

**Rich's (1600 Newnan Crossing Blvd)**
- 4.0★ on 291 reviews
- A Gulf Coast chain with over 24 locations in 5 states
- Has a $5 basic wash on Mondays, which is today
- Worse damage reports: sidewall gouges on a new Tahoe, a cracked bumper piece with no remedy from management, and complaints that they've cut back on soap

**Better options**
- **Shine Again Auto Detailing (Werz Industrial Blvd):** 4.9★ on 141 reviews. Hand wash and ceramic coating, so nothing touches the paint but people. It costs more and needs an appointment. Open Mon–Sat 9–5.
- **Riptide (Baynard Park):** 4.3★. Staff towel-dry the exterior even on the basic wash. This is a good middle ground if you want a tunnel.

Every friction tunnel in town has some scratch complaints. Rich's damage reports are more serious, and management reportedly didn't stand behind them. If the Caddy's paint matters to you, go to Shine Again. If you just need it clean today, go to Tidal Wave and dry it off yourself in their towel bay.

Then **go to Tidal Wave**, or **Riptide** if you want someone else to dry it.

- **Tidal Wave:** It has the best overall track record, and its prep crew handles the worst grime before the car goes in. The drying is its weak point, so plan on a couple of minutes in the towel bay. That also keeps water spots off a dark finish.
- **Riptide:** It's rated a little lower (4.3★), but staff towel the exterior after every wash, even the basic one. That fixes the half-wet problem Tidal Wave keeps getting dinged for. The recent reviews on management are mixed.

Skip Rich's. Its damage complaints are the most serious, and management reportedly doesn't stand behind them.

If you wash often, a membership at either place pays for itself in 2–3 washes a month. Save a hand detail for a couple of times a year to take out the swirl marks any tunnel will leave eventually.
