### CLAUDE (2026-07-27T21:02)

Oban 14 Year, 750ml — expect roughly **$75–$90** at most US retailers.

- Total Wine lists it at $79.99, which is about the national benchmark
- Wine-Searcher's average is ~$86 ex-tax, with individual shops running $71–$77
- One retailer shows MSRP at $84.99; boutique shops go as high as $93.99, and outliers hit $106.99

In Georgia's control-free market, Total Wine or Costco will be at the low end — under $80 is a fair price, over $90 means you're paying a convenience premium. Worth noting: your bottle is the standard 43% ABV (86 proof) core expression, not the rum-cask or Distillers Edition variants, which run $100+.

### CLAUDE (2026-07-27T21:03)

Different animal from the 14 — this is the Distillers Edition, and the 2000/2015 vintage is discontinued, so "retail today" splits into two numbers.

**Current-vintage Distillers Edition (what's on shelves now):** roughly $100–$120 for 750ml in the US.

**Your specific bottle (2000 distilled / 2015 bottled, OD 164.FA):** Whiskybase shows a lowest listed price of about €97 — call it $110–$140 landed from European specialists, higher with box and pristine label. It's the Montilla Fino sherry-cask finish, rated 84/100 across 90 ratings — respectable but not a collector-chase bottle.

Two things worth flagging: it doesn't appreciate much. Distillers Edition is produced in real volume every year, so it's a drinker, not an investment — these routinely turn up at general auction houses where fees alone eat 25%+. And the bottle in your photo looks empty or nearly so, which drops the value to essentially zero unless someone wants the box.
