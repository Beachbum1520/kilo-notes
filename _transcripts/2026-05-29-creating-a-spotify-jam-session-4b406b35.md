To start a Jam from your playlist (you need Spotify Premium to host, but whoever joins can be on the free tier):

On mobile:
1. Start playing a song from the playlist you want to share.
2. Tap the Connect to a Device icon at the bottom of your screen.
3. Tap **Start a Jam**.
4. Tap **Invite**, then share the link, show the QR code, or tap phones together via Bluetooth.

On desktop: Right-click on the playlist (or click the three-dot menu), then click Start a Jam and hit Invite to share the link/QR code.

A couple of things worth knowing: anyone in the Jam can add songs to the queue, pause, skip, and control volume from their own device — but only if you (the host) turn those permissions on. Each person can listen on their own device from anywhere, or you can all play through one shared speaker. And if you (the host) leave, the Jam ends for everyone.

One catch to flag: to join *remotely* (i.e., your friend listening on their own phone somewhere else), they need Premium too. Free users can join, but generally for in-person/same-speaker sessions.
