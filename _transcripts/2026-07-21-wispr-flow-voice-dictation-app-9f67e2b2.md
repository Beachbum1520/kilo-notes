You're thinking of **Wispr Flow**. It's a voice dictation app that's blown up over the past year — Wispr recently raised $81M on the back of it.

To be precise about what it does: it's not really an app for *talking to* AI — it's an AI-powered voice keyboard. You press a hotkey or activate it, speak naturally, and it transcribes into whatever app you're in — Slack, Gmail, Docs — while stripping filler words and adding punctuation and formatting automatically. So people use it to dictate emails, messages, and prompts to ChatGPT or Claude far faster than typing. It works on iPhone inside any app, and claims roughly 4x the speed of typing, plus Mac and Windows with support for 100+ languages.

The name confusion is common — OpenAI's speech-recognition model is called Whisper, which is a different thing (Wispr Flow-type apps are often built on models like it).

If you're picturing something where you actually converse with an AI by voice, that's more likely ChatGPT's voice mode. But if you've heard people raving about dictating everything instead of typing, Wispr Flow is the one.
