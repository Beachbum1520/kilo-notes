### CLAUDE (2026-07-25T15:11)

Yes — a few good options on iPhone:

**Built-in (free):** Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → turn on **Speak Screen**. Then open the PDF in Files or Books and swipe down with two fingers from the top of the screen. It reads the visible page and auto-advances. Zero cost, decent Siri voices.

**Speechify** — the most popular paid option. Best natural voices, handles scanned PDFs via OCR, syncs position across devices. Free tier is limited; premium runs ~$140/yr.

**Voice Dream Reader** — the veteran choice. Better control over speed, voice, and text highlighting; strong for long books. Subscription now, but cheaper than Speechify.

**NaturalReader** — middle ground, generous free tier.

One catch worth checking first: if your PDF is a *scanned* book (images of pages rather than real text), Speak Screen won't find anything to read. You'd need Speechify or Voice Dream, which OCR it. Easy test — try selecting text in the PDF. If you can highlight words, you're fine with the free built-in route.
