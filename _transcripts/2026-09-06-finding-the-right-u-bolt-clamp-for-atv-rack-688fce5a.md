### CLAUDE (2026-09-06T14:26)
This is a **U-bolt clamp / muffler-style U-bolt bracket** — specifically what's often sold as a "double U-bolt exhaust clamp" or "U-bolt saddle clamp." The style you have (two U-bolts sandwiching a flat mounting plate around a round tube/bar) is most commonly listed under:

- **"U-bolt exhaust clamp"** or **"muffler clamp U-bolt"**
- **"round pipe U-bolt bracket mount"**
- **"ATV cargo rack U-bolt clamp"**
- **"tube clamp U-bolt mounting bracket"**

Since this is holding a rack to a round tube frame, also try:
- **"ATV/UTV tube clamp cargo mount"**
- **"round bar U-bolt clamp kit"**

A few tips to nail down the right size on Amazon:
- Measure the **inside diameter of the tube** the U-bolt wraps around (looks like ~1"–1.25" based on the photo).
- Measure the **bolt thread size** (the broken ones look like maybe 5/16" or 3/8").
- Search adding that size, e.g. "1 inch U-bolt clamp 5/16-18" — this narrows results fast and skips the gun-mount clutter.
