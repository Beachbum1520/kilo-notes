I should keep my response brief and mobile-friendly.Two places, depending on your app version:

**In the Reels feed (fastest):** Open the Facebook app, tap the Reels tab, tap the three dots (or profile icon) in the top-right corner, then tap History (or "Watched reels" on older versions). It shows roughly the last 30 days.

**Via Activity Log (full video history):** Profile picture → Settings & privacy → Activity log → **Videos you've watched**. This lists all videos and Reels with exact date/time, and it's also where you can delete individual entries or clear the whole watch history — that part works best on the desktop site.
