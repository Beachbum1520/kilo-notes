I'm looking at a potential iPhone 11 listing from someone in the Philippines — ₱10,500 for a 128GB used model in Cebu. I should verify this against current market rates to see if it's a reasonable price.

Looking at the used iPhone 11 market in the Philippines, ₱10,500 sits right in the middle range—not a bargain, but fair pricing depending on condition. The actual value hinges on battery health, whether Face ID functions properly, if it's NTC-registered or openline, and any repair history.₱10,500 is fair market, not a steal. Comparable private-sale listings in the Philippines for an iPhone 11 128GB run roughly ₱8,000–12,500 depending on condition — resale floors have fallen to around ₱6,500–9,500 for rougher units, while dealer/refurb units with warranty run ₱13,950–18,950.

So the price only makes sense if the unit checks out. Before handing over cash:

- **Battery health** — this is the biggest price driver. At 10,500 you should be getting 85%+. Many listings at 8–9k are sitting at 75–80%.
- **Face ID and True Tone working** — broken Face ID knocks ~2,000 off value; missing True Tone often signals a screen replacement.
- **Openline/factory unlocked, no iCloud lock** — have them sign out of Apple ID and factory reset in front of you.
- **Check IMEI** against the box and settings; verify it's NTC-approved, not a smuggled/blacklisted unit.

One more consideration: it's a 2019 phone that is on its final major iOS version, with only security patches ahead and app compatibility likely dropping from late 2026. Fine as a staff phone, backup, or kid's device — not a great buy if it needs to last 3+ years.

If battery is below 85% or it's unit-only with no box, counter at ₱9,000–9,500.
