FRIDAY – LOWER B (HOME) – kids-duty edition

2 sets each leg – 30s per hand – 8 reps – 2 RIR – rear foot on the bench

2 sets – green loop band anchored low (bench leg), loop around ankles, lie prone – 12-15 reps – 2 RIR
Notes: Imperfect sub, honest hamstring pump. Log "band sub."

Single-Leg Calf Raise:
1 set each leg – bodyweight on a stair edge – 12 reps – primer
2 sets each leg – 30 DB in same-side hand – 10-12 reps – 2 RIR – full stretch, 1-count pause
