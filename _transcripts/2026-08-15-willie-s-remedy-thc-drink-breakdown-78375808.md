### SCOTT (2026-08-15T18:49)
Cbd vs thc?

### CLAUDE (2026-08-15T18:49)
The core difference: **THC is intoxicating, CBD isn't.**

- **THC (Delta-9)** — binds directly to CB1 receptors in your brain. Produces the buzz, euphoria, altered perception, and at high doses the anxiety and paranoia. This is what got you at the gummy dose.
- **CBD** — doesn't bind CB1 the same way. No high, no matter how much you take. Users report calm, reduced tension, less body ache. Effects are subtle enough that plenty of people can't tell if it's working.
- **CBG** — the third one in most of these blends. Non-intoxicating, less studied, generally marketed for clarity and focus.

**The part worth knowing:** CBD does not cancel out THC, despite how these blended products are marketed. Research on inhaled cannabis at common CBD:THC ratios didn't find CBD reliably reduced THC's acute effects, and separate oral research using much larger CBD amounts found it could actually increase THC exposure and strengthen certain effects. So the 2mg of CBD in a Willie's shot isn't a safety net.

The practical takeaway: don't use the CBD number as permission to ignore the THC number. A drink with 10mg THC and 10mg CBD is still a 10mg THC drink.

**For your goal** — mild, fast, controllable — you want the THC number low and the format divisible. CBD content is close to irrelevant to whether you get overshot again.

One more thing: CBD-only products won't produce the effect you're describing at all. If you want the buzz without the overshoot, you want low-dose THC, not CBD.
