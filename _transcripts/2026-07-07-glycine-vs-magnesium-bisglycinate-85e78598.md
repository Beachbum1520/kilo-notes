### CLAUDE (2026-07-07T01:49)
Looking at these two supplements in his sleep stack—BulkSupplies Glycine is pure glycine at 3 grams per teaspoon, while Thorne's product delivers 200mg of elemental magnesium bound to glycine per scoop, so they're serving different purposes despite both containing glycine.Two different compounds that happen to share a word.

**BulkSupplements Glycine** — pure glycine, the amino acid itself. 3g per tsp, nothing else. This is your bedtime 3g glycine dose.

**Thorne Magnesium Bisglycinate** — magnesium bound to glycine for absorption. Per scoop you get 200mg elemental magnesium plus roughly 2.5g of glycine as the carrier. The glycine is the delivery vehicle; the magnesium is the payload.

Practical overlap: your nightly stack calls for 400mg magnesium + 3g glycine. Two scoops of the Thorne gets you 400mg Mg and ~5g glycine as bisglycinate — at that point the separate glycine scoop is optional, not additive in any meaningful way. One scoop Thorne + 1 tsp glycine lands ~200mg Mg / ~5.5g glycine. Neither combo is wrong; just don't stack two Thorne scoops plus the full glycine tsp thinking you need all three doses — you'd be double-counting the glycine.
