The test is cheap. Six snap traps, peanut butter, set them perpendicular to the wall with the trigger end against the baseboard — mice run along walls, not across open floor. Kitchen behind the fridge and under the sink, your office along whichever wall backs to the outside.

Yes — corners are among the best spots, but placement matters more than location.

Other things worth doing in there:
