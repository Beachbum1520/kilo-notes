### CLAUDE (2026-04-22T21:23)
 A few methods that work, in order of how much effort they take:

1. **Push down on the rim of the top bucket** at multiple points around the edge while pulling up — this flexes the rim and breaks the vacuum seal that's holding them together.
2. **Slide a thin, stiff item** (putty knife, butter knife, screwdriver) between the rims and run it around to let air in.
3. **Hot water trick** — set the stuck pair upside down in a few inches of hot water for a minute. The top bucket (now on the bottom) expands slightly while the inner one stays cooler, breaking the seal.
4. **Twist, don't just pull** — grab the top bucket and rotate it back and forth while someone else holds the bottom one steady on the ground (or wedge it against a wall).
5. **Two people, opposite directions** — one pulls up while the other pushes down with a foot on the bottom bucket's rim.

The vacuum seal is almost always the real problem, not friction, so anything that lets air in between them will free them fast.
