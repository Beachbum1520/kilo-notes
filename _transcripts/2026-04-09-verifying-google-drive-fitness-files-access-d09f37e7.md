**.FIT files are not supported** by Claude for upload. The .FIT format (used by Garmin, Wahoo, etc.) is a binary file format, and Claude can only read text-based or document formats like PDF, DOCX, TXT, CSV, etc.

**Here's the practical workaround:**

The best path is to **convert your .FIT files to CSV or JSON first**, then upload those. A few ways to do that:

1. **Garmin Connect / Strava** — If your workouts are already synced there, you can bulk export data as CSV directly from the platform.

2. **fitdecode or fit2csv** — Free command-line tools that batch-convert .FIT files to CSV. If you have hundreds of files, a simple script could convert them all at once.

3. **GPSBabel** — A GUI tool that can convert .FIT to other formats.

Once converted to CSV, you could upload batches into a Claude Project and I could analyze trends, performance over time, training load, recovery patterns — whatever you're after.

What platform did the workouts come from — Garmin, Wahoo, something else? That might help me point you to the easiest export path.
