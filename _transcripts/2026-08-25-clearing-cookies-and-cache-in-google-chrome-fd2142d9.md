### CLAUDE (2026-08-25T15:40)
That menu is the main Chrome dropdown — "Privacy and security" lives inside **Settings**, not this list.

Click **Settings** in that same menu, then on the Settings page look for **Privacy and security** in the left sidebar (or scroll down on mobile/narrow view). From there, click **Clear browsing data**.

Or skip it entirely: just type **chrome://settings/clearBrowsingData** into the address bar and hit Enter — that opens the clear data dialog directly.
