### CLAUDE (2026-05-08T21:58)
 On iPhone, burst mode automatically activates when you use the timer — that's by design and there's no toggle to disable it in iOS. The camera takes 10 rapid shots so you have options to pick from.

Workarounds:
- **Use Live Photo instead**: Turn on Live Photo (icon at top), set your timer, and you'll get a single Live Photo instead of a burst.
- **Just delete the extras**: After the shot, open the burst in Photos, tap "Select," choose your favorite, and delete the rest.
- **Third-party camera apps**: Apps like Halide or ProCamera let you use a self-timer without forcing burst.

The Live Photo trick is the cleanest workaround most people land on.
