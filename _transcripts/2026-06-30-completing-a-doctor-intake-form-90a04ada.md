The user is asking how to fill out a medical intake form field about occupations, and I can draw on what I know about their background to provide a practical example of how to complete it.For this field, you'd list your job title and a brief note on what the work involves. Doctors ask about occupation because certain jobs carry health-relevant exposures or stressors (sitting, screen time, travel, physical labor, chemicals, etc.), so a short descriptor is more useful to them than just a title.

Given your background, something like:

**Current:** "Senior Director, Hospitality Operations — managed Wi-Fi/technology company. Desk-based/office role, primarily computer and phone work, some travel."

**Past:** "24+ years in hospitality technology, progressively senior management roles. Primarily office/desk-based."

If you want to keep it short, even just this works:

> Senior Director, Hospitality Operations (technology/management). Desk-based role with significant screen time and occasional travel. 24+ years in the industry, all office/management roles.

A couple of notes:
- The "desk-based / lots of screen time / travel" detail is the part a physician actually finds clinically useful, so it's worth including.
- Skip the "Don't know / Not sure" checkbox — that's there for patients who genuinely can't characterize their work history, which isn't you.
