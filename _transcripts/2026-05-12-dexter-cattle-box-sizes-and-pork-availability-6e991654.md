### CLAUDE (2026-05-12T02:26)
 Here are the typical averages:

**Hanging weight (dressed carcass)**
- Dexter: ~250–350 lbs
- Angus / Hereford: ~700–900 lbs

**Take-home cuts (after processing, ~60–65% of hanging weight)**
- Dexter whole: ~160–220 lbs
- Dexter half: ~80–110 lbs
- Dexter quarter: ~40–55 lbs
- Commercial whole: ~450–550 lbs
- Commercial quarter: ~110–140 lbs

That's the crux of it — a Dexter quarter (~40–55 lbs) is closer to what most customers expect from an *eighth* of a commercial steer. By the time you split it into the standard quarter mix (roasts, steaks, ground, etc.), you end up with one or two of each cut, which isn't a satisfying box for the customer or worth the processing split for us.

Halves and wholes on Dexters land in the same usable range as quarters and halves on commercial beef, which is why that's where we draw the line. Boxes solve this cleanly — customer gets the variety, we get to portion across more families.
