The real difference is stiffness. Wet concrete pushes hard sideways, and a 1x4 is only 3/4" thick — it'll bow out between stakes and leave you with wavy, bellied edges unless you stake it every 16" or so and back it up. A 2x4 at 1.5" thick holds a straight line with stakes every 2–3 feet and takes the abuse of screeding without flexing.

For a one-time pour this size, use 2x4s. They're cheap, you can reuse them, and the edges come out straight with a lot less fuss. Save the 1x4s for the stakes if you want, though 2x4 stakes or rebar hold better in the ground.
