### CLAUDE (2026-07-10T17:03)
Looking at options for managing an iPod mini without iTunes — there are several solid alternatives depending on the platform, like MusicBee or foobar2000 on Windows, Finder on Mac, or specialized tools like Copytrans and Sharepod that handle the classic iPod database format, plus open-source options like Rockbox or gtkpod on Linux.That's an iPod mini, and yes — a few ways to skip iTunes entirely:

**Third-party managers** (Windows): MusicBee, Foobar2000 (with the iPod plugin), or CopyTrans can write directly to the iPod's database. Drop in MP3 podcast episodes or audiobook files and sync.

**Linux/Mac**: gtkpod or Rhythmbox on Linux handle the classic iPod database fine.

**Rockbox** (the power move): The iPod mini is fully supported by Rockbox, a free open-source firmware. Once installed, the iPod becomes a drag-and-drop USB drive — copy any MP3/AAC/audiobook file straight over in a file explorer, no sync software at all. It also adds bookmarking, which is great for long audiobooks. It's reversible if you want stock firmware back.

Two practical notes: you'll need a USB-to-30-pin cable, and podcasts need to be downloaded as MP3 files first (most podcast sites or a tool like gPodder can grab the raw files from an RSS feed). Audiobooks from Audible won't work due to DRM unless played through iTunes-authorized software — DRM-free sources like Libro.fm or LibriVox transfer fine.
