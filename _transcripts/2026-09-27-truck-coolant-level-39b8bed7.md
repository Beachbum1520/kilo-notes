### CLAUDE (2026-09-27T14:38)
Yes. On GM surge tanks, that molded arrows-and-book symbol marks the cold fill level. With the engine cold, the coolant should sit at the arrows, not above them.

In your photo, the pinkish tint (DEX-COOL color) looks like it sits below the arrows, in the lower part of the tank. That's hard to confirm through translucent plastic with the sun behind it, though. Tapping the tank or shining a flashlight through the side will show the line more clearly.

If it is low:
- **Only open it cold.** It's a pressurized degas bottle and will spray if the engine is warm.
- **Top up with DEX-COOL 50/50 premix** to the arrows. Don't use green coolant.
- **Watch whether it drops again.** If you have to add more than a little over a few weeks, it's losing coolant somewhere. Check the hoses, the water pump weep hole, and for white exhaust smoke.
