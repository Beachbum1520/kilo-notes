I'm thinking through troubleshooting steps for an iPhone video saving error, likely stemming from Messages or Photos app issues, possibly related to iCloud Photos sync or storage constraints. I'll format this as a concise, mobile-friendly list of solutions.This usually happens when saving a video from Messages, Instagram, or similar apps. Since a reboot didn't fix it, try these in order:

1. **Check storage** – Settings > General > iPhone Storage. If nearly full, free up space first.
2. **Check iCloud Photos sync** – Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Photos. Toggle "Sync this iPhone" off, wait 30 seconds, toggle back on. A stuck sync state is the most common culprit.
3. **Check photo permissions for the app** – Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > [the app you're saving from] > set to "Full Access" (or at least "Add Photos Only").
4. **Sign out/in of iCloud** – Settings > [your name] > Sign Out, then sign back in. Fixes stubborn sync corruption.
5. **Alternate route** – If it's from Messages, try long-pressing the video > Save, or forward it to yourself via email/AirDrop to a Mac and back.

If it's only one specific video failing, the file itself may be corrupted or still downloading from the sender — ask them to resend it.

Which app are you saving from? That narrows it down.
