The cya goes in a sock, or can I add directly to pool?

### CLAUDE (2026-06-28T16:51)
 **Always sock method. Never directly to the pool.** This is a hard rule for your setup specifically.

**Why sock is mandatory for you:**

1. **Vinyl liner pool.** Granular CYA is highly acidic when concentrated. Dumped directly, it sinks to the bottom in piles that can bleach or damage vinyl where it sits.

2. **Sand filter.** CYA dissolves slowly (days to a week+). Granules pulled into a sand filter clog it and dissolve unevenly through the bed. Pressure spikes, uneven distribution, filter problems.

3. **No main drain.** Your pool only skims surface. Granules on the bottom have no return path to circulation — they just sit there until they slowly dissolve in place, with all the localized acidity that creates.

**Sock method, step by step:**

1. Get an old sock (clean, no holes), or buy a paint strainer bag (Home Depot paint aisle, ~$3 for a pack) — paint strainer bags work better than socks, less risk of holes
2. Pour CYA into the sock — don't overfill, give the granules room to circulate water
3. Tie the open end
4. Hang the sock in the skimmer with the top above water, OR hang it in front of a return jet so water flows through
5. Best practice: squeeze the sock periodically over a few days to help dissolution
6. Leave in place 5-7 days until empty
7. Retest CYA ~10 days after the sock is empty (give it time to fully circulate and convert)

**For 10-15 lbs of CYA:** don't put it all in one sock. 4-5 lbs per sock max. Multiple socks in skimmer is fine, or do it in batches over a few weeks.

**Common mistakes to avoid:**

- Don't backwash while sock is dissolving (you'll flush dissolved CYA out before it circulates)
- Don't add CYA during a SLAM (you flagged this yourself in past notes — hard TFP rule)
- Don't trust the test reading until 7-10 days after sock is empty
