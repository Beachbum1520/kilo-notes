### SCOTT (2026-07-30T11:21)
I have widex moment hearing aids and comdex device. Streaming Spotify. Every several seconds it’s beeping in my ear. Double beep. How do I turn this off.

### CLAUDE (2026-07-30T11:22)

Short answer: there's no user-facing setting to mute that tone. It's a status alert generated by the COM-DEX or the aids themselves, so you have to fix what's triggering it — or have your audiologist disable indicator tones in the fitting software (Compass GPS).

Most likely triggers, in order of probability:

1. **COM-DEX battery low.** It repeats a warning tone until charged. Easiest to rule out — charge it fully (solid green), unplug, retest.
2. **Unstable Bluetooth link.** Each drop/reacquire produces a tone. The COM-DEX beeps to signal a connection issue when it isn't receiving a stable Bluetooth audio stream. Phone in a back pocket or across the room is enough to cause this — keep it on the same side of your body, front pocket.
3. **Phone notifications routing through the stream.** Every alert plays into your ears. Put the phone in Do Not Disturb / silence notification sounds and see if the beeping stops instantly. If it does, that's your answer.
4. **Hearing aid battery low** (if you're on disposables) — swap both, since the alert can be hard to localize.

If 1–3 don't resolve it: unpair COM-DEX from the phone, delete it from the Bluetooth list, reboot the phone, re-pair. Failing that, factory-reset the COM-DEX.

If it survives all of the above, it's a hardware/firmware fault worth taking to your provider — unexplained repeating beeps on COM-DEX are a long-standing complaint with no user-side fix, and your audiologist can either turn off the indicator tones or start a warranty claim.
