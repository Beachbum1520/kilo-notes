### CLAUDE (2026-08-27T17:04)
Yes — since Cox becoming Spectrum/Charter is a corporate rebrand, not a role change, updating the company name is accurate and worth doing. The notification issue is avoidable:

1. **Turn off broadcast first.** Go to Settings → Visibility → "Notify your network when you're in the news" (or the "Share profile changes" toggle) and switch it off before editing.
2. **Edit the existing entry, don't add a new one.** Click the pencil icon on the "Senior Director, Hospitality Operations" entry and just change the company field to Charter Communications/Spectrum. Editing a field within an existing position does not trigger LinkedIn's "started a new position" congratulatory notification — that only fires when you add a brand-new position entry.
3. **Leave dates/title alone.** Keep Sep 2022–Present intact so it reads as continuity, not a fresh start.
4. **Turn the toggle back on after**, if you want it on generally.

One thing to decide: do you want the title itself to stay "Senior Director" for now, or update it if/when the VP promotion Jady promised actually gets put in writing? I'd hold off changing title until that's real — company rebrand is a factual update, title change should wait for the paperwork.

### CLAUDE (2026-08-27T17:10)
Good distinction to make — these aren't interchangeable, and the choice affects who can find you and how accurate it reads.

- **Charter Communications** — the parent/legal entity. Safe, correct, but generic; recruiters searching for the consumer brand won't necessarily land on it.
- **Spectrum** — the consumer-facing brand. Widely recognized, but broad (residential internet/TV/mobile), and doesn't reflect that you're in the enterprise/hospitality side.
- **Spectrum Business** — the actual business unit Blueprint RF rolls into. This is the most *accurate* description of where you sit organizationally, and it's meaningful to anyone in your industry (hospitality tech, MSPs, BPO) who'd recognize "Spectrum Business" as the enterprise arm doing managed WiFi for hotel brands.

Given you're actively positioning for external opportunities where hospitality-vertical and enterprise-managed-services credibility matters, **Spectrum Business** is the strongest choice — it's specific, accurate, and signals the right thing to the exact audience (Allbridge, Cloudstaff, other hospitality-tech players) evaluating you. "Charter Communications" reads more like a general telecom generalist, which undersells the vertical expertise you're leaning on.

One caveat: verify Spectrum Business actually exists as a selectable/linkable Company Page on LinkedIn (search it in the company field when editing). If it doesn't resolve to a proper page, LinkedIn won't auto-populate the logo, and it may read as user-entered text rather than a verified employer. If that's the case, fall back to Spectrum with "Spectrum Business" folded into your role description instead.
