### CLAUDE (2026-05-18T20:27)
 

Only on the A321T (transcon) configuration, not regular A321s. The A321T has 10 lie-flat Flagship First seats in a 1-1 layout and 20 lie-flat Flagship Business seats in 2-2, used on premium transcon routes like JFK–LAX/SFO and BOS–LAX. Standard A321s (190 seats) have recliner first class, not lie-flat.

Worth knowing: AA is phasing out the A321T and replacing it with the A321XLR, which has lie-flat business but no separate first class cabin. Currently the XLR is only flying JFK–LAX on limited dates.
