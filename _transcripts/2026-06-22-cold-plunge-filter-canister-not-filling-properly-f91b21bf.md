When you open a filter housing and reseat it, two things commonly happen: the lid O-ring doesn't seat back in its groove (so the canister pulls air and won't hold a full water column), or you've just got trapped air that hasn't purged yet. Either one shows up as a canister that won't fill like it used to.

How to tell if it's actually a problem vs. fine:

1. **Listen to the pump.** Smooth hum = fine. Gurgling, sucking, rattling, or surging = it's cavitating (pulling air), and that's worth fixing now before it wears the pump.
2. **Check flow at the return jet** in the plunge. Same as before = you're fine. Weaker = air in the line is choking it.
3. **Check cooling.** If it's still hitting your set temp in normal time, the system's moving enough water.

If all three are normal, a partial fill / air gap at the top of the canister is cosmetic — leave it.

If the pump's gurgling or flow dropped, fix the seal:

1. Power off. Pull the canister.
2. Inspect the O-ring — clean it, make sure it's fully seated in its groove, no twist, no debris. A light film of silicone grease helps it seal.
3. Reseat and snug it with the wrench (the chrome bracket on the unit) — firm, not gorilla-tight.
4. Top off the plunge water level while you're at it — low water lets the intake sip air, same symptom.
5. Restart and watch it fill. Should pull up to its normal level within a minute or two as air purges.
