### CLAUDE (2026-05-24T01:23)
 Those are **Bali cattle** (also called *Bos javanicus*), a breed domesticated from the Banteng. They're native to Southeast Asia — very common in Bali, Indonesia and surrounding regions.

A few things that give them away:
- **Rich reddish-brown coat** (females and young; bulls darken with age)
- **White stockings** on all four lower legs
- **White rump patch**
- **Small, upright ears** and refined facial features
- Lean, athletic build

They're highly valued in tropical Asia for both meat and draft work, and are well-adapted to hot, humid climates and rough forage — quite different from your Dexters back home! The blue rope halters suggest these are owned/working animals, likely in a village setting.
