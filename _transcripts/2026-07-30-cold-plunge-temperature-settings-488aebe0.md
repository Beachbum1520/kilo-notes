### CLAUDE (2026-07-30T09:37)
Honest read: yes, but a much narrower list than the marketing.

**Holds up:**
- Acute mood/alertness — norepinephrine roughly doubles-to-triples and stays up for hours. This is the most replicated effect and it's the one you actually feel.
- Cold habituation — real, measurable, blunted cold-shock response over weeks.
- Modest soreness reduction between sessions.

**Doesn't hold up:**
- Fat loss / metabolic change. Brown fat activation is real but needs way more exposure than 3 minutes to move anything.
- Longevity. Zero direct human evidence. The longevity claims get borrowed from the Finnish sauna data, which is heat, not cold, and far stronger. Your sauna block is the one doing longevity work, not the plunge.
- Chronic HRV improvement. Thin evidence, and for you it's a sympathetic load on your weakest system — which is exactly why 56 and not 48.

**The one that cuts against you:** the acute anti-inflammatory effect is the same mechanism that blunts MPS and hypertrophy. That's not a reason to stop, it's the reason the AM-only rule exists. Timed where you have it, it costs you nothing.

Net: you're getting a real alertness/routine anchor and mild recovery benefit, and no interference with the Armor Build. That's a fine trade for 3 minutes. Just don't count it toward bone, muscle, or longevity — those come from the lifting, the sauna, and sleep.
