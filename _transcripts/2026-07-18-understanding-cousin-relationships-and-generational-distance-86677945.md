### CLAUDE (2026-07-18T18:58)
Both come from how far you and the relative are from a shared ancestor.

**Cousin number** = how many generations *both* of you are from the common ancestor (when you're equal). Second cousins share great-grandparents — you're each 3 generations down.

**"Removed"** = the generation gap between you two when you're *not* equal.

So:

- **2nd cousin**: You and they are the same generation. Your great-grandparents are their great-grandparents.
- **1st cousin twice removed**: Start with a 1st cousin relationship (shared grandparents), then move two generations up or down. Either your 1st cousin's grandchild, or your grandparent's 1st cousin.

Quick example: Your first cousin's kid is your 1st cousin once removed. That kid's kid is your 1st cousin twice removed. Meanwhile, that first kid's own kids and *your* kids would be 2nd cousins to each other — same generation, so no "removed."
