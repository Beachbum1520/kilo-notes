The user is asking about what's in the Tdap vaccine. This is straightforward medical information—Tdap stands for Tetanus, Diphtheria, and acellular Pertussis, and it's a well-established vaccine with stable components. I can answer this directly without needing to search, and I should mention the two main brands available in the US: Boostrix and Adacel.

Now I'm going through the specific components—the active ingredients are the three toxoids (tetanus and diphtheria toxoids plus multiple pertussis antigens), along with aluminum salts as an adjuvant to boost the immune response. There are also residual chemicals from the manufacturing process like formaldehyde and glutaraldehyde, plus preservatives and stabilizers depending on which brand. The two main formulations differ slightly in their pertussis antigen composition and some of their excipients. I'll keep the response concise and use a light structure since the ingredients naturally fall into categories.Tdap (tetanus, diphtheria, acellular pertussis) contains a few categories of ingredients. The two U.S. brands—Boostrix (GSK) and Adacel (Sanofi)—differ slightly.

**Active antigens (what triggers immunity):**
- Tetanus toxoid — inactivated tetanus toxin
- Diphtheria toxoid — inactivated diphtheria toxin (reduced dose, hence the lowercase "d")
- Acellular pertussis antigens — purified pieces of the whooping cough bacterium. Boostrix uses 3 (pertussis toxoid, filamentous hemagglutinin, pertactin); Adacel uses 5 (those plus fimbriae types 2 and 3)

**Adjuvant (boosts the immune response):**
- An aluminum salt — aluminum hydroxide in Boostrix, aluminum phosphate in Adacel

**Residual processing agents and excipients (trace amounts):**
- Formaldehyde and glutaraldehyde — used to inactivate the toxins; only residual traces remain
- 2-phenoxyethanol — a preservative in Adacel
- Sodium chloride (salt) and water for injection
- Trace residuals from the manufacturing/growth media

Worth noting: Tdap contains no thimerosal/mercury, and the toxoids can't cause the actual diseases since the toxins are chemically inactivated. The "acellular" part means it uses isolated bacterial proteins rather than whole killed cells, which is why it causes fewer reactions than the older whole-cell pertussis vaccines.
