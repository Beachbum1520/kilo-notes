### CLAUDE (2026-05-25T10:24)
 That's the **Garuda Wisnu Kencana (GWK) statue** — one of Bali's most iconic landmarks.

From your position near Ngurah Rai Airport/Jimbaran, you're looking southeast across the bay toward the **Bukit Peninsula**, where GWK sits atop a limestone hill at about 263 meters elevation. The statue depicts the Hindu god **Vishnu riding his mount Garuda** (the eagle/bird figure you can see with wings spread).

At **121 meters tall** (statue + pedestal), it's one of the tallest statues in the world — which is why it's visible from miles away even in hazy conditions like in your photo.

The cultural park surrounding it is worth a visit if you have time during your Bali trip.
