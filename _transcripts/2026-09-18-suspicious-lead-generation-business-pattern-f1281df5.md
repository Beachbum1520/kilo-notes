Your read is right, and the pattern actually fits both explanations at once — they're not mutually exclusive.

This is either an automated drip sequence or an offshore VA running a script for a lead-gen operation that resells your info to whichever local pavers pay for leads, and nobody ever actually picked up your job.

The "follow up" messages being identical and untethered to your specific ask (dimensions, gravel-over-geotextile question) is the tell — a real estimator who'd seen your driveway photo and your question about chip seal vs. gravel would reference it.

You're not missing anything by walking away. If you still want an asphalt/gravel quote for the driveway, I'd search for a Troup County or LaGrange-based paver directly rather than a company running Facebook ads, since local reviews will tell you fast whether "Bill" ever actually shows up.
