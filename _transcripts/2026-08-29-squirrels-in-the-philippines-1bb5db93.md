Are there squirrels in the Philippines?

### CLAUDE (2026-08-29T01:22)
Yes — the Philippines has several native squirrel species. The most notable are:

- **Philippine tree squirrels** (genus *Sundasciurus*) — multiple endemic species found across different islands
- **Palawan montane squirrel** (*Sundasciurus rabori*) — found in Palawan
- **Northern Luzon giant cloud rat** and other cloud rats are sometimes confused with squirrels but are actually a different rodent family

Squirrel diversity is highest in Palawan and Mindanao, which have closer biogeographic ties to Borneo. Some islands, like parts of the Visayas, have fewer or no native squirrel species due to historical land bridge patterns during the Ice Age.
