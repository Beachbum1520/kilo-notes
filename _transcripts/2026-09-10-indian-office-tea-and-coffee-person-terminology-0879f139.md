### CLAUDE (2026-09-10T16:01)
In many Indian offices, that person is called a "chai wala" (or "chaiwala") — literally "tea person/seller." Some offices also use "office boy" or "pantry boy," though those terms are increasingly seen as outdated or a bit dismissive, and larger corporate offices are moving toward "pantry staff" or "office assistant" instead.
