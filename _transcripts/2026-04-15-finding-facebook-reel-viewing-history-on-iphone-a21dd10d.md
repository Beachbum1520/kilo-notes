### CLAUDE (2026-04-15T16:15)
 On the iPhone Facebook app, go to **Menu** (☰ bottom right) → **Settings & Privacy** → **Activity Log** → then filter by **Videos Watched** or search for "Reels."

Alternatively: **Menu** → **Settings & Privacy** → **Your Facebook Information** → **Access Your Information** → **Activity** → **Videos You've Watched**.

Note that Facebook doesn't always retain a full scrollable Reels history — it may only show recent activity or a limited window depending on your settings.
