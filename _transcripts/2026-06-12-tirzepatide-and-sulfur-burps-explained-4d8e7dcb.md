### CLAUDE (2026-06-13T15:51)
ORS = **Oral Rehydration Solution.** It's a precise mix of water, sodium, glucose (sugar), and usually potassium — and the ratio is the whole point.

The mechanism: your gut has a transporter in the intestinal wall that pulls sodium and glucose across *together* — it only works when both are present in the right proportion. Water follows that movement automatically. So the glucose isn't there for flavor or calories; it's the thing that physically drags the sodium and water out of your gut and into your bloodstream. That's why a correctly formulated ORS rehydrates you faster and more completely than plain water or a sports drink when you're losing fluid through diarrhea.

It was originally developed as a cheap, lifesaving treatment for cholera and severe diarrheal disease in the developing world — the WHO formula has been called one of the most important medical advances of the last century, because it stops people dying of dehydration without needing an IV. DripDrop is just that same science packaged for consumers.

Why it matters for *you* right now: your LMNT and Nuun are sodium-forward but have little to no glucose — they're built for sweat, not gut loss. The diarrhea is pulling fluid out faster than those can replace it. The ORS (DripDrop Classic) has the glucose-sodium ratio that actually counters diarrheal loss. Different tool for a different kind of fluid loss. That's the entire reason I steered you to the blue box over your daily stuff.
