### CLAUDE (2026-08-09T01:24)
"Afternoon Delight" — the 1976 Starland Vocal Band hit — is about daytime sex. The cheerful barbershop-style harmonies and wholesome-sounding title are the joke; the lyrics are a fairly transparent invitation to skip waiting until nighttime.

Songwriter Bill Danoff took the title from a menu item at Clyde's, a Washington, D.C. restaurant that used "afternoon delights" for its happy-hour appetizers. He liked the double meaning and built the song around it. It won a Grammy for Best New Artist and hit #1, largely on the strength of sounding innocuous enough for AM radio while everyone understood what it meant.

It's had a long second life as a comedic reference — most famously in *Anchorman* and *Arrested Development* — precisely because of that gap between the squeaky-clean delivery and the subject matter.
