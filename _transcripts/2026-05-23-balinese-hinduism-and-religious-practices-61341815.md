### CLAUDE (2026-05-23T02:36)
 Bali is predominantly **Hindu**, practiced by about 87% of the island's population. This makes Bali unique within Indonesia, which is the world's largest Muslim-majority country.

Balinese Hinduism, known as **Agama Hindu Dharma**, is a distinct blend of Indian Hinduism, Buddhism, and indigenous animist traditions. You'll see it expressed everywhere — in the thousands of temples (*pura*), daily offerings (*canang sari*), elaborate ceremonies, and the island's art, dance, and architecture.
