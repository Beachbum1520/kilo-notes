### CLAUDE (2026-08-18T12:09)
YouTube doesn't have its own session list — it rides on your Google account.

- **All signed-in devices:** myaccount.google.com → **Security** → **Your devices** → *Manage all devices*. Shows device type, approximate location, and last activity, and lets you sign out remotely.
- **Linked TVs specifically:** youtube.com → profile icon → **Settings** → **Connected apps** / *Linked TVs* (or in the mobile app, Settings → Watch on TV). This catches TVs you paired with a code, which don't always show as full sign-ins.
- **YouTube TV subscribers:** that service keeps a separate device list under tv.youtube.com → Settings → Devices.

If you're doing this because you suspect someone else has access, change the Google password after signing devices out — signing out alone doesn't stop a re-login.

### CLAUDE (2026-08-18T12:11)
Almost certainly a garbled reference to **3840×2160** — that's 4K/UHD resolution, four times the pixel count of 1080p. Marketers round it to "4K" because the horizontal count is close to 4,000.

A couple of nearby numbers that get confused with it:

- **3840×2160** — consumer 4K/UHD, what TVs actually use
- **4096×2160** — DCI 4K, the cinema standard, slightly wider
- **3820** — not a real spec; likely a typo or an OCR misread

If you saw it in a product listing or a device name, paste the full string and I'll tell you what it actually is.
