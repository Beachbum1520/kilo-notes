### CLAUDE (2026-04-20T11:47)
 To turn off location sharing for specific family members, the steps depend on what service you're using. The most common ones:

**iPhone (Find My / Family Sharing):**
Open the Find My app → People tab → tap the family member → scroll down and tap "Stop Sharing My Location" (this hides your location from them). To stop seeing theirs, you'd remove them from your list.

**Google Family Link / Google Maps:**
Open Google Maps → tap your profile picture → Location sharing → tap the person → Stop.

**Life360:**
Open the app → Settings → Circle Switcher → pick the circle → tap your name → Location Sharing → toggle off (this hides you from everyone in that circle, not individuals). To hide from specific people, you'd need to put them in a separate circle.

### CLAUDE (2026-04-20T12:09)
 On iPhone, you've got a couple of options depending on what you want:

**Hide your location from one specific person:**
Open Find My → People tab → tap the person's name → scroll down → tap "Stop Sharing My Location."

**Stop sharing with everyone at once:**
Settings → tap your name at the top → Find My → toggle off "Share My Location."

**If they're in your Family Sharing group:**
Settings → your name → Family → tap the person → toggle off "Share My Location" for just them.

The Find My app method is usually the quickest for picking off individual people without touching anyone else's access.
