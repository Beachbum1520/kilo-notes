### CLAUDE (2026-07-28T09:45)
Yes — always first, immediately after warmup, before anything heavy.

Box jumps are a power movement: maximum force produced fast. Power is the first quality to degrade under fatigue, and jumping on tired legs is how a 55-year-old finds his Achilles. Three sets of three takes four minutes and doubles as the best deadlift primer there is — jumps fire the nervous system, so the trap bar will feel faster, not slower.

Order is locked: jumps → trap bar → everything else. Never the reverse.

He did — 3×10. I overrode it deliberately and you endorsed the override three weeks ago. The reasoning, since it's worth re-stating once:
