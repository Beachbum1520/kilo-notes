### CLAUDE (2026-08-27T10:56)
Cold plunges trigger a few well-documented effects:

- **Norepinephrine surge** – boosts alertness, mood, and focus for hours after
- **Reduced inflammation** – vasoconstriction can help with soreness/recovery
- **Metabolic boost** – activates brown fat, increases calorie burn slightly
- **Stress resilience training** – deliberately enduring discomfort builds tolerance for other stressors (a real, if soft, benefit)
- **Cardiovascular conditioning** – repeated exposure improves vascular responsiveness

Caveat worth knowing: it can blunt muscle hypertrophy gains if done right after strength training (interferes with the inflammatory signaling needed for growth), so timing relative to workouts matters.
