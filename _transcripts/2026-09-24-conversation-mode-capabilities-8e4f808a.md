### CLAUDE (2026-09-24T21:31)

Yes — Claude has a voice mode for full spoken back-and-forth conversations. It's a beta feature available on all plans on the iOS and Android apps, desktop, and web, but it's built to work best from your phone.

**How to start it on your phone:** tap the waveform icon in the lower right of the chat box — not the microphone next to it. The mic is plain dictation (it types what you say); the waveform is the actual conversation mode.

**Things worth knowing:**
- **Talk modes:** Hands-free for more natural conversation, or push-to-talk if you want control over when the mic is live.
- **Models:** It's no longer limited to the lightweight Haiku model — it can use Sonnet and Opus too, so it can handle substantive discussion rather than just quick Q&A.
- **Your apps:** It can reach connected services like Gmail, Google Calendar, and Slack, so you can ask it to summarize recent emails by voice. You already have Gmail and Drive connected here.
- **Voice/pace:** On mobile, tap the settings button in the bottom left while in voice mode to pick a voice and speaking pace.

One practical difference from ChatGPT's Advanced Voice: Claude's version is still labeled beta, so you may notice more latency or fewer interruption smarts. It's worth a drive-time test to see if it fits.
