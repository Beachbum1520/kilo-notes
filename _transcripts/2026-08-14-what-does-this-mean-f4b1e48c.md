### CLAUDE (2026-08-14T13:10)
Two Stoic phrases that work as a pair:

**Memento mori** — "remember that you must die." A reminder that your time is finite, meant to sharpen focus rather than depress. It strips the trivial out of your priorities.

**Amor fati** — "love of fate." Nietzsche's phrase, though the idea is Stoic (Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius): don't just accept what happens to you, embrace it as necessary. Not resignation — active affirmation of everything, including the hard parts.

Together they're a complete posture: your time runs out, so want what actually happens instead of grieving what didn't. The rose is the visual shorthand — beauty that's already dying.
