I had it right — earned, not received — but let me address the substance, because your auditor argument doesn't hold and it's worth knowing why.

Under ASC 606 the trigger is transfer of control, and a deposit doesn't transfer control of anything. What you have at order time is a contract and cash; the customer can't take the beef, can't direct its use, and bears no risk of loss if the animal dies in the pasture in October.
