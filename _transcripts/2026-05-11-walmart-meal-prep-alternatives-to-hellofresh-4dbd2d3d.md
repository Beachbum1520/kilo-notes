### CLAUDE (2026-05-11T17:36)
 

Yes, Walmart has a few options that overlap with what services like HelloFresh, Blue Apron, and Factor offer:

**Meal kits (cook-it-yourself, like Blue Apron/HelloFresh):** Walmart sells meal kits with no subscription required, including brands like Home Chef and Takeout Kit. In-store kits range from $8 to $15, and Home Chef kits on walmart.com are portioned for four.

**Heat-and-eat fresh meals (closer to Factor/Freshly):** Walmart's Marketside line under "Fresh Meals" includes single-serve microwave-ready dishes like Taylor Farms salmon with mashed potatoes, plus bacon-wrapped chicken grillers and similar restaurant-style entrées in the deli section.

**Frozen single-serve:** Standard frozen aisle has Healthy Choice, Lean Cuisine, Stouffer's, Devour, etc. — not "fresh" but the same heat-and-eat use case at much lower price points.

The main gap vs. delivery services is the curated weekly rotating menu and the macro/calorie targeting that something like Factor does. If that's what you're after, the Marketside fresh single-serves are the closest match — they're refrigerated, portion-controlled, and ready in a few minutes, but selection varies by store.
