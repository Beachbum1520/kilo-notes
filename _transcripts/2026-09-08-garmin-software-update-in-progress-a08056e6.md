### CLAUDE (2026-09-08T20:37)
If it's been sitting on that screen more than 10-15 minutes without the progress bar moving, it's likely stuck rather than just slow. A few things to try, in order:

- **Wait a bit more if the bar has moved at all** — some updates (especially map/sensor firmware) can genuinely take 15-20 min.
- **Force a restart**: press and hold the top-left button (Light/power) for 15-20 seconds until it powers off, then power back on. This usually doesn't wipe data.
- **If it won't restart that way**, hold the button for closer to 25-30 seconds for a hard reset.
- **Once it boots**, check if it's on charge/USB or has decent battery — updates can stall if power drops mid-install.
- **After reboot**, open Garmin Connect on your phone and let it re-sync to confirm the update actually completed.

If it reboots into the same stuck screen repeatedly, that's when it's worth a factory reset or a call to Garmin support — but try the forced restart first.
