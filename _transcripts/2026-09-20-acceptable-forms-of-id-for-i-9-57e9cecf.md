### CLAUDE (2026-09-20T21:38)

Employees present **one document from List A**, OR **one from List B + one from List C**.

**List A (identity + work authorization, one doc suffices):**
- U.S. Passport or Passport Card
- Permanent Resident Card / Form I-551
- Foreign passport with temporary I-551 stamp/notation
- Employment Authorization Document with photo (Form I-766)
- For certain status/parole holders: foreign passport + Form I-94/I-94A with matching name and status endorsement
- Passport from FSM or RMI with qualifying Form I-94/I-94A (Compact of Free Association)

**List B (identity only):**
- State/outlying-possession driver's license or ID card (photo or identifying info)
- Federal/state/local government ID card with photo or identifying info
- School ID card with photo
- Voter's registration card
- U.S. Military card or draft record
- Military dependent's ID card
- U.S. Coast Guard Merchant Mariner Card
- Native American tribal document
- Canadian driver's license
- For under-18 without any of the above: school record/report card, clinic/doctor/hospital record, or day-care/nursery school record

**List C (employment authorization only):**
- Social Security card (unless restricted — "not valid for employment," etc.)
- Certification of birth abroad (DS-1350, FS-545, FS-240)
- Original or certified birth certificate (state/county/municipal/territory, with seal)
- Native American tribal document
- U.S. Citizen ID Card (Form I-197)
- ID Card for Use of Resident Citizen in the U.S. (Form I-179)
- Employment Authorization Document issued by DHS

All documents must be unexpired (or covered by an issuer extension). Receipts for lost/stolen/damaged documents from any list are temporarily acceptable. This is from the current Form I-9 (expires 07/31/2026) — worth double-checking uscis.gov/i-9-central if you're filling one out for the farm, since USCIS updates the M-274 handbook periodically.
