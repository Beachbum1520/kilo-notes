Imodium's active ingredient is loperamide, and it's sold under that generic name plus a few brands in the Philippines:

- **Diatabs** – the most common OTC brand, loperamide HCl 2mg, sold in nearly every drugstore (Mercury Drug, Watsons, etc.)

### CLAUDE (2026-09-08T01:54)
Standard loperamide dosing (Diatabs is 2mg per capsule):

**Adults:**
- 2 capsules (4mg) after the first loose stool
- Then 1 capsule (2mg) after each subsequent loose stool
- **Max: 8 capsules (16mg) per day**

**Duration:** Don't take it for more than 2 days without seeing a doctor. If diarrhea persists beyond 48 hours, or you develop fever, blood in stool, or severe abdominal pain, stop and get medical care — those can signal something loperamide shouldn't be masking (like a bacterial infection).

**For a typical pharmacy ask** ("how many you want?") — buying a strip of 6-10 capsules covers a short traveler's diarrhea episode fine.

One caution: don't combine with alcohol, and if you're already on other meds, worth a quick check with the pharmacist for interactions.
