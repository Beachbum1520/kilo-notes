### CLAUDE (2026-09-03T20:36)

You can't actually delete apps from Xumo — Xumo TVs come with 250+ apps preloaded, so there's nothing to install or delete; everything just stays on the device. What you *can* do is control which ones show up on your home screen:

- Scroll to the end of the Apps row and select **Manage Apps** (or go to **Settings > Viewing preferences > Manage favorite apps**)
- This opens the "Choose your favorites" screen where you can pick up to 30 apps you actually want shown
- Then arrange their order on the "Manage your favorites" screen using your remote's navigation pad, and save when done

So unchecking an app there just hides it from your row — it's still on the device, ready to reappear if you re-add it to favorites later.

### CLAUDE (2026-09-03T20:38)
That's your Apps row — YouTubeTV, ESPN, fubo, Sling, etc. are just your favorites list, not installed apps. To pull the ones you don't use off that row:

1. Scroll to the **end of that Apps row** and select **Manage Apps** (or go to **Settings > Viewing preferences > Manage favorite apps**)
2. On the "Choose your favorites" screen, uncheck fubo, Sling, or whatever else you don't want showing
3. Select **Continue**, then arrange the remaining ones in your preferred order on the next screen, and save

That'll clear them off the home row — they're not "installed" so there's nothing to actually delete, just hide from view.

### CLAUDE (2026-09-03T20:39)

A few possibilities, from most to least likely:

1. **You didn't hit save/exit properly.** After unchecking apps and pressing Continue on the "Choose your favorites" screen, you land on "Manage your favorites" — you need to press the back/exit button there too to actually commit it. Just backing out with continue selected isn't always enough.

2. **Known bug with "Manage your favorites."** There are user reports that unchecking an app there only removes its checkmark from that screen but doesn't actually clear it from "Choose your favorites," so it reappears. If that's happening, try clearing locally stored data:
   - **Settings > System management > Resets & updates > Locally stored data**
   - This wipes cached preferences (you'll need to re-sign into some apps after)

3. **These may be sponsored/pinned placements, not your favorites.** Looking at your photo — YouTubeTV, ESPN, fubo, Sling, Spectrum News — these look like Spectrum/Xumo's promoted partner apps, which sometimes get pinned to the row regardless of your favorites settings, since Spectrum pushes its own partner content. If unchecking them in Manage Apps doesn't remove them, that's likely why — they aren't really "favorites," they're promotional tiles.

Try #2 first (clear locally stored data), since that resolves the most common version of this glitch. If they're still there after that, it's #3 and there's no user-side fix — it'd be a "give feedback to Spectrum" situation, since other users have complained about this exact lack of control.
