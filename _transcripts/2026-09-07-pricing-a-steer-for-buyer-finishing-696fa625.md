### SCOTT (2026-09-07T12:37)

### CLAUDE (2026-09-07T12:39)
**Around $2,300–$2,400 flat, if the steer is basically at or near market weight now and the buyer is just taking over finishing/processing from here.**

Here's the logic: your current per-steer economics (2 halves, ~511 lb HW, retail at $6.75/lb) run:

- Revenue ~$3,450
- Minus feed $475 + processing $637 = ~$1,112
- **= ~$2,338 operating contribution**

If you sell live instead of finishing/processing yourself, you're skipping that $1,112 in costs but also giving up the $3,450 in revenue. Charging ~$2,338 for the live animal keeps you whole vs. finishing it yourself — you're not leaving money on the table by handing it off early, and the buyer is trading their own labor/feed/processing arrangements for a lower price than they'd pay you for a finished half/whole.

**One thing that changes the number:** how much growing is actually left. If this steer still needs real time and feed to get to 29 months, you haven't spent that $475 in feed yet — so pricing it at $2,338 now would overcharge relative to your actual position, since some of that "savings" hasn't happened yet. In that case I'd price it lower (something like $1,900–$2,100) to reflect that you're selling a partly-finished animal, not a market-ready one.

What's the current age/weight on this one — is it close to slaughter-ready or does it have real growing left?
