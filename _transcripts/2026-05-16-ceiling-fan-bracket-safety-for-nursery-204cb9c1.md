**Two things must both be fan-rated:**

1. **The box itself** — look on the inside or rim for stamped text like *"Acceptable for Fan Support,"* *"Listed for Ceiling Fan,"* or a weight rating (usually 35–70 lbs). Generic plastic round boxes are *not* rated and will fail over time from the vibration. Carlon makes a fan-rated plastic box that looks similar to this, so it could be fine — but you need to see the marking.

2. **The brace bar** — those black rods I can see extending out look like they could be a saddle-style expanding fan brace (Saf-T-Brace, Westinghouse Saf-T-Bar, etc.), which is what you want. It should span between two joists with spiked feet that bite into the wood, and the box should be locked into a U-saddle on the bar — not just screwed to a single joist with a regular bracket.

**Red flags to check:**
- Box only screwed to the side of a single joist with no brace bar → not adequate
- Brace bar but no markings indicating fan rating → not adequate
- Box wiggles when you push up on it → not adequate

If either piece isn't rated, the fix is a $20–30 Westinghouse 0110000 or similar fan brace from Home Depot, installed from below through the existing hole — no drywall work needed. Given this is a nursery with a crib likely going underneath, I wouldn't skip the verification step.
