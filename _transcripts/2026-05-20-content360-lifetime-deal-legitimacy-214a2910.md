### CLAUDE (2026-05-20T10:42)

**Verdict: Gray zone — not an outright scam, but buyer-beware territory.**

Here's the breakdown:

**What it is:** Content360 is a social media scheduling/management SaaS tool selling a "lifetime deal" for around $67. Founded by Dom Ricci, launched ~2025, US-based remote company. It's a real product that real people are using.

**Red flags:**
- Multiple Trustpilot reviewers report unresponsive support after purchasing, with one user describing a pattern of taking one-time payments and then ignoring issues until customers give up.
- At least one user had billing problems during a trial they couldn't cancel, and had to pursue a chargeback with their bank.
- Scamadviser rates the subdomain lower due to a young domain, shared hosting with other suspicious sites, and other automated risk signals.
- Platform reliability issues reported — one user was locked out with a broken password reset link shortly after signup.

**Mitigating factors:**
- The founder actively responds to reviews and acknowledges problems, and several users report genuinely positive experiences.
- The company claims 10,000+ active users and states the lifetime deal is exactly what it advertises.
- It's a Facebook ad for a legitimately priced low-cost SaaS tool — not a credential harvest or malware delivery vector.

**Bottom line:** It's a real (if early-stage, buggy) SaaS product with shaky support infrastructure — not a phishing scam, but the "lifetime deal" model for young SaaS companies carries inherent risk. If the company folds or pivots to subscriptions later, "lifetime" means nothing. For $67 with low expectations it might be fine; I wouldn't give it a credit card you can't dispute easily.
