### CLAUDE (2026-04-28T17:21)

Starting January 21, 2026, the TOEFL iBT transitioned to a new scoring scale ranging from **1 to 6** in half-point increments, replacing the traditional 0–120 scale. The new scale aligns with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR).

**A TOEFL iBT score of 80 (old scale) = 4.0 on the new 1–6 scale.**

This is confirmed by ETS's official mapping — a score of 80 is now equivalent to a **4.0** on the updated scale.

Here's the key conversion reference points from ETS:

| Old Score (0–120) | New Score (1–6) | CEFR Level |
|---|---|---|
| 107+ | 5.5 | C1+ |
| 95–106 | 5.0 | C1 |
| ~90 | 4.5 | B2+/C1 |
| **~80** | **4.0** | **B2** |
| ~70 | 3.5 | B2 |

During the transition period (January 2026 – January 2028), score reports will display both the new 1–6 score and the comparable 0–120 score, so no manual conversion is needed when sending scores to institutions.
