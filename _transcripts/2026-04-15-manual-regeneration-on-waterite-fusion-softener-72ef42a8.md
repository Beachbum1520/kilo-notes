### CLAUDE (2026-04-15T23:28)
 That's a **Waterite Fusion²** water softener control head. To manually trigger a regeneration:

**Immediate manual regen:**
Press and **hold the REGEN button for about 3 seconds**. The unit should initiate regeneration right away.

**Delayed regen (tonight at normal regen time):**
Press REGEN **once** (don't hold) — it will regenerate at the next scheduled regen time.

The display will typically show "REGEN" or change from the time display to confirm it accepted the command.

**A couple things to note:**
- Regen takes 1.5–2 hours typically, during which softened water won't be available
- Best to run it when water demand is low (overnight is ideal)
- Make sure the brine tank has salt before triggering

If the hold doesn't work, the unit may require you to press NEXT first to navigate to a regen menu — but on most Fusion² heads, the single hold of REGEN is the direct shortcut.
