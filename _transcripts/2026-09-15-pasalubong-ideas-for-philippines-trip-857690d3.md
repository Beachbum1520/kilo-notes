### CLAUDE (2026-09-15T00:05)

Pasalubong ideas for a trip to Concepcion, Tarlac — good mix for her, her family, and giving back:

**For your girlfriend:**
- US-brand makeup/skincare (Maybelline, e.l.f., or higher-end like Clinique — pricier/hard to find there)
- Good quality vitamins/supplements (collagen, biotin — popular and pricier in PH)
- Perfume from a US mall (Bath & Body Works is a perennial hit)
- A nice piece of jewelry or a personal item that's clearly "picked out for her," not just generic pasalubong

**For her family (very customary to bring something for parents/siblings):**
- Chocolates — Hershey's, Reese's, M&M's (in bulk, they don't spoil quickly)
- Coffee — Starbucks Via packets or specialty US coffee
- US vitamins for the parents (Centrum, fish oil — genuinely appreciated by older Filipinos)
- Snacks not available there — beef jerky, trail mix, specific cereal brands
- Cash-value item over quantity for anyone you're closer to (an uncle, a favorite sibling)

**Practical/status items that land well:**
- Nice toiletries — good shampoo, lotion (Bath & Body Works again, or Olay)
- A branded bag or wallet (Coach outlet items are a classic Balikbayan box staple)
- Phone accessories (US ones read as higher quality)

**A few notes:**
- Skip liquid-heavy or fragile items if you're checking a Balikbayan box vs. carry-on — pack accordingly
- Filipino families often value quantity/variety for extended family over one expensive thing for one person — a mix works better than concentrating your budget
- If you want to bring something status-forward specifically for her, an item from a US mall outlet (Coach, Michael Kors) tends to be well-received and is noticeably cheaper stateside than in PH retail
