Netflix gives you a few ways to sign out of devices remotely:

**Sign out everywhere at once**
1. Open Netflix in a web browser and go to your **Account** page
2. Find the **Sign out of all devices** option (under Security or Settings)
3. Confirm — this logs you out of every device, usually within about 8 hours

**Sign out a single device**
1. On the Account page, scroll to where your devices are listed
2. Select the device you want to remove and choose **Sign out**

**If you don't have the password / it's not your device**
- Change your Netflix password and check the box to sign out of all devices when prompted. This is the fastest way to kick out someone you don't want on the account.

The mobile app also has a sign-out option under the profile/menu, but for *remote* sign-out (logging out devices you don't physically have), the Account page on the web is the reliable route.

One quick check before you do it: if your goal is to remove someone using your account without your permission, the password-change route is best, since it forces everyone to log back in with credentials they may not have.
