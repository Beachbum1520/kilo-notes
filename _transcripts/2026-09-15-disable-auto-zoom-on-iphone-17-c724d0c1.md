### CLAUDE (2026-09-15T23:54)

That's Center Stage — the new front camera feature on iPhone 17. Here's how to turn it off:

**In Camera app (for photos/selfies):**
1. Open Camera, switch to front camera
2. Tap the Center Stage button (person icon in a frame)
3. Turn off **Auto Zoom** (and **Auto Rotate** if that's also bothering you)

**In FaceTime:**
1. During a call, tap your own video thumbnail
2. Tap **Camera Effects**
3. Tap **Center Stage** to turn it off

If it's happening in a third-party app (WhatsApp, Zoom, etc.) even after disabling it in Camera/FaceTime, that app may have its own camera effects toggle — check for a small green dot in the Dynamic Island during the call, tap it, and look for a Center Stage option there.

This confirms it's still Center Stage — it's just controlled separately in each app, not globally. Since you already said the Camera app toggle didn't fix it, here's where to turn it off per app:

**FaceTime**
- During a call, tap your own video thumbnail → **Camera Effects** → tap **Center Stage** to turn it off

**Zoom**
- During a meeting, tap the screen → top-left corner → **Turn Off Center Stage**
- Or beforehand: Zoom app → **Settings** → **Meetings** → turn off Center Stage

**WhatsApp**
- WhatsApp doesn't have its own toggle — it inherits the system camera behavior. Open the front camera in WhatsApp (like starting a video call or status), look for a small **green dot** in the Dynamic Island, tap it, and disable Center Stage from there.

If it's still happening everywhere after all three, it's worth a restart — some people report the setting not taking effect until the phone reboots.
