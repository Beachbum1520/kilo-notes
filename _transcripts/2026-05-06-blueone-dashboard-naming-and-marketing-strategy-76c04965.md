**The case against BlueONE:**
- "One" is overused in enterprise product naming — it's the "Pro" of B2B SaaS
- It doesn't tell a hotelier *anything* about what it does
- It could describe literally any consolidation product in any industry
- Legal risk is real, and if it gets rejected you've already built brand equity around it

**The stronger names on that list, given your "single pane of glass" vision:**

**SignalView** is actually the most defensible. It communicates *visibility*, it's network-native (signal = RF/connectivity), and it's distinctive enough to survive a trademark search. A hotelier hears it and understands the product direction immediately.

**PulsePoint** is solid too — operational heartbeat connotation, memorable, pairs well with the fleet management angle.

**If I were naming it from scratch** for the story you described — full IP device visibility across the managed network, true unified operations view — I'd push something like **PropertyIQ**, **NetCommand**, or **HorizonView**. Those telegraph intelligence and scope rather than just "oneness."

Bottom line: don't let the name that's already in circulation win by default. The product concept you described is genuinely differentiated — the name should carry that weight.
