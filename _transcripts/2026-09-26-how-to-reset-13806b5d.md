### CLAUDE (2026-09-26T12:21)
That "CL" is **Control Lock** (child lock), not an error.

**To clear it:** with the washer powered on, press and hold the **Tub Clean** button (it's labeled "*Control Lock" underneath) for **3 seconds**. "CL" will disappear and the controls will respond again.

If it doesn't clear, unplug the washer for about a minute, plug it back in, power it on, and try the 3-second hold again.
