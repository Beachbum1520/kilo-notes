### CLAUDE (2026-04-15T22:43)
 Your whites have gone dingy/yellowed — very common, and totally fixable.

**With bleach (fastest fix):**

1. Remove everything from the washer
2. Run a **hot water cycle** with **½ cup of chlorine bleach** + your regular detergent — no clothes yet, just to prep if the drum needs it (optional)
3. Reload and wash on the **hottest setting safe for those fabrics** with ½ cup bleach + detergent
4. Check before drying — **never put dingy whites in the dryer** until they look right, heat sets stains permanently

**A few cautions:**
- Check tags first — bleach damages wool, silk, and spandex blends
- That terry towel on the left may tolerate bleach fine; thin cotton shirts/sheets too
- Don't overdo bleach — it can actually cause yellowing over time if used too heavily

**For stubborn dinginess, boost it:**
- Add **½ cup baking soda** to the drum along with the bleach + detergent
- Or try **OxiClean White Revive** on a future wash — it's gentler and excellent for fabric-safe brightening

**Root cause to address:** Dinginess usually comes from detergent buildup, hard water, or washing in cold water. Switching to hot water for whites and using the right detergent amount goes a long way going forward.
