### CLAUDE (2026-05-26T19:59)
 Yes, Grab supports multi-stop booking. Here's how:

**In the Grab app:**
1. Select your transport service (GrabCar, etc.)
2. Tap the destination field — you'll see an option to **"Add stop"** (usually a "+" icon)
3. Add up to **5 stops** total on a single trip
4. Each stop is added in order, and the fare is calculated for the full route

**A few things to know:**
- The fare covers the entire multi-stop journey, not separate legs
- Wait time at each stop may be limited (driver discretion)
- Not all Grab services support multi-stop — GrabCar typically does; GrabBike options may vary by market
- Available in most markets including Philippines, though the UI may differ slightly by app version

If you don't see the "Add stop" option, make sure your app is updated to the latest version.
