The 5-pack is the best per-shirt price by a meaningful margin, and you pay only ~$12 more than buying exactly 4. ### CLAUDE (2026-04-17T19:33)
 Good question — and the fact that you're duplicating one means you should pick the color you'll actually reach for twice as often.

**Recommendation: duplicate White.**

Reasoning:
- **Highest rotation frequency.** White is the most versatile base color — pairs with anything, works in more settings (casual, under a jacket, athletic). You'll wear it more, wash it more, and wear it out faster.
- **Wear-and-tear logic.** White shows stains, yellowing, and pit discoloration faster than colored fabrics. Having a backup extends your usable inventory before one gets relegated to "farm work only."
- **Laundry cycle math.** If your favorite color is in the wash, a second white keeps you covered without forcing you into a color you like less that day.

**Counterpoint to consider:** If you already own several white shirts and are light on earth tones, duplicating **Brown or Green** makes more sense — they're harder to replace and fit the farm/outdoor aesthetic. Black is the least useful to duplicate unless it's your default; it's the easiest color to find elsewhere.

So the decision tree is basically:
- Closet already heavy on white? → duplicate **Brown** (most unique, hardest to replace)
- Closet balanced or white-light? → duplicate **White** (highest utility)
