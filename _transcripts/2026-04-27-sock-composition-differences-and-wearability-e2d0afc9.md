### SCOTT (2026-04-27T05:36)
What is Crew length?

### CLAUDE (2026-04-27T05:36)
 Crew length = mid-calf. The sock comes up roughly 6-8 inches above your ankle bone, ending somewhere on the meatiest part of your calf.

Standard sock height reference:

- **No-show / ankle** — below the ankle bone, hidden in the shoe
- **Quarter** — just above the ankle bone, ~3-4"
- **Crew** — mid-calf, ~6-8" above ankle (the "standard" tube sock height)
- **Boot / Mid-calf** — 9-11", taller than crew, designed to hit higher up the calf
- **Knee-high / Over-the-calf (OTC)** — 14"+, comes up to or past the knee

For cowboy work boots with a 10-13" shaft, **crew is borderline too short**. The top of a crew sock often sits right at the boot shaft opening or just below it — which is exactly where it'll get pulled down into the boot as you walk.

You want **boot-height or over-the-calf** — something that comes up *higher* than the boot shaft so the sock has anchor above the boot's grip zone. Over-the-calf is the traditional cowboy boot answer for this reason — old-school ranchers wore tall socks specifically so the boot couldn't pull them down.

So when shopping: skip "crew," look for "boot," "mid-calf tall," or "over-the-calf."
