**3-Day Dumbbell Program — Upper / Lower / Full**
Intermediate, moderate DB (up to 50 lb pairs), fat loss + strength focus. Rep range 8–12, rest 60–90s between sets (shorter rest = more metabolic demand for fat loss without sacrificing load). Progress by adding weight when top of rep range is hit for all sets, two sessions in a row.

**Day 1 — Upper**
1. DB Bench Press (flat or floor) — 4×8–10
2. DB Bench Row — 4×8–10
3. DB Shoulder Press — 3×10–12
4. DB Incline Curl — 3×10–12
5. DB Overhead Triceps Extension — 3×10–12
6. DB Lateral Raise — 3×12–15
Finisher: DB Renegade Rows — 3×10 total (fat-loss metabolic add-on)

**Day 2 — Lower**
1. DB Goblet Squat — 4×8–10
2. DB Romanian Deadlift — 4×8–10
3. DB Walking Lunge — 3×10/leg
4. DB Step-Up — 3×10/leg
5. DB Calf Raise — 3×15–20
6. Weighted Glute Bridge (DB on hips) — 3×12–15
Finisher: DB Squat + Press (thruster) — 3×12

**Day 3 — Full Body**
1. DB Deadlift — 4×8
2. DB Push Press — 3×8–10
3. DB Single-Arm Row — 3×10/side
4. DB Reverse Lunge — 3×10/leg
5. DB Floor Press — 3×10
6. DB Farmer's Carry — 3×30–40 sec
Finisher: 8-min AMRAP — 8 goblet squats, 8 push-ups, 8 alternating swings/snatches (or 6 rounds, whichever hits first)

**Notes**
- Warm up 5 min: light cardio + arm circles/leg swings/bodyweight squats.
- Fat-loss lever isn't the lifting — it's the finishers plus diet. Keep the main lifts heavy and slow; use finishers for the conditioning stimulus.
- If she stalls on a lift for 3 sessions straight, drop reps to 5–6 for 2 weeks at higher weight, then return to 8–12.
- Swap goblet squat for DB front squat once 50 lb pairs feel easy — that's the ceiling of this equipment for squat pattern.

### CLAUDE (2026-09-17T01:25)
For 3x/week specifically, Upper/Lower/Full is the better rotation — not PPL. Here's why:

**PPL's real advantage is frequency at 6 days/week** — each muscle group gets hit hard once, recovers, repeats. Compressed into 3 days/week, PPL means each muscle group trains only once every 7 days. That's a training frequency problem, not a volume one: research consistently shows 2x/week per muscle group beats 1x/week for both hypertrophy and strength, at equal volume. For fat loss + strength, more frequent stimulus wins over more volume in a single session.

**Upper/Lower/Full at 3x/week hits push, pull, and legs roughly twice each already** — the Full day picks up whatever got less volume on Upper/Lower. So you get PPL's variety without the frequency penalty.

**PPL only makes sense at 3x/week if she can't ever get to 6, and even then it's a fallback, not an upgrade.** The other case for PPL: if she has a single lagging muscle group that needs isolated day-long focus — not the situation here.

Keep what I built. If you want, I can restructure it as a true 2x/week-per-muscle-group split (e.g., Upper A / Lower A / Upper B, rotating) instead of the block Upper/Lower/Full — that's a legitimate alternate structure that keeps the frequency advantage. Want that instead?
