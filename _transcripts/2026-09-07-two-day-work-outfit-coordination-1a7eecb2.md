### CLAUDE (2026-09-08T00:00)
Fair question — gray would actually work fine too. Here's the actual case for navy over gray:

**Navy wins on versatility across both jackets.** The brown/tan plaid blazer has warm undertones — gray trousers next to it can look a little flat or corporate, while navy adds contrast and reads more intentional. The blue windowpane blazer pairs naturally with either, so that one's a wash.

**Gray's downside is subtlety, not compatibility.** Both blazers already have gray in their weave, so gray trousers risk blending into "all one texture" rather than looking like a coordinated outfit. Navy gives you a cleaner break between jacket and pant.

If you'd rather run gray both days — it's not wrong, just slightly safer/flatter with the brown plaid. Your call: navy for a bit more polish, gray if you want the safer/more conservative look for client-facing days.
