Cash basis — which is what Schedule F farms almost always use — you recognize it when the money hits, not when the beef does. So deposits taken in 2026 are 2026 income; final payments collected in January are 2027 income, even though the animal was processed in December.

That's a lever, and it's worth thinking about now rather than in January. If you want the revenue in 2026, collect in full before year end. If 2027 is the better year, take deposits only and bill the balance at pickup. With substantial W-2 income and a farm running at a loss, the usual play is pulling income into whichever year the loss offsets it best — but that depends on where your 2026 Schedule F actually lands, which I don't know.
