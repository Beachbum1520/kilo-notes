I should provide quick fixes for the most common browsers like Edge and Chrome, plus mention the universal padlock icon method in the address bar that works across all browsers.That error means the browser is blocking the site's access to your camera and mic. Fastest fix works in both Chrome and Edge:

1. **Click the icon to the left of the web address** (a padlock or a "settings sliders" icon) in the address bar.
2. You'll see toggles or dropdowns for **Camera** and **Microphone** — set both to **Allow** for this site.
3. **Refresh the page** — permissions only take effect after a reload.

If you don't see camera/mic listed there:

- Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Site settings → Camera (and Microphone) → make sure the site isn't under "Not allowed," and that the correct webcam is selected in the dropdown at the top.
- Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Camera / Microphone → same idea.

If it's *still* blocked after that, the problem is usually one level up — Windows itself or another app:

- **Another app has the camera locked.** If Teams, Zoom, or the Camera app is open, close them completely (check the system tray) — most webcams only allow one app at a time.
- **Windows privacy settings:** Settings → Privacy & security → Camera → make sure "Camera access" and "Let apps access your camera" are both On, and scroll down to confirm desktop apps/browsers are allowed. Same under Microphone.

One thought: if the browser teleprompter wants camera access, it's probably trying to record for you. You don't actually need that — you can deny it the camera entirely, use it purely as scrolling text at the top of your screen, and record in Teams like we discussed. That also avoids the one-app-at-a-time camera conflict.
