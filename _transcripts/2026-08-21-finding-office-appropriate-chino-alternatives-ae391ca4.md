One thing to sanity check yourself: "pass every dress code" is their marketing line, not an objective standard — worth trying one pair in person or with easy returns before ordering multiples, since "business casual" bars vary a lot by office.

Bottom line: keep 1-2 pairs of real trousers in rotation for the high-visibility days, use the chinos as your default. That's how most SDs at your level actually dress now anyway.
