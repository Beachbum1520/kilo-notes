Two things worth your attention:

**Touch time vs. calendar time.** Eric asked "a couple of weeks/days?" — he's asking about elapsed time. Your draft answers in labor hours. Those aren't the same thing, and if he reads 2 hours as 2 hours from opp creation to approved proposal, he'll set expectations with SB sellers you can't meet. I added a paragraph separating the two and gave him a directional calendar answer ("days, not weeks") anchored to a complete intake package. Change or cut that if you don't want to commit to it in writing.

**The ramp paragraph.** I turned your placeholder into a specific input request — volume, deal mix, seller count and phasing, target turnaround — and closed with the point that 97% utilization means staffing has to lead the volume. That's the sentence doing the real work: it puts the headcount conversation on the record without asking for anything yet, and it's grounded in a number rather than an assertion.

You also have the 2.5 TDEs per seller / 1 SE per 3 sellers ratio if you want to hand him a planning figure now. I left it out — it invites him to do his own math before you've seen his volume assumptions.
