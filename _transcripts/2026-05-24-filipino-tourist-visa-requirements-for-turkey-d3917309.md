Here's the breakdown for a Filipino getting a Turkey tourist visa:

**Two paths — which one depends on whether she has a US/Schengen/UK/Ireland visa:**

---

**Path 1: e-Visa (Easy — if she qualifies)**

Filipino passport holders with a valid visa or residence permit from the US, Schengen area, UK, or Ireland are eligible for the e-Visa. The visa must still be valid — expired visas don't qualify.

The e-Visa is applied for entirely online — no embassy visit needed. Once approved, it's emailed directly to the applicant. Fast, low friction.

---

**Path 2: Regular Sticker Visa (More work — no qualifying visa)**

Applicants must physically appear at the Türkiye Visa Application Center in Makati City (Unit 17E, Petron Mega Plaza, Buendia). No appointment needed. Biometric fingerprints are required, so no remote application. Hours are 9am–1pm.

Core documents required for either path:
Passport with 6+ months validity, passport photos (white background), completed application form, round-trip flight itinerary, proof of accommodation, 3 months of bank statements, travel health insurance, and proof of employment or enrollment.

---

**Bottom line:** If your girlfriend has a valid US tourist visa (even from a prior trip to the States), the e-Visa is fast and simple. If she doesn't, it's a personal appearance in Makati with a full document package — manageable but requires lead time. Worth checking her passport for any qualifying visas before planning.
