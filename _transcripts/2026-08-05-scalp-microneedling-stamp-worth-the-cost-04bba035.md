What *does* need a prescription is **oral** minoxidil (low-dose, 2.5–5mg), which dermatologists prescribe off-label and which a lot of people find easier to stick with than daily topical. That's a derm or telehealth conversation.

One practical note on combining it with the stamp: the liquid formulation is heavy on propylene glycol, which stings badly through fresh micro-channels and can cause real irritation. Two ways around it —

- Stamp and minoxidil on separate days (wait ~24h after stamping), or
- Use the **foam** version, which has no propylene glycol and is much gentler post-stamp

Foam costs a bit more but is worth it if you're stamping regularly. Either way, minoxidil is the daily habit and the stamp is the once-a-week add-on — not the reverse.
