**What the note says, in order:**

**1. Ketoconazole 2% cream: twice daily for 2 weeks, face.**
This comes first. Ketoconazole is an antifungal that calms seborrheic-type redness and flaking. Clearing that inflammation before step 2 makes the next treatment more tolerable and easier to read.

**2. SKNV: they'll text you.**
SKNV is a dermatology specialty pharmacy that makes compounded prescriptions. The Efudex/calcipotriene combo is compounded, so expect a text from them to confirm, pay, and ship.

**3. Efudex + calcipotriene: thin layer twice daily to the face for 4 days (after the 2 weeks of ketoconazole).**
- Efudex is 5-fluorouracil, topical chemotherapy.
- Calcipotriene is a vitamin D analog that amplifies the immune response.
- Together they're a short, intense field treatment for precancerous sun damage (actinic keratoses). The 4-day course is standard for this combo, compared with 2–4 weeks for Efudex alone.

**4. "Ⓛ FA 7–10 days"**
This is the ambiguous line. Most likely it means the expected reaction (redness, crusting, peeling) lasts about 7–10 days after the 4-day course. Confirm with the office when SKNV texts.

**5. Heliocare Advanced (B3 + Fernblock): 2 capsules per day.**
This is an oral over-the-counter supplement. Fernblock is a fern extract (Polypodium leucotomos) that adds UV protection, and B3 is nicotinamide, which has evidence for reducing new precancers and skin cancers. It's taken ongoing and supplements sunscreen rather than replacing it.

**Practical points for the Efudex window:**
- Keep it off your eyelids, lips, and nostril edges, and wash your hands after applying.
- Use strict sun avoidance and daily sunscreen during the course and the reaction phase.
- Skip retinoids and exfoliants during those days.
- Time the 4 days so the 7–10 day reaction doesn't land on a day you need to be on camera or at a client event.
- Sweat and a cold plunge on raw skin will sting but won't hurt anything. Outdoor runs mid-reaction need a hat and shade.

**Timeline if you start ketoconazole today:** ketoconazole runs through about Oct 9, Efudex/calcipotriene about Oct 10–13, and the reaction settles by roughly Oct 20–23.

### SCOTT (2026-09-25T22:28)
Doc said, I think, Efudex/calcipotriene only hits the area in needs. Meaning the spots with no pre-cancer. No reaction otherwise

### CLAUDE (2026-09-25T22:29)
Mostly right, with one correction and one caveat.

**The correction:** it's the reverse of how you wrote it. The reaction happens at spots **with** precancer, not without. 5-FU hits rapidly dividing abnormal cells, and calcipotriene sharpens the immune response against them. Healthy skin is largely spared. That's why you apply it to the whole face instead of spot-treating: it finds the damaged cells for you.

**The caveat:** "no reaction otherwise" is too clean.
- **Hidden spots:** it lights up subclinical precancers you can't see yet. The reaction can look more widespread than the few spots you're aware of, and on a sun-exposed 55-year-old face that's common.
- **Healthy skin:** it can still get some dryness, pinkness, and irritation, especially toward day 5–7 and on thin skin like the nose folds, around the eyes, and the lip line.

**What to expect:** a patchy reaction concentrated where the damage is (forehead, temples, nose, cheeks, and scalp line if you're thinning), with mild irritation elsewhere. How widespread it looks tells you how much sun damage was there, not that something went wrong.

### CLAUDE (2026-09-27T13:51)
Yes. Ketoconazole has no sun protection, and farm days are exactly the exposure that caused the precancers.

**Order:**
1. Apply the ketoconazole to clean, dry skin.
2. Wait 10–15 minutes for it to absorb.
3. Put sunscreen on top.

**Which sunscreen:** use a mineral one (zinc oxide, SPF 30+). It's less likely to sting skin that's already red and flaking than chemical filters.

**On the farm:**
- Wear a wide-brim hat; a ball cap leaves your ears, temples, and neck exposed.
- Reapply every 2 hours, sooner if you're sweating.
- The Heliocare adds to sunscreen but doesn't replace it, so keep doing both once it arrives.
