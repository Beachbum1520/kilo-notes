Betsy Lynn George. She played Devon, the teenager who tries to seduce the mild-mannered businessman (Joshua Townshend-Zellner) in the video, which was directed by David Fincher — five years before *Seven*. She was 18 at the time, and Propaganda Films had to request a copy of her driver's license after a concerned mothers' group questioned whether she was of age.

Idol was filmed only from the waist up in the video because he couldn't walk following a February 1990 motorcycle crash. George went on to small roles in *Point Break*, *Twin Peaks*, and *Melrose Place*.
