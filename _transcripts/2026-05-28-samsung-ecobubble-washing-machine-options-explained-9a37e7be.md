On the EcoBubble, the door doesn't unlock from a normal pause if the water level is too high or the drum is too hot/spinning. To open it:

1. **Press Start/Pause once** to pause the cycle (the light should blink).
2. **Wait for the door lock icon to clear** — the little padlock symbol. Once it goes out, pull the handle.

If the door won't release:

- The **water level may be above the door line** — the machine won't unlock for safety. You can't add clothes until it drains down. Pausing early (before fill) is the only reliable window to add items.
- If it's already filled, **turn the Spin dial to "no spin"/drain or run a short Spin/Drain** to lower the water, then pause and open.
- Give it **1–2 minutes after pausing** — there's a safety delay before the lock disengages.

Once you've added your items, shut the door firmly and **press Start/Pause again** to resume.

If the padlock symbol stays lit indefinitely and there's no water visible through the glass, the door lock mechanism may be sticking — let me know and I'll walk you through that.

2. **Press down on the door while pulling the handle.** Push the door firmly toward the machine (taking pressure off the latch) and pull the handle at the same time. A slightly misaligned latch often releases this way.

3. **Tap firmly around the latch area.** A light knock near the door catch can free a stuck mechanism.

4. **Try the drain-and-reset.** Even if you can't see water, run a Spin or Rinse+Spin cycle for ~1 minute — completing a short program sometimes resets the lock state, then pause/stop and try the door.
