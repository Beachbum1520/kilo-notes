### SCOTT (2026-09-21T14:42)
what does no kip mean

### CLAUDE (2026-09-21T14:42)
Kipping is using a swing to cheat a pullup — rocking the hips and legs to generate momentum so the body swings up instead of the back pulling it up. CrossFit made it famous as a way to do more reps faster.

"No kip" = dead-still body: from the full hang at the bottom, the only thing that moves you upward is your back and arms. Momentum steals exactly the work you're there to do — and on the assisted machine, the knee pad turns any swing into bouncing off the pad, which is the same theft with extra steps.

You almost certainly already do them strict; the cue is insurance for the last rep of set 3, where everyone's hips suddenly discover ambition.
