**A few Dexter-specific caveats worth knowing:**

- **Frame matters a lot.** Dexters are notoriously variable — a long-legged (non-bulldog) Dexter will dress better than a chondrodysplastic (short-legged) type.
- **Finish age affects it too.** Grass-finished Dexters at 24+ months will dress better than younger animals. Grain-finishing tightens the dressing % upward toward 56–58%.
- **Retail yield from hanging weight** is typically 60–65% (bones out, trim loss), so expect **190–290 lbs of take-home cuts** depending on your cut sheet.
