Is 50,000 IDR a good tip for a 90 min tour guide?

### CLAUDE (2026-05-24T04:37)

50,000 IDR (~$3 USD) for a 90-minute tour guide is **on the low end but acceptable** — not embarrassing, but not generous either.

Here's the context:

For a dedicated tour guide, **50,000–100,000 IDR per group** is the standard range. For a 90-minute engagement (shorter than a full day), 50K lands at the floor of that range.

50,000 IDR is roughly equivalent to a meal or two at a local spot — so it's meaningful to them, just not generous.

For elite private guide service, 150,000–300,000 IDR *per day* is the generous standard. Prorated for 90 minutes, that puts generous in the 75K–125K range.

**Practical call:**
- 50K = fine, won't offend
- 100K = clearly appreciated, reflects quality service
- If the guide was good and you're at Le Méridien/Ubud-area context — **100K is the right move.** It's ~$6 and they'll remember you.

 That changes the math significantly. Group activity guides are tipped less per person since the tip is spread across the group.

50,000–100,000 IDR per **group** is the standard for a dedicated guide — and for a group ATV ride where the guide is essentially a lead rider/safety escort rather than a personalized tour, **50,000 IDR per person is actually on the generous side.**

If it was just you (and your girlfriend), 50K each or 50–100K total is perfectly appropriate. If it was a larger group, 50K total is fine.

**Bottom line: 50K is a good tip for a group ATV guide.** No adjustment needed.
