### SCOTT (2026-05-25T03:19)

### CLAUDE (2026-05-25T03:19)

**Both of you need to do eTravel for arrival.** No exceptions.

The Philippines eTravel Pass is mandatory for all arrivals — regardless of nationality, visa type, or travel purpose. There are no announced exemptions for 2026.

So:
- **Your girlfriend** → required (Filipino citizen returning)
- **You** → required (foreign national arriving)

The departure eTravel she did leaving PH was a separate registration. Arrival into Manila tonight is a new submission.

Register within 72 hours before your scheduled arrival at **etravel.gov.ph** — it's free and takes under 10 minutes. You'll get a QR code to show at immigration. Showing it on your phone screen is fully accepted.

Do it now if you haven't — you're likely inside that 72-hour window already.
