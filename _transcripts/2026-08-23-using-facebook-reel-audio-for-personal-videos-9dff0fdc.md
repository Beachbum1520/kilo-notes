On Facebook, here's how to use someone else's Reels audio for your own video:

- Open the Reel with the audio you want
- Tap the **audio name** at the bottom left of the screen (it's usually a music note icon + scrolling text)
- This opens the **audio page** — tap **"Use Audio"** or the camera icon
- It opens the Reels camera with that audio pre-loaded, ready for you to record your own clip
- Record your video, then edit/trim as needed and post

A few notes:
- If the audio isn't tappable (no link), the original may have used a private/personal audio track that can't be reused, or the original poster may have disabled remixing.
- If you're using licensed/commercial music, some tracks are restricted for reuse depending on your region or account type (Page vs personal profile — business Pages have more limited access to licensed music).
- You can also do this from the Reels tab: browse **Audio** search results directly and hit "Use Audio" without needing to find the original video first.

### CLAUDE (2026-08-23T22:06)
They want to save a Reel made with Facebook audio directly to their camera roll without posting. I'm thinking through what options Facebook actually offers here—likely saving as a draft or using an export/download feature before publishing, since there's no straightforward native "save to camera roll" button for drafts using others' audio.…Yes, you can — a couple of ways depending on what you want:

**Save as a draft (keeps the audio, stays in FB only)**
- After recording your clip with the audio, instead of tapping "Share/Post," tap the **back arrow** or **X**
- Choose **"Save as draft"**
- It'll sit in your Reels drafts (Profile → Reels → Drafts) — not visible to anyone else, and you can go back and export it later

**Export/save to your camera roll**
- On the final edit screen (after adding the audio, before posting), look for a **download icon** (down arrow) in the top corner
- Tap it to save the clip directly to your phone's photo gallery

One catch: if the audio is copyrighted/licensed music (not an original creator sound), Facebook sometimes **strips the audio track** when you export to your camera roll, since downloads leaving the app aren't covered by FB's licensing deals — but original sounds/personal audio usually export fine. If it strips it, using "Save as draft" instead is the reliable way to keep the audio intact, since the draft stays inside the app.
