### CLAUDE (2026-05-01T20:25)
 "The Last Kingdom" wrapped up its main story across 5 seasons on Netflix, then concluded with a feature-length film called "Seven Kings Must Die" (2023) which serves as the true finale.

The Season 5 finale resolves Uhtred's long quest to reclaim Bebbanburg from his treacherous cousin — he finally takes back his ancestral home, which is the emotional payoff fans had been waiting for since Season 1. However, the series doesn't fully conclude there because the larger question of England's unification is still unresolved.

"Seven Kings Must Die" is what most fans consider the real finale. It's set after King Edward's death, with rival claimants fighting for control of the kingdoms. Uhtred is pulled back into the political chaos and ultimately plays a decisive role at the Battle of Brunanburh, leading to the formation of a unified England under Æthelstan. The film gives Uhtred a fitting, contemplative sendoff that honors his dual Saxon-Dane identity.

Reception was generally positive — most viewers and critics felt it delivered a satisfying conclusion, though some book readers were disappointed by deviations from Bernard Cornwell's "Saxon Stories" novels (the show compresses and changes several events). If you watch the series, definitely watch the film right after — Season 5 alone feels incomplete without it.
