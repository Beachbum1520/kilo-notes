### CLAUDE (2026-07-30T21:41)
Your 75 lb guess is defensible — I'd call it a reasonable ballpark, but I can't confirm weight from photos, so treat it as an estimate either way.

What the pictures actually show: deep, well-filled bodies with good spring of rib, length starting to outrun height, solid flesh cover over the loin and ham, no ribbiness. They read as feeder pigs, not weaners. For 11-week-old Durocs on pasture that's consistent with somewhere in the 65–85 lb band, so 75 sits right in the middle.

If you want a real number without wrestling them onto a scale, use the heart-girth formula: measure girth right behind the front legs, and body length from between the ears to the base of the tail. Then **(girth² × length) ÷ 400 = lbs**. A cloth tape and one person holding a bucket of feed gets you close enough to quote honestly.

For Kaleb, either say "right at 75 lbs" if you're comfortable committing to it, or "somewhere around 70–80" — at that size he's getting a lot of pig for $150, which is worth him knowing.
