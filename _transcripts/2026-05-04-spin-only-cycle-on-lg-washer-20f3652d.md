### CLAUDE (2026-05-04T18:16)
 Based on your LG washer's panel, here's how to do a Spin Only cycle:

1. Press the **Power** button (left side) to turn it on.
2. Press the **Spin** button (in the bottom row of the right-side digital panel — it's labeled "Spin *Spin Only").
3. Press it again or hold it (the asterisk indicates "Spin Only" mode — usually a second press or a 3-second hold activates it). Watch the display to confirm it shows a spin-only cycle.
4. Adjust spin speed if desired by pressing **Spin** to cycle through Extra High / High / Medium / Low / No Spin.
5. Press and hold **Hold to Start** (the play/pause button next to the dial) until it begins.

Tip: make sure the lid is closed and the load is balanced — top-loaders will pause and try to redistribute if it's lopsided, which can make a "spin only" run take longer than expected.

If pressing Spin once doesn't switch it to a standalone spin cycle, hold it for 3 seconds (the "*Hold 3 seconds" note in the upper right of your panel applies to the asterisked buttons).
