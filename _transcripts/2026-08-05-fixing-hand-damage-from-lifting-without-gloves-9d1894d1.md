**How to put them on (trap bar, right hand):**
1. Loop end goes over the back of your hand, tail hanging down your palm side.
2. Thread the tail through the loop, pull snug at the wrist — tight enough it doesn't spin.
3. Lay the tail across the handle, then wrap it **under and around the handle toward you**, two full wraps.
4. Close your hand over the wrap and twist the bar slightly backward to cinch it.

Both hands wrapped before you set your feet. First session, use them on your warm-up sets to learn the wrap before you're under 245.
