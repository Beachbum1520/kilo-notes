### SCOTT (2026-05-20T03:45)
What’s the name of the calcium score heart screen test

### CLAUDE (2026-05-20T03:45)
 It's called a **Coronary Artery Calcium (CAC) score** test, also known as a **cardiac CT for calcium scoring** or sometimes a **calcium score CT scan**.

The formal clinical name is the **Agatston score**, after the cardiologist who developed the scoring methodology. Results are reported as a number (0–400+), where 0 means no detectable calcification and higher scores indicate greater plaque burden in the coronary arteries.
