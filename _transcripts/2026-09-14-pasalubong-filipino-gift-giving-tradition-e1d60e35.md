### CLAUDE (2026-09-14T13:00)
Pasalubong is a Filipino tradition of bringing gifts or souvenirs home for family and friends after traveling — whether from a trip abroad, a domestic trip, or even just a visit to another city. It comes from the root word "sulubong," meaning "to welcome" — the gift itself is part of how you're welcomed back.

Common pasalubong includes:
- Local delicacies (dried mangoes, polvoron, chocolate, regional specialties)
- Souvenirs or trinkets from the place visited
- Practical items like snacks, chocolates, or small gadgets from abroad (especially from OFWs — overseas Filipino workers)

It's a strong social expectation, not just a nice gesture — showing up without pasalubong after a trip can genuinely disappoint people. Airports and bus terminals in the Philippines often have entire pasalubong shops/centers for exactly this purpose.
