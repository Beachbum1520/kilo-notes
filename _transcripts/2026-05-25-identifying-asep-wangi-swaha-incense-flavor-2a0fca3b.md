### CLAUDE (2026-05-25T06:16)
 This is **"Asep Wangi Swaha – Sari Madu"** incense, produced by **Krishna Bhakti**.

**Translation:**
- **Asep Wangi** = Fragrant Incense (Indonesian)
- **Swaha** = a Sanskrit/Hindu ritual term, spoken when offering into sacred fire
- **Sari Madu** = **Honey Essence** / Pure Honey

**Flavor: Honey** 🍯

The label art confirms it — the central image shows a clay pot of honey with honeycomb. The gold sticker near the top references **"Abu Tidak Panas"** (ash that isn't hot) and **"Minyak... AM"** (likely a fragrant oil blend).

The small red sticker is a separate product tag for **"Dupa 99 – Sari Jahe"** (Ginger Essence incense), which is just a retail label stuck on the packaging — not the product itself.

So you've got **Balinese/Indonesian honey-scented incense** with Hindu devotional branding, made for ritual or aromatic use. Given your Bali trip timing, you likely picked this up locally — good find.
