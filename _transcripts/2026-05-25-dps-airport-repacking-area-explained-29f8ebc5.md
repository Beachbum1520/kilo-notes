### CLAUDE (2026-05-25T13:21)

The answer is simpler than you might expect.

The repacking area at DPS is specifically for passengers to repack their luggage **before** check-in, after visiting shops outside or near the airport.

In other words — Bali is a massive shopping destination. Tourists buy batik, woodcarvings, silver, textiles, oils, snacks, you name it. Then they show up at the airport and their bag is overweight or doesn't fit, or they bought stuff that needs to be distributed between bags to hit weight limits, or they picked up something fragile that needs better packing before handing it to the airline. The repacking area gives them a designated spot with space (and sometimes wrapping materials or staff assistance) to sort it all out before they hit the check-in counter.

It also has a secondary customs-adjacent logic: Indonesia customs flags luxury items like bags and jewelry as potential items for resale, and excessive quantities get extra scrutiny — so travelers sometimes need to redistribute or consolidate how things are packed before interacting with airline staff.

Given you're heading there with your girlfriend and Le Méridien → Ubud itinerary, expect to use it on the way out if she shops at all. Tegallalang area has a lot of artisan stuff. Just budget time before your departure.
