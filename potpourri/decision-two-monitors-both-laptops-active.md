# Two monitors, two laptops — both active at once (no KVM)

**Decided:** Stick with two monitors, each dedicated to one laptop, so both machines are active at the same time.

**Why:** "That way I can have both active at same time."

**Rejected alternatives:** Hooking both laptops to a single monitor via input switching, built-in KVM monitor, external KVM/dual-host dock, or picture-by-picture — Scott said "nope, don't like."
