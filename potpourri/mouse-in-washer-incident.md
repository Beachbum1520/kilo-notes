# Mouse caught in washer

A mouse had been evading Scott and his wife for about a week, leaving droppings all over the kitchen and his office; it ended up trapped in their washer.
Scott's wife vetoed drowning it in the washer. Scott said letting it go free was not an option.
Scott grabbed it with tongs, put it in a jar, killed it with a brick outside, and put it back in the jar in the outdoor garbage so the dogs wouldn't find it.
