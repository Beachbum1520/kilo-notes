# First job at 13 — commercial rabbit farm

At 13 he was "a bit of a wild child"; he has two brothers; his stepdad decided he was the one who needed a job to stay busy.
Stepdad found him a job at a small commercial rabbit farm (~1,000 rabbits) in Theodore, Alabama.
Paid $2/hour cash, 48 hours a week, Monday–Saturday.
Work: mostly shoveling rabbit manure, plus general labor — cutting grass, cleaning up remains after butcher days.
Was alone about 90% of the time, with WKSJ country radio piped all over the farm; couldn't escape it.
Worked there two summers and the school year in between; walked there after school.
His dad drove a propane truck whose office was across the street; rode in with him and got picked up at the end of the day.
Has been working pretty much full time ever since.

**Approx date:** mid-1980s (age 13; told July 2026)
