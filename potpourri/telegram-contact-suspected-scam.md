# Suspected scam contact met on Telegram

Scott met a woman on Telegram and suspected a scam but didn't know what the play was.
They did a brief video call, so he believed she was the Asian woman in her pictures.
He guessed she had used AI to put herself in a photo and that another photo was taken from the internet.
She claimed to be in NYC; he doubted it.
He looked up material connected to her, including a Yandex profile for "Альтпром" (Altprom) and a photo of the Citadel Business Center in St. Petersburg.
He later deleted her and got a "why did you delete" reply.
