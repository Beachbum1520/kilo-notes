# Giving to street kids in the Philippines

Every Philippines trip he gets handfuls of small coins and bills to give street kids he sees on his morning runs.
Last trip he made a big batch of bacon spaghetti, portioned it into small single-serve plastic bowls, and gave it out to the kids.
Doesn't know whether kids are part of organized begging; gives because it's right, not for the payoff.
Never runs the same route twice.
When kids sell small fabric squares or towels, he gives them the money and lets them keep their products.
Sometimes buys kids sodas, water, and treats like chips at 7-Eleven.
Does this in Manila, Ortigas, Cebu, or wherever he travels; also gives to young moms with babies.
