# What Scott needs in compactor bags

Scott wants a bag that does not collapse in the middle during compaction.
He rejected the genuine plastic Slip-Grip bags Claude suggested. His reason was that those don't have draw strings or the small holes that fit over the buttons on the compactor.
At his last house he used heavy-duty, plastic-coated paper compactor bags with handles.
