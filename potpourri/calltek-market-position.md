# CallTek market position and decline

Scott currently uses CallTek as a supplier, with about 3 dozen agents with them.
CallTek controls ~85% of the market, meaning it also serves his competitors.
About a year and 4 months earlier, CallTek's founder/CEO died unexpectedly at 46.
Parent company Allied Global took over, and it's been a downward spiral since — hence a big opportunity for a new provider.
At HITEC (two weeks before) the industry confirmed it is looking for another provider in this space.
