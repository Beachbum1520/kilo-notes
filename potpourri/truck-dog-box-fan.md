# 2018 Chevy 2500HD diesel with dog box and fan

Scott's truck is a 2018 Chevy 2500HD diesel.
It has a dog box with a 12V fan installed by the dog box installer, run through an aftermarket sealed toggle switch in the cab (red/green LEDs), always-hot, via a 40A relay.
The fan worked fine for a few years before failing in May 2026 (0V at the truck-side connector; switch LEDs dark).
The center high-mount third brake light over the back window also doesn't work.
He owns a multimeter.
He had no spare fuses on hand.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** Testing truck connector voltage with multimeter, 2026-05-03
