# Treated iron-stained whites with Iron Out

Scott had already washed his brown-stained whites twice in hot water with bleach; they had not gone in the dryer.
He had Bar Keepers Friend Soft Cleanser and Iron Out Rust Stain Remover spray gel on hand.
He soaked the whites in a bucket with Iron Out (more than a few sprays), found it worked quickly, soaked an hour, then washed on cold.
He likes his whites "super white" and wanted to add bleach to the cold wash.
