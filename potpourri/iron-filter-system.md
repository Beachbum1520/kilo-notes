# Home iron filter system (Waterite Fusion2 head)

Scott's home water system with the Waterite Fusion2 control head is an iron removal filter, not a softener.
He installed it himself on 11/5/2024.
The company he bought it from says it is maintenance-free for at least 3 years.
He has never added iron filter cleaner to the brine/resin tank.
He manually triggered a regen by holding the REGEN button, which worked.
