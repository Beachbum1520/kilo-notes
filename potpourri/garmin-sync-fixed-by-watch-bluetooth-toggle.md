# Garmin connection problem fixed

Scott's Garmin Epix showed "connected" in iOS Bluetooth but "not connected" in Garmin Connect, with no Bluetooth toggle in the app's iOS settings and no way to forget the watch in iOS.
Reinstalling Garmin Connect, restarting, and Reset Location & Privacy did not fix it.
Turning Bluetooth off on the watch made the stuck entry disappear from iOS; after re-pairing through the app, Scott reported "Fixed the garmin."
