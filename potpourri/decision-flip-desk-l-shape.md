# Flip the desk so the window is behind him, with an L-return

**Decided:** Flip the desk around so the window is at his back, and add a return piece along the wall to make an L-shaped desk.

**Why:** Scott's idea — makes the L-return work alongside his chair, and the walkway in front of the desk becomes natural.

**Rejected alternatives:** Keeping current orientation facing the window with a credenza on the window wall (not reachable); pushing the desk against a wall.
