# House has two water heaters

Scott's house has two hot water heaters. He already replaced one himself.
The one the washer draws from is original, of unknown age, and was there when they bought the house in mid-2024.
In April 2026 his whites were turning brown and an empty washer run showed brown water; he suspected the old water heater rather than the filter.
He had already purchased the replacement water heater; it was sitting in his garage waiting for him to install it himself.

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** Manual regeneration on Waterite Fusion² softener, 2026-04-15
