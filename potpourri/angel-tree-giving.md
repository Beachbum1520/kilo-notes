# Angel Tree Christmas giving

Does the Angel Tree at Christmas.
It angers him when donors give only "needs" (clothes, hygiene items) instead of wants (toys, games) and expect kids to be thankful; believes Angel Tree kids deserve the same kind of Christmas as other kids.
His practice: goes way over and above, covering both needs and wants, and also makes a Christmas meal box.
