# Dog box fan fault found: corroded connector on switch wire

Scott traced the fan wiring and found green corrosion at the connector on the switch wire.
Only the white wire was corroded; blue and black looked fine. He planned to repair just the white wire.
He confirmed fuse #43 and fuse #38 (CHMSL) were fine by swapping the fuse into #54 (washer), which worked.
