# Weekly coaching chat structure

Start a new conversation each week and continue it through the week with insights, feedback, symptoms, sleep/HRV, and work/travel/farm context. Give week analysis as it progresses: what landed, what slipped, trends against Armor Build (lean mass, BMD, axial loading), aerobic efficiency, and recovery; call out stress accumulation; say if he should go easier or harder. Do not sandbag.

**Source:** w/c 6-22 - weekly coaching discussion and feedback, 2026-06-23
