# Dead hang grip: thumbs on top

**Decided:** Dead hangs are done thumbs-on-top (not wrapped).

**Why:** Scott's own results: 31 sec thumbs on top, 7 sec thumbs wrapped, 11 sec back to thumbs on top; the bounce back was due to thumb position.

**Rejected alternatives:** Thumbs-wrapped full grip, which Claude initially recommended.

**Would revisit if:** unknown.

**Approx date:** June 2026
