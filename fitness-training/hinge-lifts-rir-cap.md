# Axial hinges capped at 1 RIR

**Decided:** Trap Bar DL and Barbell SLDL cap at 1 RIR (never to failure); all other lifts take the last working set to failure in peak weeks.

**Why:** Failure on free-loaded axial hinges is a spine risk training solo at 55; hinges drive the BMD stimulus and failure isn't worth the axial/systemic recovery cost. Guided/machine/DB/assisted lifts fail safely.

**Rejected alternatives:** Taking hinges to failure.

**Would revisit if:** unknown.

**Approx date:** July 2026
