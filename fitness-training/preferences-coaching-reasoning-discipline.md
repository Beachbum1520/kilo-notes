# Reasoning discipline for coaching chats

When Scott hands over numbers or lists, compute the answer from them before theorizing — diff first, theorize only if the data doesn't resolve it. Anchor on the counts/totals he gives. Do not reverse a correct answer just because he pushes back without a factual reason. When he is entering things live, go one day / one exercise at a time — do not dump multiple days unless asked. If he says a task is simple, treat that as a signal to stop over-engineering it.

**Source:** W/C - 7/6 Weekly Coaching, 2026-07-05
