# Scott's view of deload weeks

Scott felt the deload workout was "sissy level" and questioned only 12 minutes of sauna after it.
His son, a personal trainer, is not a fan of deload weeks and does not see the value.
In his Ironman days Scott loved recovery weeks as a real break from heavy training.
From a strength/gym perspective a deload feels to Scott like a bit of a waste.
