# "HR cap always overrides pace" is not accurate

**What was wrong:** Claude's draft kickoff prompt stated as an invariant rule: "HR cap always overrides pace."

**Correct version:** Scott said this was not accurate and told Claude to search memory and other chats about it and his blood donation. The corrected rule he accepted: on easy/aerobic runs the HR cap governs and pace is whatever it is; on prescribed quality sessions pace/effort is the target and HR rises to the session ceiling (≤145 efficiency, up toward threshold for hard work); the post-donation cap is temporary and recovery status must be confirmed before prescribing.
