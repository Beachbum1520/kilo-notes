# Weekly coaching kickoff expectations

At the weekly kickoff, load all project memory and treat it as active context: goals, physiology, history, constraints, formats.
Pull current files from the "Scott Watts 2026 ATP Data" Drive folder (completed workouts, test data, planning docs) without waiting for uploads.
Before anything else, state where Scott is in the arc: today's date, current phase, recovery status, which strength block/meso and week he's in, days out from the next race, and any active travel.
Deliver back: (1) a week analysis covering what landed, what slipped, trends against Armor Build (lean mass, BMD, axial loading), aerobic efficiency, and recovery (sleep/HRV), calling out stress accumulation; (2) the next 1–2 weeks scheduled with the right workout on the right day and real progression; (3) pushback where what he did conflicts with where he's going.
After orienting, ask only for what is missing.

**Source:** ATP training week setup and data access, 2026-06-03
