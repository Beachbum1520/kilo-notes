# Why Scott started cold plunging

Brown fat development was one of the big things that originally got Scott into cold plunging.
Scott says he added the cold plunge knowing it wasn't significant for longevity; the idea was that a daily dopamine spike would help his motivation to get to the gym and help him not crave bad habits as much.
He recalls Andrew Huberman citing about 11–12 minutes of total weekly cold exposure for maximum benefit.
He compares the plunge to going to the gym and running: he'd rather be in bed eating Oreos, but values the challenge and suffering of doing it.
