# How the RP app actually works

Days can be changed through "Manage Days" in the 3-dots menu without going back.
Day names can't be renamed; they stay tied to weekdays.
Scott concluded the colored bars on muscle groups just identify muscle groups, not priority tier.
Turning on "Use preferred exercise types" prevents turning barbells off, so it was left off.
