# Dislikes HR-capped shuffle running

Scott does not enjoy the walk/shuffle running needed to stay under the 122 HR cap. He asked how long it would last.
He described it as feeling like a "walking dead geriatric cancer recovery patient," with old ladies on walkers passing him.
He says he doesn't look at pace. His frustration is that as soon as the running starts to feel good, his HR gets too high and he has to walk.
