# Training plan presentation style

Copy/paste ready for TrainingPeaks; fully executable with zero interpretation; no fluff, motivational commentary, narrative paragraphs, emojis, or ambiguous shorthand; clean spacing, structured blocks, consistent punctuation.
Strongly dislikes: incomplete instructions; missing HR targets, pace, distance, or treadmill mph; ranges instead of exact values; "coach speak"; overly verbose explanations.
