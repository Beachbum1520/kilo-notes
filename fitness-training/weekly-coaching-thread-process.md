# Weekly coaching thread process

Scott starts a new coaching conversation each week and continues it throughout the week with insights, feedback, symptoms, sleep/HRV, and work/travel/farm context.
He provides completed strength workouts as RP app screenshots, text copy, or pasted workouts; cold plunge and run data come from FIT files in Drive, or he uploads them or pastes the capture doc.
He builds the next week's plan on Sunday at his desk.
At week's end he has the chat produce an end-of-week handoff that gets pasted verbatim into the next week's chat.

**Approx date:** July 2026
