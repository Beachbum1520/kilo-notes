# TrainingPeaks cold plunge entry

Cold plunges are separate TP entries formatted "Cold Plunge – 3:00 – 48°F"; usually 5–6x/week, immediately upon waking, before coffee or food, typically before training.
Progression: post-marathon reset to ~60°F, then drop 2°F progressively back toward 46°F.
