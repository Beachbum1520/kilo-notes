# Weekly end-of-week handoff + kickoff command system

**Decided:** Every Sunday, Scott will post a command in the current week's chat asking Claude to summarize in detail everything relevant from the week-long conversation, then paste that summary into a new chat along with his weekly kickoff command. Claude drafted two commands (END-OF-WEEK HANDOFF and an updated WEEKLY COACHING KICKOFF with a "reasoning discipline" section); Scott asked for both so he could save them in a notepad on his laptop, then said "generate the handoff. we'll see how it goes."

**Why:** Scott said he doesn't have time to give up every Sunday to sessions like this one; the command was meant to be the trigger to discuss the week's workouts and provide feedback Claude uses as his coach to keep him moving forward, injury-free, achieving goals. He wants to feel he's getting his money's worth from the $100/month expense. "We need to fix this or I can't continue."

**Rejected alternatives:** Continuing one long chat without a structured handoff.

**Would revisit if:** The trial ("we'll see how it goes") doesn't work.
