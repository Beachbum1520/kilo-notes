# One lunch for the whole week

**Decided:** To start, Scott settles on a single lunch for every day of the week, so breakfast and lunch are both locked in and dinner is where the variety comes from.

**Why:** He doesn't mind eating the same thing several times a week.

**Rejected alternatives:** Five different prepped lunches.

**Would revisit if:** He later said it's "not a locked lunch forever."

**Approx date:** June 2026
