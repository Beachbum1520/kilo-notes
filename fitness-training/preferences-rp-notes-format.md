# RP exercise notes: loads plus cues and progress-based reminders

For RP exercise notes, include the number of sets, target weight and reps, the RIR/failure call, cues, and reminders based on his progress, goals and weekly feedback. He wants "both" the numbers and the cues, not stripped-down loads alone.
Spell out the number of sets clearly so there's no ambiguity (e.g., don't mix in feeder sets in a way that makes the set count unclear).
The notes should help him in the moment to get the most effective, efficient workout.

**Source:** Wc 6/30 - Weekly coaching, 2026-06-29
