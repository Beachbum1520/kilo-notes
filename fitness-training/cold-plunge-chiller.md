# Cold plunge chiller

Scott's cold plunge has a 1.5 hp chiller that can hold anywhere from 34°F to 102°F wherever he sets it.
His plunge setpoint has been 56°F for weeks.
Scott finds 56°F easy; 3 minutes is easy and it doesn't produce much of an initial cold response anymore — he feels he could go much longer (not suggesting he should).
