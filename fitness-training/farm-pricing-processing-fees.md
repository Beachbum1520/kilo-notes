# Farm pricing and processing fees

Pork: $5.50/lb hanging weight; kill fee $40; cut fee $1.15/lb.
Beef: $6.75/lb hanging weight; kill fee $75; cut fee $1.10/lb.
Dexter HW typically 475–550 lb; whole Dexter ~$3,200–$3,700 all-in (310–360 lb take-home).
Take-home rates: beef ~65%, pork ~80%.
