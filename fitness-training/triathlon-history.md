# Triathlon and endurance history

Scott's first long-distance triathlon was IM Augusta 70.3 on September 28, 2014.
He went on to do a few more 70.3s and two full Ironmans in 2016 and 2017.
After that his "all or nothing" personality kicked in and he got fat again, up to 232 lbs, before his recent get-fit journey.
Swim training is his least favorite part of triathlon, but the swim is his favorite event on race day; he considers it easy, relatively short, scenic, and refreshing.
He sold his tri bike a few years ago and will not buy another; he considers a road bike fine for a 70.3.
He has a pretty good indoor bike setup and doesn't really need to ride outside much, though he can.
