# Perilla mint on property

Perilla mint (Perilla frutescens) is present on the property; most toxic at flowering/seed stage. Pigs are less susceptible than ruminants but should be excluded from shaded fence lines, creek banks, and woodland edges where it concentrates. Watch for respiratory distress.
