# GI effects after tirzepatide restart

After returning to the 10mg shot, Scott had diarrhea (around June 12–13, 2026).
He took Imodium once around 2 AM (two pills) and 4–5 Pepto chews the next day.
By June 14 he felt a little constipated, and the scale was up almost 3 lb day over day.
He considered a MiraLAX dose to prevent getting stopped up.
