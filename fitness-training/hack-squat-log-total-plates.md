# Hack squat logging convention: log totals

**Decided:** Scott logs the hack squat as a total ("I'd rather log totals I think") rather than plates per side. The convention Claude spelled out and Scott then used: plates only, both sides, leaving out the carriage and the machine's percentage math (e.g., 45+25 per side logged as 140).

**Why:** His preference. Per side vs. total had caused confusion ("So long as 135 and not 270 total?").

**Rejected alternatives:** Logging plates per side; logging total including the carriage; logging effective resistance.
