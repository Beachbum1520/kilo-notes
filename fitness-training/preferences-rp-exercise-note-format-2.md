# RP exercise note format

Format: "## sets – ## lbs (dial/pin OR spelled-out plates per side) – ## reps – ## RIR – cues." Set count leads every line.
Barbell/trap/sled: spell plate math per side in plain English, never jargon. Machines: "set the pin, no math."
