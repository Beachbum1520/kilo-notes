# OneLife gym locations

Scott has two OneLife locations: one near his home and one near the office.
The OneLife near his home has "the biggest range of machines and equipment I have ever seen at a gym," including a hack squat.
The home-side OneLife has a sauna.
He expects the hack squat machines at the two clubs to be close to each other.
The home OneLife trap bar is 45 lb.
Leg day at the home OneLife on Tuesday evening was busy, with people camped on the leg extension and calf machines. Wednesday after work the gym was "crazy crowded."
