# Cold plunge setpoint changed to 56°F

**Decided:** Set the chiller to 56°F (from 58°F); TrainingPeaks cold plunge cards to read 56°F.

**Why:** Scott thought 58 was too close to the ~59°F threshold; he believes the plunge is tuned in Celsius and converts for display (often shows 59 when programmed to 58), and suspects hysteresis of ~3 degrees before cooling triggers. He does not want to get colder just for the sake of getting colder.

**Rejected alternatives:** Holding at 58 (Claude's recommendation); 55; returning to 48-50.

**Would revisit if:** unknown.
