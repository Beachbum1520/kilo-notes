# Account takeover attack on wife's email and joint checking (Aug 1, 2026)

On Saturday morning (Aug 1, 2026) Scott woke at 6am to his wife receiving hundreds of 2FA code emails from someone trying to reset all her accounts.
He discovered her email had been hacked and a forward set up so the attacker received the codes.
The attacker accessed their joint checking account, changed the address, and set up a new Zelle recipient and bill pay.
Scott spent most of the day trying to get ahead of the hacker; he still got to the gym for his strength workout.
