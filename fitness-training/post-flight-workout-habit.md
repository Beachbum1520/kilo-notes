# Likes a workout after a long-haul flight

Scott finds a workout after a long flight "actually kind of nice."
On long-haul flights he usually wears about the same thing he wears to the gym on strength days, so he can check his bags at the hotel front desk and go straight to the hotel gym.
He is Marriott Titanium Elite and expects hotels to allow this. His fallback is an Anytime Fitness day pass nearby, and his worst case is working out later in the day.
