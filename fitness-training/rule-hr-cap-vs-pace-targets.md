# HR cap vs. pace targets on runs

On easy/aerobic runs the HR cap governs, and pace is whatever it turns out to be.
On prescribed quality sessions pace/effort is the target and HR rises to the session ceiling: ≤145 for efficiency work, up toward threshold for hard work.
The post-donation HR cap is temporary (donated May 6, 2026). Confirm where he is in recovery before prescribing; don't treat it as permanent.
