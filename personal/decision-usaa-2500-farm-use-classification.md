# USAA: 2018 Silverado 2500 classified as farm use

**Decided:** The USAA rep said Scott had both farm-use and business-use options and it was up to him. He first picked business use and was transferred. The next rep said it's farming, not business, since business use means something like a consultant driving to clients, and a truck used only to run the farm is farming. Scott went with farm-use classification, which saved him $200.

**Why:** The truck only runs the farm. Scott asked whether farm use covers a farm that sells product commercially, and the rep said yes.

**Rejected alternatives:** Business-use classification, which cost more and, per the rep, didn't fit.
