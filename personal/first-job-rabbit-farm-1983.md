# First job: commercial rabbit farm, 1983

In 1983, at age 13, Scott worked at a commercial rabbit farm shoveling rabbit manure 48 hours a week.
He was paid $2/hour in cash, which he says was less than the minimum wage of about $3.35/hour at the time.
His 48 hours netted about $96 a week in cash, which he spent on basic necessities at Winn-Dixie.
He felt he was paid very poorly even for 1983.
He describes himself then as a young boy willing to do whatever it took to scratch out a living.

**Approx date:** 1983 (discussed August 2026)

**Source:** 1983 rabbit farm wages in today's pesos, 2026-08-07
