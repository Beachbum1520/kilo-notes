# Car-buying approach: no dealer visits, best out-the-door price wins

**Decided:** Not going to any dealer and not doing a lot of negotiation. Find 2–3 dealerships with the truck, call or email them for their best out-the-door price, and give the deal to the best price. No test drive, since they are familiar with the product.

**Why:** Avoids games and back-and-forth. They have had a Yukon since 2011 and rented a Suburban for a family trip.

**Rejected alternatives:** Dealer visits, test drives and back-and-forth negotiation.

**Would revisit if:** Not stated.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** New SUV Purchase, 2026-05-05
