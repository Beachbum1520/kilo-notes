# Son's Veloster is titled in Scott's name

Scott's 26-year-old son doesn't live with him. The son refuses to get the title of his car transferred to his own name and get his own insurance.
The car (a 2013 Veloster) is still in Scott's name. That is why Scott keeps it insured: if the son has a wreck, "they'll come after me."
They have tried "many many times" to get him to transfer it. Scott can sign the title over, but believes his son has to be the one to take it in and transfer it.
Scott listed the son as the Veloster's primary driver and updated the garaging address. That added about $100 to the premium.
