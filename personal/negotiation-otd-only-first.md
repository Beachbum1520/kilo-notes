# Negotiate out-the-door price only; keep trade and cash down out of it

**Decided:** Scott asked for the dealer outreach to leave out the trade and cash down and ask only for the OTD price. "Trade is not introduced until have agreement on price. Thats car buying 101."

**Why:** So the dealer can't play games across several variables at once.

**Rejected alternatives:** Bundling the trade and down payment into the first ask.

**Would revisit if:** none.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** New Caddy, 2026-08-19
