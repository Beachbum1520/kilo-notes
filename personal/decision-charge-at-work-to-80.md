# Charge at work to 80%

**Decided:** Charge at the Cox work ChargePoint to 80% the next day, and generally prefer work charging.

**Why:** Work charging costs about half as much per kWh as home. Scott's actual sessions: $4.15 for 53.95 kWh at work vs $4.45 for 27.78 kWh at home. It also adds range buffer for the 77-mile drive home.

**Rejected alternatives:** Relying on the range buffer and charging only at home.
