# Original criteria for a commuter vehicle

**Decided:** Scott wanted "a decent crossover suv style vehicle that will last many many years with little to no hassles." An EV as his daily commuter appealed to him (no gas stations), but he said "I'm not married to that idea." He would install a home charger. He will also drive it to the farm now and then, so it needs enough ground clearance: not full off-road, but it can't be low clearance.

**Why:** Low hassle and long ownership.

**Rejected alternatives:** none stated at this point.

**Would revisit if:** He later changed his top criterion to self-driving (see the next item).

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** New Caddy, 2026-08-19
