# Granddaughter Poppy Lou Street

Scott's daughter had her baby on Saturday, July 18, 2026: Poppy Lou Street, his first granddaughter.
Scott's father was "Pop" to all the grandkids except Scott's daughter, who, unprompted, called him "Poppy" her whole life. His daughter chose the baby's name from that.
The son-in-law's father is already "Pop" to grandkids on that side of the family.
Scott will be "Poppa," a nod to a long-running family joke ("I love it when you call me Big Poppa"). He wanted "grandpa," but his wife and daughter decided otherwise.
In the week after the birth, Scott's role was "do whatever my wife says so she can help my daughter."
