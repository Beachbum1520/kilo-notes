# 2500HD glow plug codes

A scan of the 2500HD (with some duplicates) showed glow plug codes for cylinders 2, 3 and 5: P0672, P0673, P0675, P06C6, P06C7, P06C9, P1339 and P133A.
The truck starts and runs fine despite the codes.

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** Oil pressure gauge reading zero while engine running, 2026-04-13
