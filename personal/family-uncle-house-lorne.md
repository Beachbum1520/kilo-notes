# Uncle Randy's house and brother Lorne

Randy is Scott's uncle, his dad's brother.
Scott's grandparents, whom he calls "Gran Gran and Pop", built the house.
Scott's father lived in the house after retiring, until he died in 2018.
Scott's younger brother Lorne moved in shortly after and has lived there rent-free.
Randy's only real requirement was that Lorne keep the yard up. According to Scott's wife, Randy sent pictures about a month earlier showing it all overgrown.
Randy never liked how Lorne maintained the house. He didn't want to sell for sentimental reasons and didn't want it sitting empty.
Randy has now decided to sell.
Lorne recently remarried and seems to no longer live there. Lorne's son Ethan does.
On Scott's last visit, Lorne had piled their dad's belongings in one or more rooms and his own things in others.
