# 2500 towing setup and Scott's view on ratings

The 2500 has SuperSprings installed.
It runs Load Range E tires, and Scott always airs them to 80 psi cold when hauling.
Scott's view: he doesn't care what the yellow door sticker says. He believes the only difference between the 2500 and 3500 is one set of leaf springs, which the SuperSprings more than make up for, so his truck can pull whatever a 3500 is rated for.
He believes that if insurance denied a claim, he'd have a strong case to litigate.

**Approx date:** September 2026

**Source:** Rentals near Cox headquarters, 2026-09-24
