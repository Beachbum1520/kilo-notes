# Self-driving became the top and only criterion

**Decided:** Scott asked which car he should look at "if my top decision criteria is self driving." He then made clear it was the top and only criterion: consider every car available in the US today, not a shortlist. Clearance was not a filter, and cheapest was a separate criterion.

**Why:** He was watching YouTube videos of hands-free highway driving. His commute is highway-heavy.

**Rejected alternatives:** Reliability-first ranking, which favored the Lexus RX 350h.
