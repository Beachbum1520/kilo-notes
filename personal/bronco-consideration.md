# Ford Bronco considered as the commuter

**Decided:** Scott floated dropping EVs entirely for a Bronco. He wanted the trim that looks most like the old traditional Broncos, "not the girly models." He first said he didn't want the 4-door, then said "I think the 4 door is ok. Probably preferred." (the Heritage Edition is 4-door only). He called it "just a consideration": if he got one, it would definitely be the commute vehicle. He asked for fuel costs to be ignored.

**Why:** Classic look.

**Rejected alternatives:** 2-door Base/Badlands.

**Would revisit if:** In the end he bought the Lyriq instead.

**Approx date:** August 2026
