# Move to the Philippines at retirement

Scott plans to move to the Philippines "as soon as humanly possible." Realistically, that means when he retires.
He plans to live on Social Security plus however much of the retirement account a divorce judge leaves him. He says he is OK even if that is zero.
His goal is to protect enough to live comfortably in the Philippines, take a few small vacations a year, and have decent healthcare.
In his words, the aim is "to finally get a shot at living the life I want with the time I have left."

**Approx date:** July 2026

**Source:** Retirement balance projection to age 62, 2026-07-19
