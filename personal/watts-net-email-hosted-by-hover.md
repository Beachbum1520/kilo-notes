# watts.net email is rented from Hover, not owned

Scott does not own the watts.net domain; Hover owns it and Scott buys email on it (scott@watts.net).
Scott's Hover account holds mailboxes for himself, his wife, and his kids.
2FA is on Scott's Hover account, and the 2FA email on it is still his own.
Hover's webmail interface lets a mailbox user change the password and set up forwarding without the Hover account login.
Scott's wife's address is Angie@watts.net, and it is her only email address.
