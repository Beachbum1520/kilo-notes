# Home charger: do it once

**Decided:** "I'm doing this once." Scott wanted a single charger purchase and install with no future upgrade. He wanted the Tesla Universal Wall Connector, but Tesla's store required Tesla ownership, Best Buy was sold out, Home Depot didn't have it and Amazon wanted $800.

**Why:** Buy once.

**Rejected alternatives:** Tesla Universal (not available at a fair price). GM PowerUp 2 ($899). ChargePoint HomeFlex (open-box listing, NEMA 6-50).

**Would revisit if:** n/a
