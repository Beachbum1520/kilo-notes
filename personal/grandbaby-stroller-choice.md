# Stroller for the grandbaby

Scott was choosing between a Thule Urban Glide 3 2-in-1 bundle (~$1,100) and a Joie Ginger DLX (~$400) as a gift for his granddaughter.
His daughter is a runner.
His daughter said she can't put the baby in the stroller seat until she can sit up, so she wants the bassinet for walks during the first 6 months.
The granddaughter was 2 weeks old as of August 1, 2026.
