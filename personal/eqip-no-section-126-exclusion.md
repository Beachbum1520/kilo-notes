# EQIP payments reported as income, no Section 126 exclusion

**Decided:** No Section 126 exclusion on the EQIP payments. They are reported as income, and all EQIP contracts will be finished before the farm is sold.

**Why:** The farm loses enough money to absorb the EQIP payments. Finishing the contracts before a sale avoids passing obligations to a buyer.

**Rejected alternatives:** Excluding the EQIP payments under Section 126.

**Would revisit if:** unknown

**Approx date:** July 2026

**Source:** Retirement balance projection to age 62, 2026-07-19
