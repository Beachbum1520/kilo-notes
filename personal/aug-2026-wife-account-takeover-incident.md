# August 2026 account takeover of wife's accounts

Someone set a forward on his wife's watts.net mailbox to an unfamiliar Gmail address. Scott didn't set it up, and his wife doesn't know how to set up forwarding.
It came to light when she woke up to a pile of 2FA code emails and showed them to Scott, asking "what's this."
Per his wife, it started a couple of days earlier with her Walmart account. At the same time Scott got a fraud alert on one of his credit cards (a Visa), apparently saved in her Walmart account. He answered "No," which declined the charge and cancelled the card.
Her Walmart account had a pending curbside pickup order at a store in southern Florida (Hialeah) for beer and energy drinks, for the same amount as the fraud alert. The charge was declined, so the order never went through.
Her Instagram account was taken over and the username changed; Instagram asked her for a backup code she doesn't have.
Her Disney+ account was switched to Spanish and had active sessions from devices in Peru.
Reset emails kept arriving from Disney+ and HBO Max after the forward was removed.
Someone added an unauthorized Zelle recipient on her USAA logon; his wife said it wasn't her. Her USAA address and email were then changed.
Her mailbox also held a defaced Upside verification email ("Cloud Lounge"/"Content Secured" overlay). It arrived before her password change.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Email forwarding security concern, 2026-08-01
