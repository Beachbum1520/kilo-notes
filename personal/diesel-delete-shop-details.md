# Diesel delete shop answers

Allison transmission tuning is extra, not included in the $5,000.
The tune has 5 settings. The shop recommends staying at 3 or below, because higher settings risk the transmission, and 5 is for the drag strip.
The drop tube is 3" and turns into the factory 4" further down. The last 2–3 feet can be upgraded to 5".
The shop wouldn't give an itemized breakdown.
Tunes are custom-written for the specific truck. Dusty gets them custom-made from a guy in Tennessee.
Scott's wife took the truck to the shop and asked the questions while Scott was in the city.
