# Watts Way Farms property

The farm is 90 acres in Franklin, GA.
It is not raw land. It is a fully operational cattle ranch.
It has a barn, multiple deep water wells, automatic cattle waterers, and over 8,000 feet of interior fencing.
It has improved crossings over year-round, cold-water, spring-fed creeks.
It has a large 2-acre pond and full cattle-handling facilities.
Scott called it "a no-shit cattle ranch."
