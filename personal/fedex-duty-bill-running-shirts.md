# FedEx duty bill on running club shirts for Philippines team

Scott ordered running club shirts for his team in the Philippines before his last trip there.
He didn't realize at order time that the seller was overseas; he paid the seller including a shipping fee, received the shirts, and handed them out to his team in the Philippines.
FedEx later billed him import duty; the invoice (final demand, past due June 22, 2026) totaled $26.88: $11.88 customs duty plus a $15.00 FedEx fee.
The shipment came from Karachi, Pakistan: 20 men's cotton shirts declared at $40.
