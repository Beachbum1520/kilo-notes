# Personal Training Coach tool — what it is

A private, single-user (later two-user) tool that reads Scott's existing fitness data, sends it to an LLM with his athlete profile, and returns adaptable training plans in his TrainingPeaks format.
Not a product: no other users, no auth for strangers, no deploy required for v1; runs locally.
The coaching engine is the point; everything else is plumbing around it.

**Approx date:** June 2026
