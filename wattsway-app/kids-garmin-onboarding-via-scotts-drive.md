# Kids' Garmin onboarding goes through Scott's Drive, done asynchronously

**Decided:** Joshua's and Danielle's FitnessSyncer files go to Scott's own Google Drive (their subfolders). The kids create free FitnessSyncer accounts and connect Garmin on their own time, then send Scott their FitnessSyncer logins. Scott finishes the Google Drive destination and the folder-ID step himself.

**Why:** Scott saw no reason for a screen-share session and wanted to just give them the code. He felt having the kids connect their own Drives and share folders was extra setup work for them.

**Rejected alternatives:** A live screen-share or in-person onboarding session. Kids connecting their own Google Drive and sharing the folder to the service account ("seems like extra work for them"). A Terra-class unified API ("sounds expensive").

**Would revisit if:** Garmin's official API reopens, or WattsWay becomes a scalable commercial product. Scott asked what the answer would be in that case: one-click OAuth.

**Approx date:** July 2026

**Source:** Importing Garmin data with FitnessSyncer, 2026-07-10
