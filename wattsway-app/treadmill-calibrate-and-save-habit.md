# Scott calibrates treadmill distance on his watch after every run

At the end of every treadmill run, after stopping the activity, Scott uses the watch's "calibrate and save" option and changes the distance to whatever the treadmill says.
So Garmin Connect holds the treadmill-console distance, while the TCX export carries the watch's original estimate. On the June 28 run the two were 4.73 mi versus 5.1 mi.
Garmin Connect confirmed an average HR of 117 for that June 28 treadmill run.

**Approx date:** July 2026

**Source:** Importing Garmin data with FitnessSyncer, 2026-07-10
