# Initial scope of the fitness app: personal use, not a saleable product

**Decided:** The fitness training front end (integrating his existing data pipelines plus AI coaching for adaptable plans toward specified goals) was for his own use, and maybe his daughter's — not a saleable product.

**Why:** Personal tool; he was not looking to produce a product for sale.

**Rejected alternatives:** Building a commercial product.

**Would revisit if:** Later superseded — scope expanded to family distribution and "treat it as commercial long-term" (see separate items).

**Approx date:** June 2026

**Source:** Watts Way Fitness App, 2026-06-12
