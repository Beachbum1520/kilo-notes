# Get strength sessions into Strava via Garmin watch activity

**Decided:** Log strength sessions as a Strength activity on the Garmin Epix so they sync Garmin Connect → Strava.

**Why:** Wants strength days visible in Strava for the social side — he has a network of friends who are also trying to live a healthy life.

**Rejected alternatives:** Direct RP app → Strava sync (not available). Noted Fitbod used to post more exercise detail to Strava, but accepted the thinner Garmin entry.
