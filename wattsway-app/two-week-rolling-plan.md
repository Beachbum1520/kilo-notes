# Workout plan builder uses a two-week rolling plan

**Decided:** Move to a two-week rolling plan: build two weeks at a time, then every Sunday update the following week's plan based on the completed week's data (workouts, recovery, etc.).

**Why:** Plans adapt to actual completed data.

**Rejected alternatives:** Generating one week at a time.

**Would revisit if:** unknown.

**Approx date:** July 2026

**Source:** Watts Way Fitness App, 2026-06-12
