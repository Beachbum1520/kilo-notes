# Build WattsWay as a PWA, not a native iOS app

**Decided:** Proceed with a web app (PWA) and "stay the course" rather than native iOS.

**Why:** He doesn't have a Mac and can't use one. After asking what a PWA is and looking before deciding, he proceeded with the PWA path; after his son suggested alternatives, he chose to stay the course.

**Rejected alternatives:** Native iOS using his oldest son's Apple dev account; son's suggestion to get around Xcode via a GitHub-hosted virtual machine or Codemagic.

**Would revisit if:** He wants to commercialize and needs a native/App Store presence (he asked how hard switching would be later).

**Approx date:** June–July 2026

**Source:** Watts Way Fitness App, 2026-06-12
