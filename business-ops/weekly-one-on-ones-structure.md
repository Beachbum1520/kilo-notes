# Weekly 1:1s with direct reports

**Decided:** Put 1:1s with direct reports back on the calendar as weekly, one-hour meetings. Direct reports must come prepared to discuss their top items currently in progress, long-term plans, headwinds and tailwinds, obstacles and opportunities. The agenda is a guide, not a rigid plan. 1:1s are to be prioritized over other meetings, and scheduled for times when Scott and the report are in the office together.

**Why:** To level-set expectations and make the 1:1s productive; he wanted the invite message kept at a macro level.
