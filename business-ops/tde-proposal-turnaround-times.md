# TDE proposal and FSR effort by deal type

Budgetary proposal effort: EOL/MACD about 2 hours; New Build/Takeover about 4 hours; Network Upgrade about 4 hours.
Work starts once all required information is in (site assessment, survey if needed, etc.).
Delays are usually caused by missing information that Sales or the SE has to get from the customer.
After verbal confirmation on the budgetary proposal, time to final depends on how many changes are needed.
Final Sales Review: EOL 2 hours; everything else 4 hours.
