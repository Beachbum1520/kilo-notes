# DG2 deck positioning: certified gateway and approved provider

**Decided:** Lean into the fact that the DG is certified and call it out, and clearly call out that BPRF is the approved provider for major brands.

**Why:** Approved-provider status is what gets BPRF to the table to sell; the DG plus service and support is what wins the deals.

**Rejected alternatives:** Slide selling co-equal features without credentials.
