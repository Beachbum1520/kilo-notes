# Vicky promoted off Scott's account

Vicky, one of Scott's NOC team leads, is being promoted off his account to a competitor account also serviced by CTC. Scott supports it if it's what she feels is best for her career.
Scott says CTC is still, to this day, moving trained people off his account, which he considers a direct violation of the contract's training-investment commitments.
Vicky's NOC Lead role is a named, pay-per-headcount position under a different SOW than the call center agreement, so Scott doesn't see what Tier 1 support levels have to do with it.
Scott said whether to recruit for any position is CTC's decision.
