# TDEs build the proposal, not SEs or sales managers

**Decided:** TDEs build the proposal itself, pulling the FSR-approved design and technical specs into the existing proposal templates. It was never "2 SEs do proposals", and Scott told Jady he had not communicated that to anyone. The plan had been in motion for a while.

**Why:** There were multiple cases of technical info being transposed between FSR approval and the customer-facing proposal when a non-technical sales manager owned the entry step. This cost BPRF on installs. Because TDEs work from existing templates, the incremental time is minimal.

**Rejected alternatives:** Non-technical sales managers or 2 SEs doing proposal entry.

**Would revisit if:** Jady's concern about bottlenecking the 58-seller team proves out.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Navigating boss disagreement on merger position, 2026-08-20
