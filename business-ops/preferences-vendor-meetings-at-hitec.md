# Vendors who want a meeting must schedule it

When a vendor requests a meeting, they do the work: book a scheduled time. Scott is the customer; he won't go to a vendor's booth. He's "not a booth babe" — he's only at BPRF's own booth if he has absolutely nothing else on the calendar (historically just a couple of hours total across a multi-day event), so don't offer his booth as a fallback either.

**Source:** Clarifying meeting expectations for HITEC, 2026-05-29
