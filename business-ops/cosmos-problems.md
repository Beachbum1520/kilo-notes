# COSMOS monitoring is failing

COSMOS, the proactive monitoring system CTC provides, "just ain't cutting it," according to Scott.
His team recommended turning off the Cosmos-to-CAS integration and going back to PNOC manually creating tickets for legitimate alerts.
That disrupts Helen's plan to integrate PNOC into the NOC, which has been planned for years and was waiting on Cosmos to be fully functional.
Figures Scott supplied: 45,642 tickets were created on August 31, 2026 alone, and only about 104 of them were email tickets. He said August produced about a third of all tickets created since 2014.
Cosmos ticket generation was switched on toward the end of 2025 (around July/August), with several false starts where it had to be turned off because of Cosmos issues. Scott said the specifics aren't worth digging into: Cosmos is blowing up the ticket counts, making it hard to know what to work, when, and how to properly serve customers.
Multiple critical customers have complained that the alert emails are overwhelming their email servers, and one (Renodis) threatened to file complaints against BPRF's domain. Atrium is the other customer.
Scott considers Alison's assertion that this is "by design" unacceptable: any system designed to blow up customers' email servers is not good design.
