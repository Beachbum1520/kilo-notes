# Outcomes of the Spectrum domain migration call

The migration covers everything people log into with an @blueprintrf.com address: Salesforce, Blueprint One, Zendesk, OAB, SharePoint, DL workflows and so on.
The end result will be @blueprintrf.com as everyone's primary address, with @spectrum and @charter as aliases.
No migration timing has been set yet, but the team wants to move quickly.
There will be some downtime for customer-facing email workflow distros, but email will be queued, not rejected.
The Rapidscale Salesforce instance is moving to its own independent instance outside Spectrum, handled by the Rapidscale team. Scott asked Rachael McIntosh to confirm whether BPRF's Salesforce is still part of the Rapidscale instance.
Scott will work with his teams on contingency and communication plans before the migration happens.
The migration team's starter list: user identities, SSO app registrations (PowerBI, Salesforce and others), security groups, Teams groups/files, SharePoint sites, shared mailboxes, DLs, Lucid spaces.

**Approx date:** September 2026

**Source:** Maintaining SharePoint sites and email distribution lists, 2026-09-02
