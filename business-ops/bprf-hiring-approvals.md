# Hiring approvals

The BPO endorses candidates to Kyle and Marie. For agent-level hires, Kyle and Marie approve or reject. For team lead and above, they send the final one or two candidates to Scott for final approval.
Kyle and Marie take part in all hires; Scott takes part in all hires at team lead and above.
