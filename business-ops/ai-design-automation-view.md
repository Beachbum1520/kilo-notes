# AI for network design, and the shift to the customer portal

Scott started exploring AI for network design a while back, then switched to using AI for the customer portal because he was worried about sharing confidential data with an AI model.
He thinks much of BPRF's design work could be done through an AI integration tool: they know the design standards and hardware capabilities, have thousands of past designs to train a model on, and have the Ekahau AI hook. He expects a large share of manual design work to go away.
