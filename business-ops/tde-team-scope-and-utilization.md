# TDE team scope and utilization

Scott's team has 14 Technical Deployment Engineers. They handle Design, BOM and Final Sales Review (FSR), act as the remote technical resource for installs, and take support escalations and technical special projects such as network audits.
At 14 heads the team runs at about 97% utilization, leaving less than half an FTE of headroom.
Current OLAs are based on supporting the existing BPRF sellers and the current install pipeline.
BPRF says "OLA" (operational level agreement) and "SLA" when discussing delivery commitments to customers and brands.
