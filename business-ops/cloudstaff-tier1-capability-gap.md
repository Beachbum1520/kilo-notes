# Cloudstaff's gap and agent lead time

Cloudstaff has nobody who has run a Tier 1 voice operation at that scale. They are more of a staffing BPO, and this venture would move them into true managed services.
You can't find a customer first and then build the team. Scott estimates a minimum lead time of about eight weeks from hiring a new agent to having them trained and taking calls, which is how long it takes now with CallTek.
Since Cloudstaff has never done this, Scott doesn't know whether they could do it that quickly.
Scott pointed to Cloudstaff's investor Ares and its directive to grow North America as evidence that Cloudstaff will fund ahead of revenue.

**Approx date:** September 2026

**Source:** Negotiating return-to-office requirements, 2026-09-24
