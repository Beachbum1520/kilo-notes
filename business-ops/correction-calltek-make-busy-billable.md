# Make-busy time is billable under the CallTek agreement

**What was wrong:** Claude said the Section 4.3 CPPH definition limited billable hours to talk, hold, available and wrap time. On that basis Claude said about $6,101 of make-busy time (Reviewing Tickets, Long Case Notes, Special Tasks) was not billable, and drafted a rejection email.

**Correct version:** Scott pushed back ("i thought they could bill for make busy time"). SOW Section 3.1.1 says "including but not limited to", and the MSA definition of a Productive Hour is "total time Agents provide BPRF services". Service-related make-busy time is billable, so the June hourly invoice is substantially correct.
