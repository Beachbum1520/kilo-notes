# Grand Hyatt Vail fiber issue

The root cause was not the wrong SFP. The fiber infrastructure was out of limits for a 10G connection under Hyatt standards.
The hotel pays BPRF for monthly support, not for oversight of its equipment. Infrastructure is not in BPRF's scope of support, though BPRF often helps with it as a good partner.
BPRF is replacing the fiber free of charge (a few thousand dollars) as a good partner, not because it is in scope.
BPRF sells the hardware and installs and supports it, but the hardware belongs to the hotel, and BPRF doesn't pay for it when it goes down.
Hard cost was $2,197.07. The invoice to the customer was $2,644.93.
A heat map was done at the end of the install, and the install was done as designed. Signal degrades over time, which is normal. Scott understands that the hotel's new heat maps found dead spots in areas outside the original scope.
The hotel contact is Vey Calkins (Area IT Director). Hyatt corporate is cc'd on the thread.
