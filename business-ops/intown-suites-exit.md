# Exit from the Intown Suites customer

BPRF used to be Intown Suites' exclusive provider. BPRF and Intown mutually decided to part ways.
Intown just wasn't profitable business for BPRF, and it used a disproportionate share of calls, which is why they decided to part ways.
The transition away from Intown started in March 2026 and was just wrapping up in August 2026.
The decline in CallTek monthly spend comes from losing Intown, and it won't change until Spectrum sellers start feeding the funnel.

**Approx date:** August 2026
