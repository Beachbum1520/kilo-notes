# BPRF service scope

BPRF is a managed Wi-Fi provider, and more broadly an end-to-end network management provider: if it runs on the network, BPRF supports it.
BPRF is the only party with admin access to the gear.
BPRF sells the gear to the hotel, installs it, and manages and supports it.
BPRF serves two customers: the hotels and hotel management companies (under standards mandated by the national brands), and its customers' customer, the hotel guest.
On Scott's side of the house, managed Wi-Fi is all they do.

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** Charter/Cox merger customer support integration, 2026-04-30
