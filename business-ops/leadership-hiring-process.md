# Scott's hiring process for leadership roles on his account

The BPO posts the job and recruits qualified candidates.
Candidates are routed to Scott's BPRF leaders, Marie and Kyle, for interviews. BPO-specific leaders such as Helen are sometimes included, depending on the role.
Once Marie and Kyle agree on a candidate, that person is endorsed to Scott. Kyle and Marie then book a time on Scott's calendar for the final interview, and Scott approves extending an offer.
Scott personally interviews every candidate endorsed for a leadership role. It is usually the single candidate his leaders endorse, and occasionally two when his leaders disagree or feel strongly about more than one; in those cases Scott casts the deciding vote.
The process has not changed in at least two years.
In the Josh dispute, Scott refused to deviate from the process: no candidate goes directly to him, and there are no "second choices" from CTC.
"Client interview" in BPO updates means Scott's interview.
