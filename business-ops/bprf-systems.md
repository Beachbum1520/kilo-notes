# BPRF systems

Salesforce is the CRM and the source of truth for inventory and asset records.
Zendesk is the ticketing system for the NOC/PNOC.
Tier 1 and 2 use the BPO's proprietary ticketing system, CAS.
Property records (install workbooks, heatmaps, network diagrams, etc.) are kept in BPRF's in-house Online As-Builts (OAB).
COSMOS is the monitoring and alerting platform.
