# Hospitality group logo rollout

In late July or early August 2026, new logos came out for the Cox hospitality group: Hospitality Network, Blueprint RF and Cox Private Networks.
Hospitality Network and Cox Private Networks got Spectrum-branded logos. BPRF's logo was left unchanged, the only one of the three without "Spectrum".

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Charter's international travel policy research, 2026-08-08
