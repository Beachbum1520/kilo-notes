# Goals for messaging Charter about the managed WiFi business

Charter's audience is senior-level. Jady told Scott to treat all of this like a job interview, and Scott's goal is to keep his job.
Managed WiFi is new territory for Charter, but a space they are very interested in.
Scott wants to communicate that, properly funded and supported, this can be a very profitable space, but one with unique requirements Charter probably hasn't seen before.
He wants Charter interested, without making BPRF's work look so easy that Charter can absorb it into its large machine, or so complex that "the juice ain't worth the squeeze."
