# Cloudstaff employee cost tracker

Scott keeps "CS Employee Costs.xlsx", a summary of his Cloudstaff team with salaries, EDC and cost to him. He uses it to reconcile the monthly Cloudstaff invoice.
Cloudstaff recently had a slight EDC cost increase, so the invoice no longer reconciled to the tracker. CS sent a new EDC calculator.
Scott records each employee's invoice total in column AD of the tracker and does the math to see if it reconciles.
His Cloudstaff invoice and EDC calculator are in USD.

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** Automating EDC cost calculations in employee tracker, 2026-04-15
