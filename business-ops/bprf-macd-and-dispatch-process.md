# MACDs and tech dispatch process

MACDs follow the same process as new sales, on an accelerated timeline. Break/fix (tech dispatch) follows it too, and all of it sits with Scott's team.
MACDs often come in through calls or emails to the NOC.
BPRF doesn't pre-stage hardware.
BPRF doesn't stock spare parts unless they were part of the original sale.
Tech dispatch can be triggered by the NOC or PNOC, but the PNOC handles it. Dispatches need hotel approval because they're billable, and dispatch is high-margin work.
Not all truck rolls are billable: exceptions include warranty and contractual carve-outs where BPRF can't bill (e.g. Hilton). Scott didn't want to use the phrase "BPRF-caused issues."
Break/fix on-site work is 100% contractor (WorkMarket and Field Nation smart hands), supported by BPO-staffed NOC agents on Scott's team.
