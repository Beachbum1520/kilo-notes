**Decided:** Scott offered Gustaaf a no-charge IRE proof of concept at the New Orleans hotel, run for 6 months or however long needed, with no-charge uninstall if Omni decides BPRF isn't the best choice. **Why:** To show what a true single-source provider can deliver and win the support business back from Sonifi.

**Rejected alternatives:** none stated.

**Would revisit if:** unknown.
