# Cap install starts at 7 per week instead of splitting the team

**Decided:** Limit the install team to 7 install starts per week for now rather than dividing the TDE team in half. Let the install team flex more as new TDEs are onboarded and opportunities convert to installs.

**Why:** Splitting the teams is a dumb idea and creates idle time. Capping starts is basically the same thing but still lets Scott cross-utilize TDEs as business demand dictates.

**Rejected alternatives:** Jady's suggestion to split the team into fixed install and design sides.

**Would revisit if:** The new TDE hires are approved and onboarded, and opportunities convert to installs.

**Approx date:** September 2026

**Source:** charter ramp up for new sellers, 2026-07-30
