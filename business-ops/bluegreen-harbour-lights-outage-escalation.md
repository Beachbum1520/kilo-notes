Frank Vargas (Sr. Director, Field, Network and Telecom Services, Hilton Grand Vacations/HGV) escalated to Scott and Joe Peeples on Sept 11, 2026 about an extended network outage at Bluegreen Harbour Lights (BGHLR).
Cc'd: Jim Wright, Ken Renouf, Tiffany Everard (HGV), Kiana Vereen (Bluegreen).

HGV said this was not an isolated incident and was impacting confidence in the partnership.

HGV's claimed costs: $2,064.88 hotspots and equipment; $1,590.00 hotspot service charges; $1,290.00 upcoming service costs scheduled Sept 14; $1,456.71 guest compensation; $681.32 Blueprint RF troubleshooting charges. Total claimed: $7,082.91.
HGV requested: confirmation of tech onsite date, detailed remediation plan with ownership and timelines, executive oversight, and discussion of reimbursement including the Aug 3 charge.
