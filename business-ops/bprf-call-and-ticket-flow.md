# BPRF call and ticket flow

Guests call Tier 1 and Tier 2. Issues they can't resolve escalate to the NOC, which rarely happens.
Hotel staff and management companies call the NOC directly.
The NOC and PNOC are two different teams. The NOC handles calls. The PNOC handles contractually required proactive monitoring, responding to COSMOS alerts, and tech dispatch (truck rolls).
COSMOS does the monitoring and alerting and auto-generates tickets. Zendesk is the ticketing system only; it doesn't monitor.
The PNOC acts on alerts across the 200K+ endpoints BPRF manages.
Every customer communication must be ticketed, and the team has to be trained to notate tickets correctly to avoid brand-imposed SLA credits.

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** Charter/Cox merger customer support integration, 2026-04-30
