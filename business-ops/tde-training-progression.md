# TDE training and ramp model

BPRF has an established multi-month TDE training progression. TDEs work through CO, EOL, takeover, full upgrades and full designs such as a new build.
TDEs also step into installs, following the same complexity levels. The FSR (Final Sales Review) process works the same way.
TDEs are not "trained then ready." Ramp depends on the skill they arrive with: an experienced NOC agent ramps differently from someone with technical chops but no industry experience.
As TDEs advance, they take on more and more complex work.

**Approx date:** July 2026

**Source:** charter ramp up for new sellers, 2026-07-30
