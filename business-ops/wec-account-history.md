# World Equestrian Center (WEC) account history

The WEC deals were originally sold and entered in CRM as "World Equestrian Center."
The Riding Academy was later set up under WEC even though the contract carried a DBA of The Riding Academy (TRA) Hotel; when TRA staff called, the NOC had no record of TRA and couldn't match them to an account.
When Scott and Perry dug into it with Mason a couple of years ago, they found four calling locations: The Equestrian Hotel, The Riding Academy, Expo 1, Expo 2 — all designed, installed, and recorded in CRM as WEC.
In June 2024 BPRF did an upgraded design and proposal to deliver the redundancy Mason wanted and separate the networks.
Outcome: Mason took the network over himself, and BPRF moved the APs to their controller for them to support directly.
