# Cox MyID outage blocking Philippine contractor access

Scott runs an offshore team in the Philippines of about 100 call center agents and technical support engineers, all contractors hired through multiple BPOs.
Before the merger, Scott and his team used Cox MyID to manage their access to BPRF email, tools, and systems.
MyID went down a few weeks before the merger closed. The banner said it would be restored by 8/21, but it wasn't.
Accounts that expire are immediately deleted (email, SharePoint files, system access) with no way to reactivate. Scott's workaround had been to create a new account, but the option to add non-employee users is also gone.
IT helpdesk (incident INC13726266) directed him to the Charter HR team. Jady gave him Angie as the new Charter HR point of contact.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Restoring Philippine contractor network access, 2026-08-27
