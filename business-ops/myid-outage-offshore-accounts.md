# MyID outage affecting offshore contractor accounts

Scott manages roughly 100 offshore call center agents and technical support engineers in the Philippines, all contractors hired through multiple BPOs.
Before the merger, Scott's team used Cox MyID to manage their access to Blueprint RF email, tools, and systems.
MyID went down a few weeks before the merger closed; the banner promised full functionality by 8/21, which did not happen.
Several accounts expired because renewals could not be done; on expiry, email, SharePoint files, and system access are immediately and irreversibly deleted.
The option to add non-employee users was gone, and the renewal date field was zeroed and greyed out (ServiceNow incident INC13726266, Severity 3).
IT helpdesk said contractor extensions/terminations must go through the Charter contractor office / Charter HR.
Escalation path: IT helpdesk → Charter HR (Angie Pehrson, named by Jady as the new Charter HR contact) → Joe Peeples → Lisa Pinkey-Coleman → Mike Horton / Jeff Trotter → Denise Lopez-Herrera and her leader Rob Fox.
Jeff Trotter stated the organization will no longer work out of MyID and must transition to IDM.
Mike Horton suggested Scott connect with Tom Lake, who may be working the same scenarios onboarding offshore agents.
