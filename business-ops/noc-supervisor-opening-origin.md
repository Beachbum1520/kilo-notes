# How the NOC Supervisor opening arose

CallTek moved Alison off Scott's account without his consent, calling it a "promotion"; Scott says this is a lot of what started the dispute.
Scott had invested in paid training for Alison and believed that meant she was committed to his account for 12 months.
Josh acknowledged the training investment but moved her anyway, and offered to reimburse the training cost.
It happened in the middle of the merger, and Jady didn't want to create bad optics, so he didn't let Scott push back harder.
Helen was then promoted to Alison's position, creating the NOC Supervisor opening.
Scott gave CallTek more than 90 days to fill the supervisor role. CTC surfaced only two internal candidates, and neither was approved by Scott and his leaders.
Scott then posted the role at Cloudstaff, and within a week had multiple qualified candidates, two of them CallTek employees on other accounts.
Scott says he has proof of the training investment.
