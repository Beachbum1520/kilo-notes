# Cloudstaff-hosted mid-year BPRF party (July 11, 2026)

There is a lot of discussion and concern about the future of the Philippines-based team, most of whom are split between CallTek and Cloudstaff in Cebu.
To build morale, Cloudstaff is hosting a mid-year party for the whole Blueprint RF account on July 11, themed "The Devil Wears Prada." It is on the scale of the year-end party, with costumes, performances, prizes, raffles, and performance awards (custom plaques and small gift cards).
Cloudstaff is funding the whole event, and everyone on the BPRF account is invited regardless of BPO affiliation.
The December 2025 year-end party theme was "The Great Gatsby." Scott attended that one in person.
Scott asked Jady to record a greeting video for the party.
