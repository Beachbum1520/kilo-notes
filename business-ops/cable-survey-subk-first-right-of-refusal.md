# Cable survey process: the surveying SubK quotes, with first right of refusal

**Decided:** After an AAR on the $30K change order at the Carlsbad by the Sea Hotel, the SubK who does a cabling survey will also quote the cable work. If BPRF wins the deal, that SubK gets first right of refusal on the work. Scott also proposed that the SE and the surveying SubK work directly together during the survey, possibly with a joint review call before the SubK leaves the property. He is open to suggestions on that part.

**Why:** From running install teams and managing SubKs, Scott doesn't want BPRF tied to a SubK's schedule, and first right of refusal avoids that. At larger full-service hotels, survey results take 2–3 weeks to reach his teams, which forces too many assumptions and slows design when SEs have to go back to the SubK weeks later (seen on a Blue-Green survey in design).

**Rejected alternatives:** None stated.

**Would revisit if:** Cross-functional input suggests better options on the survey-lag piece.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** Improving cable survey and installation processes, 2026-05-04
