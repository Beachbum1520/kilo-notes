# BPRF's support covers equipment the hotel owns

**What was wrong:** The draft said the support agreement covers "support of the network equipment we provide and manage".

**Correct version:** BPRF sells the hardware, installs it, and supports it, but it remains the hotel's property. BPRF doesn't pay to replace it when it goes down.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Fiber infrastructure support scope and pricing, 2026-08-25
