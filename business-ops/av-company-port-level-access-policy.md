# Policy: third parties get port-level access only

When a hotel wants a third party (for example an AV company) to manage conference Wi-Fi, BPRF can accommodate it and has other properties doing exactly this. BPRF gives port-level access but not access to the switches or APs. Because BPRF is accountable to Hyatt for SLAs, reporting and so on, it has to keep control of the network gear. The third party can use the conference tool the hotel already has for conference management.

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** Hyatt Vail - EGEGH - Escalation, 2026-04-23
