# Charter LTIP will be paid in restricted stock

A Charter "notice to shareholders" filing of about 400 pages, published roughly a year earlier, said the LTIP going forward would be paid in restricted common stock. Scott first recalled it as "restricted stock options".
Scott later confirmed the instrument is RSUs.
