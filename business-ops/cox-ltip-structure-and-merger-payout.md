# Cox LTIP structure and merger payout

The Cox LTIP ("the golden handcuffs from Cox") is awarded every year and pays out three years later.
Cox said that "on or about closing" it will pay at least 100% of the 2024 and 2025 LTIP awards and 33% of the 2026 award. After that, employees move to the Charter LTIP program.
Scott's estimate of about $190K is calculated at 100%; anything above that is a bonus.
The 2023 award, paid out earlier in 2026, paid at 171%.
The 2026 award was not due to pay until 2029. Scott considers 33% for about 8 months generous, and notes they could have rolled the balances into Charter and kept the handcuffs on.
It is a cash payment, not a retention payment.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Charter Cox merger California vote update, 2026-08-13
