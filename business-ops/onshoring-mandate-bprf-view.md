# Scott's view on whether the onshoring mandate reaches BPRF

Scott reads the mandate from the FCC via Charter as covering Cox's offshore resources. He knows of about 2,200 Cox agents in the Philippines, and possibly more in other countries.
Scott is a Cox employee but works for Blueprint RF, a separate, wholly owned subsidiary. He does not think the mandate will be applied to BPRF.
He thinks they won't get rid of his team and are more likely to build it.
