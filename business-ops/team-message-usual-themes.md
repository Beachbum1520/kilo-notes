# Scott's usual themes when addressing the team

Scott's usual message to the team thanks them for their dedication, service, and loyalty.
He stresses that they are the face of the business to BPRF's customers and to its customers' customers, and how much he appreciates how well they show up and represent the company.
He likes to say that customers can buy Ruckus and Aruba switches anywhere, and the gear BPRF sells is identical to its competitors'. Customers buy from BPRF because of the service and support, and this team provides it.
He praises the team for getting the job done day after day while always striving to do better.
