# Train before promoting into leadership

**Decided:** Coaching and training must happen before a promotion, not after. Leadership positions should be posted and opened to anyone, then filled from whoever applies. Scott gives final approval on all leadership positions. He listens to and seriously considers his leadership team's advice, but will not endorse anyone he doesn't believe is qualified and prepared when the offer is made.

**Why:** When they tried promoting first and training afterward, people who could not grow into the role had to be removed. That cost them people who had been great in their previous positions and who ended up off the account. CTC also has a history of pushing people into positions they later told Scott they didn't want.

**Rejected alternatives:** Promote-then-train (Josh's approach), and handpicking or forcing candidates into roles.

**Would revisit if:** unknown
