# NOC Supervisor: RJ vs. Wendy

Scott doesn't want to hire RJ at CTC given the Q1 exit plans. If Josh is right that she only wants to be on Scott's account as a CTC employee, there is a real risk she won't move to Cloudstaff at the exit.
Scott's fear: if he loses too many critical leaders in the transition, agents will start jumping ship and the migration becomes an operational nightmare.
Scott says the risk and exposure on the Q1 move are on him.
Wendy is the #2 candidate at Cloudstaff. She currently works at CTC on a different account. Marie thought she'd be a good fit; Kyle thought she wasn't technical enough and wasn't sure she could grow into the role.
Scott admitted part of him wanted to hire Wendy "just to poke Josh in the eye," that hiring someone from a competitor account is a plus, and that he felt "a little butt hurt" when RJ turned down the 50K bonus and legal protection.
Setting those aside, Scott said he was split. He noted RJ's pattern: she left his account within the last year, applied at Cloudstaff, accepted, rescinded, and is now applying through CTC. He said she "is appearing a bit flaky."
Scott said he was leaning toward Wendy but feared it was for the wrong reasons.
