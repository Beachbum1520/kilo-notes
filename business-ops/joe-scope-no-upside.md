# Why Scott doesn't want Joe's departments

The added scope comes with no new title. Scott is a Sr Director and would stay one.
Post-close, Jady (an AVP under Cox) will be demoted to Sr Director with the promise of a VP role later.
Scott believes that if he accepts without discussing comp, the comp will never come.
Taking it would mean handing the parts of his job he likes most to his managers and others: the Filipino team, the twice-yearly trips there, and the day-to-day interactions at that level.
He'd be giving up the work he likes best for work he won't like, with no more money and no more title. He sees no upside.
