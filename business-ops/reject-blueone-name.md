# Reject the BlueONE dashboard name

**Decided:** Scott doesn't like the BlueONE name.

**Why:** Agreed with the reasons: "One" is overused in enterprise naming, says nothing about what the product does, could describe any consolidation product, and carries legal risk.

**Rejected alternatives:** BlueONE; Scott also set aside the circulated list (PulsePoint, SignalView, GuestPulse, SiteSphere, BluePrism, BlueEdge) to surface new ideas.

**Would revisit if:** unknown

**Approx date:** May 2026
