# Hilton documentation submission process

Hilton requires proof of term letters, collection attempts and invoices.
Scott gets the list of sites and the required documents from Ayesha and Craig.
Submissions must be made at least 10 business days in advance, so Hilton can inform its owner relations team, which usually also contacts the properties.
Scott sends the finalized list to Hilton (Mark Jenkins and Mary Ann) with a direct link to the documents, which he puts in BPRF's vendor library with Hilton because of email file size limits.
