# CallTek invoice approval and upload process

CallTek emails its invoices to Scott for approval.
Once Scott approves, he gives CallTek the OK to upload the invoices to the Cox Oracle payment portal.
Scott has nothing to do with early-payment discounts. Cox AP handles them, and Scott believes AP has the discount built into its system and will take it.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Invoice verification against staffing records, 2026-08-03
