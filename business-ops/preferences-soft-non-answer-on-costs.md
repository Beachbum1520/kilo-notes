# Handle reimbursement asks with a soft non-answer

When a customer asks for cost reimbursement, give a non-answer. Position the outage cause (for example, a fiber break) as outside BPRF's scope, and say BPRF is doing everything it can to restore service quickly. Avoid harsh contract citations that will "trigger everyone on the thread." Cut any phrase that "rubs salt into the wound," and don't write anything that opens the door to BPRF absorbing costs.
