# BPRF is not an approved Marriott GRE provider

BPRF is not one of the current approved Marriott GRE system integrators/providers.
There are only 4 approved GRE providers.
BPRF is the managed WiFi provider at over 1,000 Marriott properties.
BPRF has been trying to get into GRE for close to a decade.
Marriott knows full well BPRF is not a current GRE provider.
Scott's guess is that Marriott intends the existing providers to support GRE 1.0 and 2.0 hotels until they upgrade to GRE&T.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** RFP clarification questions, 2026-05-05
