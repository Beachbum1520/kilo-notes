# Return-to-office mandate: four days a week

Scott has been told he must return to the office at least four days a week.
The official notification from Spectrum leadership said only that they would move toward four days in office and that more would come from business leaders. No date was given.
Scott's boss set an October 1 date himself. When Scott said he'd go find another job, the boss pushed it to January as a verbal concession.
Scott says his boss invented the October date to look good to the people above him.
