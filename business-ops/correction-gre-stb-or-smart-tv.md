# GRE&T supports set top box or smart TV

**What was wrong:** Clarification Q4 described GRE&T as smart-TV-only (no set top boxes).

**Correct version:** The RFP says the cloud-rendered UX is compatible with either a set top box connected to a standard TV or a smart TV.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** RFP clarification questions, 2026-05-05
