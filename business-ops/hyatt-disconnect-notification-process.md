# Courtesy 3-week disconnect notification process for Hyatt

**Decided:** As a courtesy (not a contractual requirement), on Mondays the Hyatt brand manager sends Hyatt a list of all properties on the disconnect list 3 weeks before the disconnect date; BPRF continues collections and sends an updated list every week until disconnect (disconnects happen Tuesdays). Each time new hotels hit the disconnect list, a new 3-week notice report is generated, shared, and updated the same way. Framed "in the spirit of our continued partnership."

**Why:** Scott had already planned to implement it; responds to Hyatt's complaint without conceding a contractual obligation.

**Rejected alternatives:** Telling them to "go pound sand" (his instinct); making it a contractual requirement.

**Would revisit if:** unknown

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** Hyatt disconnect notification and collection process response, 2026-05-07
