# BPRF will produce proposals for Spectrum SB sellers

**Decided:** Scott told Eric Nowak (Spectrum) that BPRF's teams can produce proposals for Spectrum SB sellers, at least initially, with turnaround depending on volume.

**Why:** Spectrum sellers won't have Salesforce access on Day 1. Over time, as sellers learn the standards and the "gotchas", Scott sees no reason they couldn't do designs themselves.

**Rejected alternatives:** None stated.

**Would revisit if:** Spectrum sellers get Salesforce access and learn the design standards, at which point they could take over the step. The FSR check stays mandatory.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Refining draft reply to Eric, 2026-08-14
