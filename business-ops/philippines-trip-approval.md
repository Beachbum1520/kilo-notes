# Annual Philippines trip approval

Scott has taken a Philippines trip at this time of year every year since 2024. He calls getting his boss's approval a "ridiculously long process."
He strongly feels he needs to be on-site to manage the critical phone system cutover to the solution BPRF controls.
As on previous trips, the flight is covered through Cloudstaff, and the only costs he will expense are lodging, meals and incidentals. He stays in an inexpensive Airbnb and cooks for himself. Because living there is cheaper than at home, he won't expense most of his meals.
He likes to book as far in advance as possible because that gets the best lodging (cheapest and closest to the office).
The trip may be longer than in past years because of the cutover.
He will also meet with Conectys, another call center BPO he is talking to as a possible replacement for the CallTek-based Tier 1 and Tier 2 guest WiFi support teams.
The flight is paid from a Cloudstaff "deposit account": credits BPRF accrues on its bill during the year (for example, credit back for employees' missed time), which Scott uses for employee outings, his plane tickets and similar costs.
