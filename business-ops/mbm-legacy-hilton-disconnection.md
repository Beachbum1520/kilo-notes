# MBM Legacy: Hilton properties dispute and disconnection

MBM Legacy Inc. owns Embassy Suites Fayetteville Fort Bragg (FAYFB) and Hilton Garden Inn Fayetteville Fort Bragg (FAYNH), plus a Marriott property (FAYFP, Four Points).
The owner had invoiced Hilton corporate. BPRF intended to disconnect the hotel's Wi-Fi service on Sept 1, 2026. Hilton corporate asked for a summary.
FAYFB has an outstanding balance of $30,564.44.
The demand letter was never sent. The owner is very aware of the issue, and there have been many conversations with BPRF's teams trying to resolve it.
At FAYNH, the executed Work Order sets monthly support at $414. A handwritten notation added after execution reduces it to $310. Scott is pretty sure the owner has been paying the correct rate.
Legal's edit: the alterations were never "agreed to" (instead of "initialed") by BPRF. The Participation Agreement is with BPRF Holdings, LLC, dated February 20, 2024.
