# Stance on Charter onshoring: wait and find out, don't stress

**Decided:** Scott's posture on a possible Charter onshoring mandate is to wait and find out and not stress either way.

**Why:** Charter has already had enough meetings with him to realize that how BPRF runs its call center for brand standards compliance and SLA credit mitigation is night and day different from how Charter runs call centers for its customers. As the executing operations director, he reads that as making him more secure if they move to an onshore mandate; Charter has no leaders on its side who know how to do this — "I am it."

**Rejected alternatives:** Claude's suggestion to treat the mandate as high-variance and accelerate the external exit pipeline in parallel was not adopted.
