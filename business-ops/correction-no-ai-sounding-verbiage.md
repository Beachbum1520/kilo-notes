# Plain language, no AI-sounding phrasing

**What was wrong:** The draft called the workbook "the engine," used "the demand side is built from," included low-value sentences, and wrapped points in fancy wording such as "I can give you a clear number, and I would rather set it by capping install starts than by fixing a split."

**Correct version:** Call it what it is: a workbook. "The engine" sounds too AI. State points simply, e.g. "Versus splitting the teams, I want to limit starts to 7 per week," followed by a brief explanation. No fancy AI-sounding verbiage.
