# Gaps in setting up BPO contractor accounts in the new identity system

The setup form has required fields that are blank on Scott's one migrated reference account. One required field is a mobile number, which most of his Philippines-based agents don't have (no company phones).
The agents represent Blueprint RF, not Cox or Spectrum, to customers, so their primary email must be @blueprintrf.com. Scott can't find where the form sets that.
Under legacy MyID, contractor accounts expired after a year and, if not renewed in time, were deleted entirely (email, SharePoint, system access). Scott needs to know whether the new system has an equivalent.
Under MyID, Scott could designate select contractors as supervisors to create and renew accounts, with approval routed to him. He manages 100+ agents.
Lower priority: under BPRF's SOC 2 / ISO 27001 obligations, Scott runs a quarterly access attestation. MyID had a workflow for this.
