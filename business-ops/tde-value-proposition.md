# Why BPRF uses TDEs

Scott's framing: BPRF Technical Deployment Engineers (Philippines-based; always spell out the name, not just "engineers") give installs a consistent experience and better adherence to brand standards. They reduce the technical skill needed from on-site field techs, which makes installs more consistent, more profitable, and easier to support for the life of the contract.
TDE candidates should be able to read and create Ekahau heatmaps.
