# BPRF VOD app vs. Sonifi in Marriott GRE&T RFP

Appendix 2 of the Marriott GRE&T RFP calls out Sonifi as the optional VOD app.
Sonifi is a current GRE provider and a competitor.
BPRF has its own VOD app with full licensing and hospitality release windows.
