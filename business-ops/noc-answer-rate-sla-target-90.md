# NOC call answer rate incentive target raised to 90%

**Decided:** Scott moved the green (target) level of the internal NOC call answer rate SLA, which drives the team's monthly incentives, from 80% to 90%. The scale tops out at 100% and keeps roughly the same number of levels. Final thresholds: 100, 97, 93, 90 (target), 87, 83, 80, 77, 73 or below. The incentive ladder is unchanged: add 100/50/20/10% at and above target, deduct 10/20/30/40/50% below.

**Why:** To raise the standard to 90%.

**Rejected alternatives:** Even 5-point steps, which dropped a reward tier. Scott said there is no reason the intervals have to be 5 points. Decimal steps (3⅓ points) were rejected because he wants whole numbers.

**Would revisit if:** Not stated.

**Approx date:** June 2026

**Source:** NOC call answer rate SLA adjustment, 2026-06-29
