# Operating philosophy: normalize to the strictest standard and the 4-hour rule

On Scott's side of the house, BPRF normalizes the whole operation to the strictest brand standard rather than managing each brand separately.
Scott's standard: he has to be able to get a tech to any BPRF property within 4 hours of dispatch, and once on site the tech is held to a 4-hour resolution for issues within BPRF's control.
Outside BPRF's control: ISP circuit problems, a switch or AP needing an RMA, and the hotel not giving access to the right guest room or wiring closet.

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** Charter/Cox merger customer support integration, 2026-04-30
