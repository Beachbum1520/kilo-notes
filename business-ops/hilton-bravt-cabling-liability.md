# Hampton Inn Brattleboro (BRAVT) — liability for hotel-side cabling issue

Hampton Inn by Hilton Brattleboro (GM Jolene Belair) reported front desk Wi-Fi/connectivity issues and demanded a technician (Zendesk request 1606620, May 20, 2026).
Corey Stevens (Hospitality Support Manager) said the issue was with front desk computers BPRF does not support or have visibility into.
Hilton's Mary Ann Hutchison (Sr Lead, Guest Facing Technologies) said the remaining suspect was cabling, which is the hotel's responsibility, and asked Scott about a MACD for a tech to troubleshoot.
Scott's position: happy to have his team propose a tech visit, but without Hilton support ticket details the tech may be ineffective and the hotel could pay without resolution; BPRF cannot be accountable for resolving issues on the hotel's side of the network it doesn't access or control.
