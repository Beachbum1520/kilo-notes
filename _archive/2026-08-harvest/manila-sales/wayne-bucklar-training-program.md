# Wayne Bucklar sales training program

- Approximately 80 hours over roughly one month, at no cost to BPRF through Lloyd, including Wayne's airfare from Manila to Cebu.
- Structure: 16 hours face-to-face (8 sessions × 2 hours, two nights in Ortigas), 56 hours online consultancy, 8 hours site visit in Cebu.
- Core frameworks: Definition of Done (a shared standard for a complete call, lead, and proposal), Give to Get (every ask paired with value delivered), and one-liners (repeatable lines that raise call quality rather than raw volume).
- Curriculum sequence: foundations, product and buyer, call openings, discovery using the Site Assessment Form, qualifying, proposal, objections and close, follow-up and team-lead development.
- The correct internal term is Site Assessment Form (SAF). Early program materials used "SAQ" in error.

**Approx date:** May 2026.
