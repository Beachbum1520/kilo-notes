# DG2 gateway — product and naming rule

* The gateway product's current generation shorthand is DG2; prior generations were DG1 and DG1.5; DG3 is in development.
* Per BPRF Legal, "Dominion," "Dominion Gateway," and "Dominion Platform" cannot appear anywhere in external or seller-facing material — only "DG2" is permitted.
* DG3 is never to be mentioned in external or forwardable material.
* Internal platform, monitoring, and back-office system names are never named externally.

**Approx date:** July 2026.
