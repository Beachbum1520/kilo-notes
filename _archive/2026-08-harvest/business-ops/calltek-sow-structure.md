# CallTek SOW and pricing structure

* SOW No. 2 covers the Technical Help Desk (Tier 1 and Tier 2) on a Cost Per Productive Hour model at $9.71/hr.
* Pricing Table 3.1A prices Team Leaders and Supervisors at no cost to BPRF; the $9.71 CPPH is inclusive of TL and Sup time.
* SOW No. 3 covers the NOC.
* A Productive Hour is defined (MSA) as total time Agents provide Blueprint RF services; the SOW uses "including but not limited to" language.
* Change Orders (SOW 3.1.4) are required before additional costs; Section 14 governs change control.

**Approx date:** August 2026.
