# Wayne Bucklar training program

* Wayne's program is roughly 80 hours over one month, provided at no cost through Cloudstaff (offered by Lloyd).
* Structure: 16 face-to-face hours (8 sessions × 2 hours, two nights in Ortigas), 56 online consultancy hours, and an 8-hour Cebu site visit.
* Core frameworks are Definition of Done, Give to Get, and One-liners.
* The program's conversion baseline is Scott's benchmark, not Wayne's.

**Approx date:** June 2026.
