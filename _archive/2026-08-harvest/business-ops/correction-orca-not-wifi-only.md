# ORCA is not Wi-Fi-only
**What was wrong:** A reviewer comment (and the working assumption) treated ORCA as a Wi-Fi-only platform.
**Correct version:** ORCA is Marriott's RFP platform for any service governed by a certified/approved vendor list — including all GPNS tiers (Basic, Full, EBIO), guestroom entertainment (GRE&T), and conference/meeting services.
**Approx date:** August 2026.
