# Blueprint RF — company profile

* Blueprint RF (BPRF) is a managed Wi-Fi and hospitality technology provider.
* BPRF is a LAN Service Provider (LSP) — "LAN," not "Licensed"; the correct credential framing is "certified and approved LAN Service Provider."
* As the LSP, BPRF is accountable for services running on the property network; other vendors point back to the LSP.
* BPRF is owned by Cox/CCI and is going through a Cox → Charter (Spectrum) merger.
* Per Salesforce, BPRF's active portfolio is 346,763 guest rooms across 2,595 properties.
* BPRF serves major hotel brands including Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, Choice, Omni, and Wyndham.

**Approx date:** July 2026.
