# Make-busy codes are billable
**What was wrong:** I built a Section 4.3 dispute around excluding make-busy codes (Reviewing Tickets, Long Case Notes, Special Tasks) from billable hours.
**Correct version:** The SOW's "including but not limited to" language and the MSA's functional definition of Productive Hour ("total time Agents provide Blueprint RF services") make those codes legitimately billable.
**Approx date:** August 2026.
