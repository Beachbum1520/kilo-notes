# Core systems and tools in use

* Salesforce is the CRM and inventory source of truth.
* Cosmos is the network monitoring / auto-ticketing platform.
* Zendesk is used for ticketing; CAS is CallTek's ticketing system.
* OAB (Online As-Builts) holds property technical records, heatmaps, and network diagrams.
* Microsoft Teams is used for team communication; Workday for HR/performance; Field Nation for subcontractor work orders.
* Dan's team launched a customer dashboard called Blueprint ONE (in time for HITEC) and is building a replacement for Cosmos.

**Approx date:** July 2026.
