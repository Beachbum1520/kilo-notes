# NOC SLA tiers don't need 5-point increments
**What was wrong:** I built the answer-rate SLA chart on fixed 5-point increments, which eliminated one of the reward tiers.
**Correct version:** The thresholds don't need fixed 5-point increments; freeing the spacing (alternating 3/4-point whole-number steps) recovered all four reward tiers while keeping whole numbers with no decimals.
