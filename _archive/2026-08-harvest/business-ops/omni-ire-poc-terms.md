# Omni IRE POC — proposed terms

* Scott proposed a no-cost IRE proof of concept at the Omni New Orleans property.
* The POC runs six months with a no-charge removal option.
* It is paired with an offer for BPRF to serve as single point of contact for Wi-Fi and IRE support across the broader Omni portfolio during the POC.

**Approx date:** June 2026.
