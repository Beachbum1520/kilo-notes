# Reject CallTek's claim that Scott's approval delay forfeited the 2/15 early-payment discount
**Decided:** Scott's position is that CallTek's 2/15 early-payment discount trigger is payment, not approval (MSA 8.3.1), and the window runs 15 business days from portal receipt — not calendar days from the email date — so CallTek's forfeiture assertion is rejected as a weak interpretation (their reading makes the discount structurally impossible to capture). (This is the analytical position established; whether Scott asserted it back to CallTek is not confirmed in the record.)
**Why:** CallTek proactively claimed Scott's approval delay forfeited the discount; CallTek's own process requires Scott's approval before AP can upload to the Cox Oracle portal, so AP cannot pay until after he approves.
**Rejected alternatives:** Accepting CallTek's claim that the discount was forfeited by the approval delay.
