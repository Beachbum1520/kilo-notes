# Hardware and network vendors

* Wi-Fi hardware includes Ruckus and Aruba; competitors sell the identical gear.
* Gateways include Nomadix (and BPRF's DG2); firewalls include Watchguard.
* Telephony/voice: Mitel and PhoneSuite (PBX), ConnectWare Hospitality IP PBX, Cox Business SIP Trunking.
* Ruckus ChannelFly is used for channel management; 2.4 GHz at 40 MHz channel width is a documented industry error.

**Approx date:** July 2026.
