# CallTek QA monitoring obligation

* Section 2.6 of the two CallTek SOWs requires a minimum of 2 QA monitors per agent per month plus monthly QA Reports.
* These obligations have been in effect since the SOWs took effect in April 2025.
* The Guest Wi-Fi team is roughly 26 agents.
* CallTek was not meeting the per-agent monitoring coverage, and NOC QA reporting was entirely absent.

**Approx date:** June 2026.
