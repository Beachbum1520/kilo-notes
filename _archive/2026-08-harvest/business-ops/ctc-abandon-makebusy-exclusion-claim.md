# Abandon the Section 4.3 make-busy-code exclusion dispute
**Decided:** Abandon the invoice dispute built on excluding make-busy codes (Reviewing Tickets, Long Case Notes, Special Tasks) from billable hours, before any external communication.
**Why:** Scott identified that the SOW's "including but not limited to" language and the MSA's functional definition of Productive Hour ("total time Agents provide Blueprint RF services") made those codes legitimately billable.
**Rejected alternatives:** Disputing the make-busy codes as non-billable.
**Would revisit if:** Scott could still pursue a formal agreed standard for the codes via Section 14 change control or the monthly staffing meeting (a separate, forward-looking path).
**Approx date:** August 2026.
