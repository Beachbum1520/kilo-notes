## Written record discipline

- [stated] Keep politically sensitive reads, person-specific performance critiques, strategic rationale for commercial decisions, and exit intent entirely verbal — never in writing
- [stated] Factual, defensible, forwardable: every written communication is treated as if it could be escalated or used in formal proceedings
- [stated] Never make written commitments to future economics or scope before leadership approval
- [stated] When holding a position, create a paper trail rather than arguing — let the record speak
- [stated] Deliberately withholds information from written records that could be used against him (e.g., the Cloudstaff hire of Mary Rose kept out of CTC correspondence; exit intent kept out of vendor emails)

## Vendor management

- [stated] Moves between BPOs are performance-driven, not vendor-preference-driven — document this consistently
- [stated] Cloudstaff has exclusive rights to hire/fire/discipline under their MSA (Section 6a)

## Communication and drafting style

- [stated] Direct and spare: no meta-framing, no AI-tell phrasing, no announcing what he's about to say before saying it
- [stated] Informal and fast internally; formal, polished, and audience-calibrated for external/forwardable output
- [stated] Significant typos in his own drafts — parse through without flagging; output should be clean
- [stated] Prefers single polished drafts for iteration over multiple variants
- [stated] Corrects errors phrase-by-phrase; does not relitigate settled decisions
- [stated] Lead with the bottom line; bury caveats in the working conversation, not the deliverable
- [stated] Numbers and deltas in emails rather than prose-only analysis
- [stated] Numbered items and named individuals when creating accountability on the record
- [stated] Asks Claude to flag not just what to say but when and why to hold back information
- [stated] Flags when numbers don't feel right based on his own knowledge before anything goes external
- [stated] Prefers immediate usable output over explanation; corrects course quickly
