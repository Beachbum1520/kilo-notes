- [stated] On every Philippines trip, carries small coins and bills to hand out to street kids he passes on morning runs
- [stated] Last trip, cooked a large batch of bacon spaghetti and portioned it into single-serve plastic bowls to hand out to the kids
- [stated] Aware he doesn't know whether the kids are in genuine need or part of an organized begging operation; gives anyway because he considers it the right thing, not for a payoff
- [stated] Moved by the 2014 Thai Life "Unsung Hero" ad — says that's the type of man he wants to be
- [stated] Never runs the same route twice on trips
- [stated] Kids are often selling small fabric squares or towels — pays them and lets them keep the goods
- [stated] Sometimes buys them sodas, water, and chips from 7-Eleven
- [stated] Does this wherever he travels in the Philippines — Manila, Ortigas, Cebu; also gives to young mothers with babies
- [stated] Participates in the Angel Tree program at Christmas; believes those kids deserve the same kind of Christmas as any other kid — toys and games, not just clothes and hygiene items
- [stated] Irritated by donors who buy what they think kids "need" and expect gratitude for it
- [stated] On Angel Tree tags, buys both the listed needs and the wants, typically going well over the ask; also puts together a Christmas meal box for the family
