# Claude project memory: Watts Way Farms

**Purpose & context**

Scott and his wife Angie run Watts Way Farms, LLC — a 90-acre pasture-raised meat operation in Franklin, GA (wattswayfarms.com, 706-883-5644). Angie farms full-time; Scott manages sales, strategy, marketing, and finances alongside outside W-2 employment. All profits are reinvested. The farm raises approximately 60 Irish Dexter cattle and heritage breed pigs, targeting ~50 piglets/year. No antibiotics or hormones; pasture-raised throughout.

**Core business model:** Direct-to-consumer beef and pork. Pricing is all-in (processing included): Pork $5.50/lb HW; Beef $6.75/lb HW (~65% take-home yield). Dexter HW typically 475–550 lb; whole Dexter ~$3,200–$3,700 all-in (~310–360 lb take-home). Products: whole/half hogs, whole/half Dexter beef, curated boxes, and build-your-own boxes. Delivery meet-ups along the Atlanta–Montgomery corridor; UPS Ground to 20 states. Cut sheets released only after deposit. Facebook targeting: Alpharetta, Johns Creek, Roswell, Cumming, Marietta, Milton.

**Strategic direction:** Transitioning from wholes/halves → curated boxes → retail cuts. Moving toward AI-based pig breeding.

**Key people:** Angie (daily animal care, AI breeding, hands-on husbandry); NRCS contact Andrew Cobb (Carrollton field office), EQIP contract; Daniel-Jackson Meat Processing (beef, ~34 mi away, Ranburne, AL); Resaca Meat Processing (pork, ~125 mi away, USDA); accountant (Philippines-based, handles QuickBooks and payroll); employee Sarah Barnard (part-time farm hand, W-2, no W-4 on file as of 9/19/26 — payroll calculations use Single/0 default pending accountant finalization).

**Key learnings & principles**

**Epistemic standard ("Fair Witness" principle — Heinlein):** State only what is directly observed. Label inferences as inferences. Never present conclusions as facts without documentation. Scott applies this rigorously and will catch and correct violations. This is the governing rule for all Claude outputs on this farm.

**Critical pedigree correction:** "Roll the Dice 19-4" was fabricated by Claude as a sire for Ginger and Nutmeg with no documentation — this error propagated across multiple sessions. The confirmed on-paper sire for the Garrett-line animal photographed is SDH3 Too Deep 262-1. **Never restate Roll the Dice 19-4 (or any Roll the Dice variant) as the sire of Ginger or Nutmeg without an actual registration paper in hand.** Ginger and Nutmeg's confirmed details: full sisters, b. 7/6/25, registered NSR Durocs, W.H. Garrett Durocs bloodline. Sire per registration paper: SDH3 Too Deep 262-1.

**Dexter hanging weight:** Correct range is 475–550 lb. The old 250–350 lb figure was wrong — any customer quote issued before May 2026 may be off by ~$1,000.

**Pricing anchor:** Always use all-in pricing ($6.75/lb HW includes processing). Never add processing costs on top.

**Date calculations:** Do not override Scott's provided dates with mechanical calculations without flagging the discrepancy first.

**Search before responding:** Always search conversation history before stating that information is unavailable. Scott will correct this pattern firmly.

**Dexter yield:** Breed-documented retail yield ~55.3% HW-to-take-home (NADC). Farm's 65% baseline was used previously but the NADC figure is more defensible for Dexter-specific disputes.

**Employee conduct:** Scott has no interest in employees' conduct outside of work hours; external parties submitting unsolicited opinions about staff are not owed a substantive response.

**Beef finishing ration — always lead with per-bag-of-corn ratio first:** Per 1 bag cracked corn (50 lb): 1.5 scoops cottonseed hulls, 1 scoop beet pulp, 0.5 scoop soybean meal, 0.5 scoop BOSS. Per-barrel/per-batch scaling only if explicitly requested.

**Perilla mint (beefsteak plant):** Serious toxicity risk on the farm, especially August–October. Do not allow cattle access until plants are visibly dead and brown after 2,4-D application.

**Hobby loss / tax:** Farm losses offset W-2 income via material participation (Angie full-time). Standard 3-of-5 profit test applies (not horse-breeding 2-of-7). Georgia does not conform to federal bonus depreciation but conforms to Section 179.
