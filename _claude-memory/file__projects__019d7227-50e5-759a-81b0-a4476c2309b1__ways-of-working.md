## Epistemic standard (core operating principle)
- [stated] Scott applies a strict Fair Witness standard (from Heinlein's *Stranger in a Strange Land*): state only what is directly observed; label inferences explicitly as inferences; never convert observations into conclusions without flagging the gap
- [stated] Claude must hold this standard at all times — observations as observations, inferences flagged, uncertainty stated rather than filled with plausible content
- [stated] Scott will catch and correct lapses

## Corrections and records accuracy
- [stated] Animal records: accuracy of breeding dates, litter counts, and animal identities is a business-critical function, not a convenience; errors in established records are taken seriously
- [stated] Never override Scott's provided dates with mechanical calculations without flagging the discrepancy first and asking for clarification
- [stated] When Scott provides a corrected figure or exact text, treat it as the authoritative version going forward
- [stated] Scott provides exact corrections and expects them adopted immediately without re-explanation
- [stated] Animal inventory default: if Scott hasn't reported an animal as sold/gone, treat it as still on the farm — don't flag unsold animals as unconfirmed

## Communication and output style
- [stated] Direct, terse, no filler
- [stated] Responses should lead with the specific answer requested
- [stated] Clean copy-paste output with no surrounding commentary
- [stated] Scott communicates informally and expects established context to be understood

## Drafting patterns
- [stated] Search before drafting: for standard business messages (piglet pricing, customer replies), search existing approved messaging before generating new variations
- [stated] Reconciliation tasks: finish the numerical task first; don't front-load tax or accounting commentary during an active reconciliation
- [stated] Customer messaging: draft in first person as the farmer; warm, conversational, plain-spoken tone; no invented product details
- [stated] Facebook posts: positive and buyer-focused only
- [stated] Upwork/contractor communications: be aware of platform off-platform payment TOS considerations when drafting contractor messages
- [stated] Feed recipe format: always lead with per-bag-of-corn ratio first — per 1 bag cracked corn (50 lb) → 1.5 scoops cottonseed hulls, 1 scoop beet pulp, 0.5 scoop soybean meal, 0.5 scoop BOSS; per-barrel or per-batch scaling only if explicitly asked
