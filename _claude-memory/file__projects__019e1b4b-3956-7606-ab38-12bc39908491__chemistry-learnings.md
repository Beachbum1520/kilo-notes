## Iron management (pool)

- [stated] Well water iron causes oxidation events; periodic large fills are far worse than continuous trickle autofill
- [stated] The deck-mount autofill float valve (installed late July 2026) largely solved recurring iron oxidation events
- [stated] Purple Stuff dosing: approximately 1 quart per 800 gallons of well water — heavier than the label calls for
- [stated] For mild, early-stage iron tinting where the pool bottom is still clearly visible, start with smaller doses (Scott corrected Claude's tendency to overdose)
- [stated] Purple Stuff should circulate several hours before chlorine is added — not 30 minutes
- [stated] Drop SWG output during the sequestering window to limit chlorine interaction with iron
- [stated] CircuPool cell plates accumulate iron oxide, not calcium scale, given low CH; iron oxide dissolves silently in acid — no fizzing, and the visual endpoint is the solution turning yellow-amber

## Pool chemistry and SLAM

- [stated] SLAM target FC for CYA 50 is 20 ppm (prior Claude error said 40 ppm)
- [stated] pH testing is unreliable above 10 ppm FC and completely bleached above 20 ppm during SLAM
- [stated] Brushing must precede chlorine dosing to break algae biofilm
- [stated] CYA added via paint strainer bag dissolves in ~18 hours but requires 7–10 days before the test reading is reliable
- [stated] Never add CYA during a SLAM
- [stated] Calcium chloride must be broadcast directly into the deep end per label — do not pre-dissolve

## Cold plunge

- [stated] Borax dose for 50 ppm borate in 65 gallons is ½ cup (3.8 oz), not ½ tablespoon (prior Claude error)
- [stated] Purple Stuff (sequestrant) should circulate several hours before chlorine addition
- [stated] Swap the cartridge 48 hours post-refill rather than immediately, so it can catch sequestered iron first
- [stated] Scott reads FC with a 50/50 distilled water dilution when expecting above 5 ppm
- [stated] Uses a DPD comparator (Leslie's brand) — not OTO, not FAS-DPD titration
- [stated] pH correction sequence: soda ash → circulate 30 min → chlorine → circulate 15–30 min → shut off → retest pH after one hour
- [stated] The pump does not run its morning cycle on Wednesdays and Scott does not plunge that day — factor this into FC readings
- [stated] 1 tsp of 10% liquid chlorine = ~2 ppm in 65 gallons; twice-weekly dosing at that rate is not enough — FC hits zero well before the next dose
- [stated] Plunge dosing schedule chosen Sept 26, 2026: 2 tsp of 10% on Monday, Thursday and Saturday (skips Wednesday, when Scott is away), with a Sunday afternoon check to see where the floor lands
- [stated] Purple Stuff for the plunge: ~2–2.5 oz (4–5 tablespoons) for 65 gallons; dilute before adding, since the acid load crashes pH in unbuffered well water
