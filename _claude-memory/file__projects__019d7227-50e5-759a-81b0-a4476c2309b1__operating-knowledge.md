## Pricing and weight corrections
- [stated] Dexter hanging weights: correct range is 475–550 lb. The old 250–350 lb figure was wrong and corrected in May 2026 — any quote before that date may be off by ~$1,000
- [stated] Beef pricing structure: $6.75/lb HW is all-in with processing included. Do not add processing costs on top
- [stated] Pork hanging weights (actual, Apr 2026 Resaca batch, invoice #2801, replaces prior 220–240 lb estimate): 4 hogs — #1 runt (whole) 129 lb HW/109.44 lb take-home; #2 (Order 22, half) 115 lb HW/105.6 lb take-home; #2 (Order 23, half, same animal) 115 lb HW/96.4 lb take-home; #3 Peter & Amy Karr (whole) 204 lb HW/155.73 lb take-home; #4 Amanda Burnum (whole) 241 lb HW/201.85 lb take-home. Excluding the runt: hang weights ran 204–241 lb (avg 225 lb), take-home yield averaged ~79% of HW (range 76–84%)
- [stated] Pork pricing includes standard processing (cut, wrap, vacuum seal) plus curing/smoking on up to 20 lb of ham and bacon per half (40 lb per whole) — cure/smoke beyond that may carry an additional per-lb charge. Standard customer-facing pricing message (use verbatim): "$5.50/lb hanging weight, all-in — includes processing into retail-style cuts to your specifications, vacuum sealing, and deep freezing at the processor at zero degrees or below. This includes curing and smoking on up to 20 lb of ham and bacon per half (40 lb per whole), in line with what a typical hog yields; cure/smoke requests beyond that amount may carry an additional per-pound charge. Hogs typically hang 200–240 lbs: whole ~$1,100–$1,320, half ~$550–$660. Cut sheet sent after deposit. Pickup at the processor or an arranged meetup along the Atlanta–Montgomery corridor."
- [stated] Intact 6–8 month old bull calves sell for $1,500

## Sales, shipping, and customer policy
- [stated] Customers "buy the story, not just the beef" — warm, personal, farm-authentic tone
- [stated] Facebook sale posts are strictly positive and buyer-focused; no farm backstory or uncertain animal details
- [stated] No discount codes — removed permanently from farm strategy
- [stated] Whole/half hogs are NOT shipped — in-person pickup or meetup only; only retail cuts ship via website
- [stated] Meetup can be arranged for 5+ live animals

## Regulatory
- [stated] Georgia's Small Poultry Exemption requires GDA registration and facility standards — the federal Producer/Grower exemption alone is insufficient in Georgia
- [stated] Get written confirmation from GDA before building processing facilities

## EQIP mechanics
- [stated] All items pay on payment rate basis (no receipt matching)
- [stated] Cost overruns absorbed by operator; savings kept by operator
- [stated] Contracted quantities are hard ceilings
- [stated] Do not begin contractor work within practice footprints until each year's items are approved

## Pasture hazards and weed control
- [stated] 2,4-D Amine 400 used for pasture weed control
- [stated] Perilla mint (*Perilla frutescens*) is a known serious toxicity hazard on the property, particularly August–October — keep cattle out until plants are visibly dead and brown after spraying
