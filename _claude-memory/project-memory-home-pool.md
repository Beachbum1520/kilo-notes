**Key learnings & principles**

*Pool — iron management:*
- Well water iron causes oxidation events; periodic large fills are far worse than continuous trickle autofill. The deck-mount autofill float valve (installed late July 2026) largely solved recurring iron oxidation events.
- Purple Stuff dosing: approximately 1 quart per 800 gallons of well water (heavier than label). For mild, early-stage iron tinting where the pool bottom is still clearly visible, start with smaller doses — Scott corrected Claude's tendency to overdose. Scott has 7 bottles on hand as of late August 2026.
- Purple Stuff should circulate for several hours before chlorine is added (not 30 minutes).
- Drop SWG output during the sequestering window to limit chlorine interaction with iron.
- CircuPool cell plates accumulate iron oxide (not calcium scale) given low CH; iron oxide dissolves silently in acid — no fizzing, visual endpoint is solution turning yellow-amber.

*Pool — chemistry and SLAM:*
- SLAM target FC for CYA 50 is 20 ppm (not 40 ppm — prior Claude error).
- pH testing is unreliable above 10 ppm FC and completely bleached above 20 ppm during SLAM.
- Brushing must precede chlorine dosing to break algae biofilm.
- CYA added via paint strainer bag dissolves in ~18 hours but requires 7–10 days before test reading is reliable. Never add CYA during SLAM.
- Calcium chloride must be broadcast directly into the deep end per label — do not pre-dissolve.
- Well water fights pH constantly through every top-off and backwash.

*Pool — equipment:*
- The buried suction line has a cracked 90° elbow (identified May 2026); a timer-cycling workaround (periodic off-cycles to allow soil re-saturation) is the current pragmatic solution — full repair deferred to a future project justifying demolition.
- Air leak in suction line was resolved in August 2026 by replumbing with true union ball valves.
- Standard PVC true union ball valves are bidirectional; flow direction arrows are preference markings only.

*Cold plunge:*
- Borax dose for 50 ppm borate in 65 gallons is ½ cup (3.8 oz), not ½ tablespoon (prior Claude error).
- Purple Stuff (sequestrant) should circulate several hours before chlorine addition.
- Cartridge should be swapped 48 hours post-refill, not immediately, so it can catch sequestered iron first.
- Scott reads FC with a 50/50 distilled water dilution when expecting above 5 ppm; uses a DPD comparator (Leslie's brand) — not OTO, not FAS-DPD titration.
- pH correction sequence: soda ash → circulate 30 min → chlorine → circulate 15–30 min → shut off → retest pH after one hour.
- Pump does not run its morning cycle on Wednesdays; Scott does not plunge that day — factor this in when interpreting FC readings.
