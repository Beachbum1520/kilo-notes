# Hospitality support career/training path (Scott's description)

Normal career/training path: hire as Tier 1 agents taking guest WiFi connectivity calls; certify to Tier 2 (still guest calls, harder ones); graduate to TLs, NOC and PNOC agents; then supervisor, manager, etc.
The candidate pool for NOC, PNOC, TLs, etc. is the Tier 1 and Tier 2 team.
Scott says he already built this model with Cloudstaff "to the extent that they have it."
What he did, in effect, was staff a team at Cloudstaff with agents who were on his account at CTC but came up through the CTC call center program — which is why it was so disruptive to CTC.
