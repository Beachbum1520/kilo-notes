# AI agents vs. brand live-answer requirements

The big-brand hotels (Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt) are reluctant to let Blueprint RF use AI agents to answer guest or customer calls. Their SLAs still require that around 85% of all calls be answered by a live person within 30 seconds.
For those brands, Blueprint RF is looking at AI that listens to the call and prompts agents with troubleshooting and resolution steps, pulls up relevant hotel and network data, and automates ticket entry. Scott describes this as using AI to coach the agent, because the brands won't let it talk to guests directly.
For economy-brand hotels, which are more receptive, they are looking at models where an AI agent actually answers the calls.

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** VP Support Ops - Velocity, 2026-04-14
