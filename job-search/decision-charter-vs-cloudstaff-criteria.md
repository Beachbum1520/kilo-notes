# Criteria for choosing between a Charter offer and a Cloudstaff offer

**Decided:** It depends on how the two offers compare. If Cloudstaff offers just a job and a paycheck, and Charter offers a strong onshoring lead role, Scott would probably turn down Cloudstaff. If Cloudstaff offers equity or a better position with a real chance to build and lead something meaningful, he would turn down Charter "every day of the week and twice on Sunday."

**Why:** Scott says the Cloudstaff opportunity carries more risk but also more reward.

**Rejected alternatives:** Taking a salary-only Cloudstaff role over a solid Charter role.

**Would revisit if:** The actual offers differ from these scenarios.

**Approx date:** July 2026

**Source:** Pitching a BPO opportunity to Cloudstaff without overstepping, 2026-05-27
