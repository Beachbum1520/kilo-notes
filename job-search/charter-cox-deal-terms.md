# Charter–Cox deal terms

Charter acquiring Cox Communications, announced May 2025, ~$34.5B enterprise value.
Cox Enterprises to own ~23% of the combined entity's fully diluted shares.
At closing: Charter pays $3.5B cash for Cox commercial fiber and managed IT assets; Charter Holdings pays $650M cash plus $6.0B of 6.875% convertible preferred units and ~33.6M common units; combined entity assumes ~$12.4B of Cox net debt.
Within a year of close the combined company renames to Cox Communications, with Spectrum as consumer brand; HQ stays in Stamford, CT with significant Atlanta campus presence.
Liberty Broadband merger closes immediately prior to the Cox close.

**Approx date:** July 2026
