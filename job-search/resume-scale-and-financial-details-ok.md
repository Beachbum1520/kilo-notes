# Operational scale and margin figures are fine on a resume

**Decided:** It's fine to put "more than 3,000 hotels", the 60%+ dispatch margin and the 200,000+ monitored devices on the resume sent to Velocity. The 3,000+ hotels figure probably doesn't belong on LinkedIn.

**Why:** Confidentiality is assumed during a hiring process; LinkedIn is public.

**Rejected alternatives:** Leaving those figures off the resume.

**Would revisit if:** unknown.
