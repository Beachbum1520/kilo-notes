# The asset is a ready-made team to attack CallTek's market

**What was wrong:** Claude framed the team as mostly CallTek's to move, and read onshoring as working against the play.

**Correct version:** CallTek serves ~85% of the market, including most of Scott's competitors, many of whom are shopping for a replacement. If Cox onshores Scott's 100+ Philippines team, that creates a ready-made team to go after his competitors. It is a real competitive advantage against what is probably the biggest barrier to entry in the space.
