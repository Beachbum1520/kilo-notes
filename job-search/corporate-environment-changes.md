# Changes in Scott's corporate environment ahead of the merger close

Scott is tired of the large corporate enterprise environment. Every decision takes about 15 meetings, and decision makers want 10 buy-ins to dilute blame until no one is responsible.
His boss, an AVP, has been told he will be reduced to senior director at closing, with the promise of a VP role in the near future.
A peer director got a VP offer in a different part of the business after close. As part of his succession plan, he recommended to Scott's boss that Scott take over the support side of Hospitality Networks (HN), a sister company Scott's boss also oversees.
That would mean more work for Scott without more title or pay. Scott expects his boss to say this was the original scope when he was hired.
Technically Scott is Sr Director of Operations for both BPRF and HN, but in almost 4 years he has had nothing to do with HN.
The talk at Charter is a return to office 4 or 5 days a week (from the current 2), and business casual is going away: no more jeans in the office.
