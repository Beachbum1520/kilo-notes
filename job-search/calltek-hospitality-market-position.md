# CallTek's position in the hospitality BPO market

CallTek serves about 85% of the hospitality market for BPO/call center support.
CallTek is one of Scott's two BPO vendors (the other is Cloudstaff).
CallTek's founder/CEO passed away in March 2025, and the new leadership has struggled in his absence.
Scott knows firsthand that many of his competitors are looking for a CallTek replacement. He knows all of the players in this market, and most of them are with CallTek.
Scott says pretty much all of his competitors use Philippines-based BPO services.
CallTek had a booth at HITEC.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** Pitching a BPO opportunity to Cloudstaff without overstepping, 2026-05-27
