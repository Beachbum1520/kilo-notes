# Charter leadership's view on onshoring, and Scott's likely role

From Scott's meetings with Charter senior leadership: they want to onshore but are not sure it can or should be done.
If the decision to move the team onshore happens, Scott is fairly confident the merged company will ask him to lead that effort over a multi-year process.
He thinks there would be a very strong desire to retain him to lead the onshore transition.
