# The GuestTek Way

At GuestTek, the full end-to-end lifecycle operations playbook Scott developed came to be known as "the GuestTek Way."
He believes developing and rolling out the GuestTek Way was probably the main reason he was promoted so quickly to the Managing Director positions in other regions. He traveled the globe rolling it out to every region GuestTek served, and when that was done he returned to North America as President of that region.
At GuestTek the handoff gates were FSR (Final Sales Review) and SSO (Support Sign Off). SSO was GuestTek's equivalent of the PIC he later built at Blueprint RF. FSR had the same name at both companies.
