# FSR and PIC quality gates at Blueprint RF

PIC (Post Install Checklist) is a check Scott created as the gatekeeper for handing a project from the install team to the support team. Support doesn't take a project on until his PIC team has audited the install.
FSR (Final Sales Review) is the matching gate for handing a deal from sales to the install team. His TDE (Technical Deployment Engineer) team reviews every deal before it is closed-won, to confirm the BOM, design, SOW and so on are complete and accurate, so the install happens on time with no change orders.
His PIC commitment is an 80% first-time pass rate this year and more than 96% next year, which he believes is achievable. He has similar targets for FSR.
[UNCERTAIN: figures Claude read from Scott's December 2025 scorecard] PIC first-time pass rate rose from 23% to 72% during 2025, and the review error rate fell from 11% to 4%.
