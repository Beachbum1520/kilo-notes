# CallTek guest WiFi support model for BPRF

Scott's CallTek team for hotel guest call center support takes about 12K calls per month, exclusively for his customer base.
CallTek hires these semi-skilled resources, trains them, and gets them ready to take calls on day 1 when they hit the floor on Scott's account.
Scott's company starts paying when agents hit the floor. It does not pay for any of the pre-work.

**Approx date:** July 2026

**Source:** Pitching a BPO opportunity to Cloudstaff without overstepping, 2026-05-27
