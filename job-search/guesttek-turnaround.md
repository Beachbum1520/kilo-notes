# GuestTek turnaround track record

At GuestTek, Scott took a global company with negative margins, which was using a corporate credit card to make payroll. Over 4 years he turned it into a profitable, saleable company, and the owner got an offer of more than $100M.
Scott says the owner "got stupid and greedy" and the sale didn't happen. If it had, Scott would have received a large payout and would have either retired or stayed a few years after the sale.
Scott describes himself as having a track record as a fixer and builder around the globe. He thinks there is a very good chance he would succeed with the Cloudstaff opportunity.
He says building something at Cloudstaff is exactly what he did at GuestTek, and that it is very exciting to him.
