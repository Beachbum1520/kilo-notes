# Cloudstaff's gap: managed services vs. traditional BPO

Cloudstaff acknowledged that hospitality support is more of a managed services environment than a traditional BPO resource model.
Cloudstaff does managed services in other verticals and has access to workforce management tools and a team for it. For hospitality, though, they are in a "don't know what they don't know" position.
They asked Scott for metrics (redacted as needed) to help them understand what they would need to run a call center operation.
Scott explained that as a BPO, Cloudstaff works more like an employer of record: it finds skilled offshore resources and hands them off to the client company. In hospitality, especially guest WiFi support, Cloudstaff would need to become a managed services provider instead.
