# Scott's view of Option A (lead onshoring for Charter)

Scott said Option A, if he is offered it, is the safe bet: more money and more security as he enters the "golden years" of his career.
Option A also angers him. Firing 100+ people because of where they live feels to him akin to firing someone over the color of their skin.
Option B (Cloudstaff) is a strong consideration partly because he feels responsible for his team; he has always cared deeply about the teams he leads.
Option B also appeals as a way back to a more entrepreneurial org, away from enterprise corporate politics, decision by committee, and blame diffusion.
On "protect what you've built vs. build one more thing," Scott says he has struggled with this for at least a year. His answer is "yes, no, and I don't know," and it depends on the day. Some days he wants to attack the world. Other days he feels his age and fatigue and thinks keeping his head down for about 7 more years to build his retirement account is the way to go.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** Pitching a BPO opportunity to Cloudstaff without overstepping, 2026-05-27
