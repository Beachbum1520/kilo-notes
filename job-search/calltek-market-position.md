# CallTek market position

CallTek controls ~85% of the hospitality BPO/managed-services supplier market, serving both Blueprint RF and its competitors.
CallTek's founder/CEO died unexpectedly at 46, ~16 months before May 2026.
Parent company Allied Global absorbed it; service quality has been declining since.
At HITEC 2026 (two weeks prior to May 27), the industry was actively shopping for an alternative provider.
