# At GuestTek, Support was a boundary partner

**What was wrong:** The resume said Scott "built and led ... teams across multiple continents with full responsibility for NOC, technical support, installations, and service delivery."

**Correct version:** At GuestTek, Support was a boundary partner. Once a project passed SSO, Scott was largely hands-off operationally. Support was one global organization serving all customers, while sales, operations and P&Ls were regional. Scott owned the regional P&Ls and received a support allocation. Support was accountable to him as regional head for the results he needed, and as long as they delivered he stayed out of their swim lanes.
