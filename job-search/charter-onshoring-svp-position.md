# Charter SVPs' view on the onshoring requirement

Two different SVPs at Charter have told Scott that the onshoring requirements apply to customers who call in to Cox/Spectrum.
Their position is that BPRF customers don't think they are calling either company. For most BPRF calls (guest WiFi support), callers most likely think they are calling Marriott, Hilton, and so on.
Scott says this will never be put in writing.
