# NOC/PNOC and Tier 3 structure

**What was wrong:** The resume listed "Proactive NOC" as a separate group, and described the organization as "a NOC, PNOC, tiered contact center, Tier 3 Engineering, and ATL Engineering teams," as if all of them reported to Scott.

**Correct version:** Scott has a NOC and a PNOC. Tier 3 and the NOC are the same team. ATL Engineering (Atlanta Network Engineering) is a boundary partner that reports to Scott's peer, not to him. They are accountable to him for what he needs operationally, but they are not direct reports.
