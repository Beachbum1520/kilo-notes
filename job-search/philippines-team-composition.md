# Scott's Philippines team composition and loyalty

Scott's team of 100+ people is mostly in the Philippines.
About 25 of them are employed through Cloudstaff and the rest through CallTek.
Most of the current Cloudstaff team was hired from CallTek.
Scott says the team is fiercely loyal to him. He may not be able to hire all of them in a new venture, but he could hire most.
Some team members have been on the BPRF account for decades and are 100% dedicated and exclusive to BPRF.
Scott considers it the best-skilled, highest-performing NOC in the industry ("a little bit of bias there, but not much"), and he has a strong reputation in the industry.
Scott said he truly cares about these 100+ people and their families, and that they made commitments to him.
If Charter pulls the plug, Scott says there is no way CallTek could retain all of them.
