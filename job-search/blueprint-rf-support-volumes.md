# Blueprint RF support call volumes and routing

Scott's team takes about 15,000 guest support calls and about 5,000 customer calls a month.
Guest support calls go to his Tier 1 and Tier 2 teams; customer calls go straight to his NOC.
They create far more tickets than calls, because of email-submitted tickets and tickets generated by proactive monitoring (as shown on his December 2025 support scorecard).
Blueprint RF supports more than 3,000 hotels.

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** VP Support Ops - Velocity, 2026-04-14
