# GuestTek turnaround and global tour

The North America region was GuestTek's biggest and was losing money at around a -4% margin when Scott started.
Scott recovered NA, then the owner sent him on a global tour to fix the other regions so the owner could sell the company.
Scott did that and helped get what he felt were very good offers for the company, but the owner got greedy and passed on them, and then COVID hit.
As he moved to other regions he never gave up his previous role/region responsibility.
He hired help: a VP of Ops for NA when he went to APAC, and an MD of APAC when he went to EMEA; both reported to him and he remained accountable for those regions.
He does not remember specific metrics for the MD APAC and MD EMEA roles.

**Approx date:** April 2026 (events 2016–2021)

**Source:** Teams interview preparation with Weston and Anthony, 2026-04-24
