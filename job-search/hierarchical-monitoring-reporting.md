# Proactive monitoring and hierarchical reporting

Blueprint RF monitors all network gear and IP endpoints (APs): over 200,000 devices, 24 hours a day.
They are adding AI logic to monitoring. Under "hierarchical reporting," a failure such as a switch going down produces one ticket listing every connected device, instead of separate tickets for the switch, the uplink and each AP. When the parent device recovers, the platform tests every connected device and tickets and responds to any that are still down.
The hierarchical view is also reported monthly to the brands in the monthly SLA compliance audits.
The "22 tickets" case (a 24-port switch with 20 APs and a fiber uplink) was only an example; a core switch failure could be more, a small switch less.
Last year's roughly 419K proactive tickets will drop substantially this year because of hierarchical ticketing.

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** VP Support Ops - Velocity, 2026-04-14
