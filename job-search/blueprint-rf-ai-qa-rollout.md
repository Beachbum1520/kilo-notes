# AI-based QA rollout at Blueprint RF

Blueprint RF was about to roll out a new AI-based QA tool.
Today QA is manual: about 2 monitors/audits per agent per month, done by the QA Manager for guest WiFi support and by NOC Team Leads for the NOC and PNOC. The AI will audit 100% of calls.
Monthly calibration calls will shift from keeping manual QA scoring consistent to making sure the AI scores consistently.
The tool adds new data points such as customer/guest mood and how it changes through the call: whether they're getting happier or more frustrated, and their mood at the start versus the end.
It is a vendor platform being customized internally and co-developed with one of Scott's BPO providers. The platform is Inteligems' OdysseyAI QA Evaluator.
