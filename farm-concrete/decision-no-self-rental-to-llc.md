# Don't charge the farm LLC rent for the land (for now)

**Decided:** Not worth setting up a self-rental arrangement where Scott personally charges the farm LLC rent for using the land he owns in his own name.

**Why:** The farm already runs at a loss, so there is no self-employment tax to save, and the rent would be a wash for income tax. Claude laid out this reasoning and Scott concluded "so not worth it."

**Rejected alternatives:** A written lease with the LLC paying fair-market rent to Scott, reported on Schedule E.
