# Polyurethane bead around the outside of the waterer base

**Decided:** Run a bead of Loctite PL Concrete polyurethane sealant around the outside edge of the waterer base for better sealing. This was Scott's idea.

**Why:** Extra sealing on top of the butyl.

**Rejected alternatives:** A siliconized acrylic latex caulk (easy water clean-up) that Scott proposed, and dropped after Claude said to pass on it.

**Would revisit if:** unknown.

**Approx date:** August 2026
