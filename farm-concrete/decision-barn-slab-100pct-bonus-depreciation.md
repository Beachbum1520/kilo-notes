# Take 100% bonus depreciation on the barn slab

**Decided:** Treat the $15,104 barn slab as one integrated asset that was finished, both acquired and placed in service, on February 10, 2025, and take 100% federal bonus depreciation on it for 2025.

**Why:** The slab took several steps paid on separate checks (rock base, contractor forms and concrete, Home Depot sealant and supplies, then his own labor to seal). To Scott, the slab wasn't done until the sealing on Feb 10. He has photos as proof if audited.

**Rejected alternatives:** 40% bonus plus Section 179 on the remainder, which is what Claude first said applied because the check was written 1/3/2025, before the Jan 20 cutoff. Scott pushed back and rejected that framing.

**Would revisit if:** unknown. Scott asked for a summary to send to his accountant, who knew about the lower bonus rate but not the 100% position. Implied: his accountant's review.

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** Concrete slab depreciation classification, 2026-04-14
