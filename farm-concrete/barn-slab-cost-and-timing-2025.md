# Barn concrete slab — cost and timeline (2025)

Scott added a concrete slab to his barn in January 2025 at a total cost of $15,104.
He wrote the check to the concrete contractor on 1/3/2025.
The slab was built in stages, each paid separately: rock brought in for the base, then the concrete contractor who built forms and had concrete delivered, then sealant and supplies bought at Home Depot.
Scott opted not to pay the contractor to seal it; he and his crew sealed it themselves.
Heavy snow right after the pour delayed the sealing, so the slab went into service on February 10, 2025.
Scott has photos documenting the timeline, as proof if audited.

**Approx date:** April 2026
