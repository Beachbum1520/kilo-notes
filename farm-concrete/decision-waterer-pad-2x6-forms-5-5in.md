# Waterer pads: 2x6 forms, 5.5" thick

**Decided:** Build the waterer pad forms from 2x6s for a 5.5" pad, figured at 25 bags (80 lb) per pad, with two pads.

**Why:** 2x4 and 1x4 forms only give 3.5". Scott asked whether he should consider 2x6s instead, and the Tusker manual calls for a 4–6" pad.

**Rejected alternatives:** 1x4 forms, and 2x4 forms at 3.5".
