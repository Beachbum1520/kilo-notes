# Butyl tape, two beads side by side

**Decided:** Seal the waterer base with 1/2" butyl tape (found to be 3/32" thick), in two parallel beads next to each other, not stacked. One roll per pad.

**Why:** The 1/2" tape was the only one readily available. Scott proposed "two wide and 2 tall." Since the pad is level with a broom finish, he settled on two wide, one layer.

**Rejected alternatives:** Stacking two tall. EMSEAL foam gasket. Ordering wider RV putty tape.

**Would revisit if:** unknown.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Corrosion-resistant bolt selection for wet environments, 2026-08-08
