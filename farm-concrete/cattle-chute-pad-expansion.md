# Cattle chute pad expansion

The existing cattle chute pad is 5' × 11', formed with 2x4s, so it is 3.5" thick.
Plan: add 4' to one long side, 11' to the other long side, and 4' to the front.
The truck can reach both the chute pad and the well pad; that's how the initial pads were poured.
The extension is not for the cows. It's for the humans to stand on while working the cows.
