# Offshore farm accountant in the Philippines

Scott used his offshore experience from his day job to hire an accountant in the Philippines for the farm.
The accountant is degreed, a CPA, and holds multiple QuickBooks certifications. He is paid $6/hr.
He handles all farm expense and income bookkeeping, and reconciles QuickBooks monthly.
Scott uses QuickBooks for the farm.
Each farm operation is set up in QuickBooks as a separate "enterprise."
The accountant knew about the lower bonus depreciation rate on the slab, but not the 100% position. Scott wanted a summary to send him.

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** Concrete slab depreciation classification, 2026-04-14
