# Level forms on tamped clay, no gravel or plastic

**Decided:** Pour the waterer pads directly on tamped clay, with no gravel base and no plastic underneath. Set the forms perfectly level and accept that the base may be a little off, since the form tops set a level pad.

**Why:** Scott had already done the groundwork a week earlier: eyeball level down to the clay, with topsoil and vegetation removed. He said, "if the base is a little off, no biggie. The tops will be level and as such so will the pad when dry." He also planned to weed the pads well before pouring.

**Rejected alternatives:** A compacted gravel (crusher run) base, which Claude recommended, and a plastic vapor barrier under the pad.

**Would revisit if:** unknown.
