# Permanent wedge anchors for the waterer bases

**Decided:** Use permanent wedge anchors, not removable drop-in anchors, for the Tusker waterer bases.

**Why:** In 10 years it won't be his farm. Even cutting off and redrilling 3 times covers 30 years, and "I'll be 95 years old then. For sure won't care."

**Rejected alternatives:** 316 or 304 stainless drop-in anchors with bolts. Claude had recommended 304 drop-ins for removability. Scott had noted that "'May' never need to pull is the challenge," and 316 drop-ins only came in 50-packs for about $300.

**Would revisit if:** unknown.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Corrosion-resistant bolt selection for wet environments, 2026-08-08
