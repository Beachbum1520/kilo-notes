# Zinc wedge anchors with stainless nuts and washers

**Decided:** Buy the 50-pack of zinc-plated 3/8" wedge anchors, plus 3/8" stainless nuts and washers, a 3/8" masonry bit, and anti-seize (Permatex).

**Why:** Scott doesn't think Home Depot has stainless wedge anchors. He asked, "if is permanent for 10 years why do I care?" He needs 40 anchors, and the 50-pack is much cheaper per unit. He adopted Claude's suggestion of stainless nuts and washers.

**Rejected alternatives:** All-stainless wedge anchors, and buying singles.

**Would revisit if:** unknown.
