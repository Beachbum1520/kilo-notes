**Concrete preferences and rules of thumb I've established**
- Bag yield: 60 lb bag ≈ 0.45 cu ft; 80 lb ≈ 0.60 cu ft. Bags per cubic yard: 60 sixties or 45 eighties.
- I mostly stock **60 lb High-Strength (4,000 psi)** — Sakrete or Quikrete, interchangeable; don't blend brands within a single batch but mixing brands across the job is fine.
- Water: ~½ gal per 60 lb bag; water in drum first, then bags, then top off to a **stiff-oatmeal/soft-peak** consistency. Err dry, never soupy.
- Base: pour on **tamped bare clay** (no gravel, no plastic underneath — plastic on clay makes a slip plane). Weed clean, re-tamp, dampen clay before pour. Cure the **top** with poly for 3–5 days.
- Forms: **2x6** (5.5" actual depth), set to a **level plane** by level/string, cut high side / pack low side so slab is level regardless of ground. Long boards full length, short boards + board thickness each end for corner overlap. Screws not nails (reusable forms). Brace/stake well, check diagonals for square.
- Finish: **broom finish** for livestock traction, never slick-troweled.
- Reinforce with rebar or wire mesh.
- Pour **early morning** in GA summer heat.
