## 12. Livestock & husbandry principles
- Blue-Kote (gentian violet) FDA-prohibited in food animals; use Triodine (7% iodine) for castration wound care.
- Dexters nurse others' calves; udder condition unreliable for dam ID — use post-calving vulvar signs + head count.
- Year-round bull exposure loses calving-season anchors for loss tracking.
- Boar pheromone priming improves conception; hybrid model = Chester with group default, AI for outside genetics with brief separation to avoid sire ambiguity.
- Large litters (12+): intervene after 30–45 min stalled late-farrow; always final manual check after apparent last piglet.
- Perilla mint (Perilla frutescens): serious cattle toxicity, peaks Aug–Oct (in peak window now); control 2,4-D Amine 400; wait until dead/brown before cattle access.
- Sweet gum near wood lines: long-term suppression, not one-time fix.
- CATTLE CALVING EASE [watch]: 2026 first-calf heifers show a dystocia cluster (2413 died on a stuck calf; 2411's & 2401's calves needed pull/came with tight tendons). Vet calving ease / heifer development before breeding replacements to Sugar Daddy. Detail in Cattle Herd Memory Card §8.
