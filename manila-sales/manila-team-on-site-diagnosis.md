# Scott's on-site diagnosis of the Manila inside sales team

From meeting and digging in with the team in Manila, Scott confirmed they had received little to no sales training, coaching, or feedback.
They understood their priority to be getting through the list of calls to make.
Scott listened to their pitches; his old saying "telling ain't selling" applies.
They were not asking if they could send a proposal.
They let the gatekeeper (GK) be the roadblock.
They didn't understand that for Choice, the GK is often part of the family that owns/operates the hotel, and they should try to sell to them if they can't get to the GM.
Scott: the team is "starting at less than zero"; Kathy in effect took the team he stood up and said "go sell."
Scott remains confident the team can succeed in Manila, but it's up to leadership to set them up with the right training and clear communication of expectations and priorities, which hadn't been done.
