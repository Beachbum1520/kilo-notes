# Choice contracts and cancellation-for-convenience

In Scott's industry, pretty much all hotel contracts include a cancellation-for-convenience clause.
Scott had been working under the assumption that, like Marriott, Hilton, and Hyatt, Choice hotels can cancel contracts at any time for any reason.
Scott had been told the Choice/economy segment is very price-driven, has little provider loyalty, and will "cancel over $5."
Whether Choice hotels are contract-bound materially impacts the inside sales strategy.
Scott was coaching the team to the pitch "If I can save you money on managed Wi-Fi, can I send a proposal?" — which only works if properties can leave their provider.
Marie (one of Scott's managers) sought clarity from Mark (Cox hospitality group leader); the reply indicated Choice gives no guidance on termination terms, and the Cox contract she pulled carries a 100% ETF for the full remaining term. Scott viewed the reply as a corporate non-answer.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** Choice Hotels cancellation clause clarification, 2026-05-28
