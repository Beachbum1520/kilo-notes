# Declined Wyndham pay-to-play approved vendor program

**Decided:** BPRF decided not to join Wyndham's pay-to-play program (which would make BPRF a certified/approved vendor).

**Why:** No enforcement mechanism by Wyndham at the brand corporate level, unlike Marriott, Hilton, etc. — so BPRF can still sell to Wyndham properties without being in the program.

**Rejected alternatives:** Joining the program.

**Would revisit if:** unknown

**Approx date:** July 2026

**Source:** Manila sales team training chat, 2026-07-30
