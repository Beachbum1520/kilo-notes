# Manila inside sales team — origin and purpose

Scott stood up a 3-person inside sales team in Manila to go specifically after Choice Hotels, an economy brand BPRF doesn't typically service.
The segment is low margin, so it was not worth the investment of a US-based seller.
After Scott stood the team up, his leader (Jady) took the small department and transferred it to Scott's peer who runs sales (Kathy); Scott said that move made sense at the time.
The team is staffed through Cloudstaff (BPO).
The team started actively calling on new hotels in late January / early February 2026 and had not closed a single deal as of late May 2026.
