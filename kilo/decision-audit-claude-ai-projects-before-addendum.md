# Audit what Claude.ai currently does in each project before writing the coaching addendum

**Decided:** Before drafting the §9.1 coaching addendum, Scott ran a self-audit prompt in each of three Claude.ai projects: Fitness/Training, Business Ops, and Job Opportunities/Cloudstaff. It asked about data inputs, decision logic, pushback behavior, cross-session memory, gaps and goal alignment. He brought the responses back to design Kilo.

**Why:** Scott wanted Claude to understand what Claude.ai currently does for him as an AI coach, including all the data points and inputs it considers, so Kilo would be built toward that end goal.

**Rejected alternatives:** Designing the fitness coaching mode on its own before seeing the other projects.
