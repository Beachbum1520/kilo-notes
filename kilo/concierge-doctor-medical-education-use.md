# Medical questions are for education, not decisions

Scott pays a large sum of money to a concierge doctor for medical advice.
He would never use AI as medical advice or make a medical decision based on an AI chatbot.
He wants to be able to ask what a specific medical test and its results mean, for his own education and context before his next doctor appointment.
Ideally, on health and fitness topics he wants fewer guardrails.
