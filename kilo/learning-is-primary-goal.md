# Learning is the primary goal, no time crunch

**Decided:** Build the assistant as a learning exercise, not on a deadline. Scott is not in a time crunch or "dire straits" to get it done quickly.

**Why:** The WattsWay fitness app came to life primarily as how he learns: he needs to learn how to better use AI in his business, and he learns by doing. Secondarily, there is a need for an AI coach because existing ones are bad. The same applies to this AI assistant.

**Rejected alternatives:** A fast, delivery-focused build.

**Would revisit if:** unknown.
