# Cattle sorting lane layout

The sorting lane is part of the cattle working system and directs cattle to one paddock or another as they exit the chute.
A 6-foot gate on the middle post swings to the left or right post to send the animal left or right.
Two additional posts to the left and right of the middle post complete the lane.
The lane uses four 2x6 rails; the span between posts is about 7 ft.

**Approx date:** August 2026
