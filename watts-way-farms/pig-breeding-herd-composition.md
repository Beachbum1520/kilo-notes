# Pig breeding herd composition

Boar: Chester, purebred Duroc, not registered.
Gilts and sows: Scarlet, Hazel, Mabel, Ginger and Nutmeg.
Ginger and Nutmeg are the only registered stock, registered Durocs.
Scarlet is a purebred Duroc, not registered.
Hazel and Mabel are purebred Berkshires.
Willow and Goldie are F1 crosses out of Hazel. These are the first F1s they have kept for breeding.

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** Hog pricing and customer response, 2026-04-26
