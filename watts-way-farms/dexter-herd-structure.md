# Dexter herd structure

More than 60 head of Dexters.
16 calves on the ground in 2026; Scott assumes that's an average annual calf crop, with roughly the same number each year.
Roughly 50/50 bull/heifer split; all heifers currently retained; all bull calves become steers.
Steers are raised approx 24–29 months to processing.
One herd bull.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** Memorial Day campaign setup for Watts Way Farms, 2026-05-14
