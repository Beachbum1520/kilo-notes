# Switch to $2.50/lb shipping with a minimum order

**Decided:** Move GrazeCart shipping to per-pound at $2.50/lb and set a minimum order threshold (Scott's idea to pair the minimum with per-pound). Configured in GrazeCart: Delivery Fee Type Per Pound, $2.50, Tax Delivery Fee No, Cap Delivery Fee Yes.

**Why:** Flat fee was losing money on multi-box orders, which are a common pattern; per-pound with a minimum avoids tiny orders with high shipping.

**Rejected alternatives:** Flat $40; tiered shipping (not supported); order caps; baking shipping into prices.

**Would revisit if:** unknown

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** Memorial Day campaign setup for Watts Way Farms, 2026-05-14
