# Keep the original multi-ingredient finishing recipe for the first batch

**Decided:** The first steer finishing batch uses the original multi-ingredient recipe, not a simplified mix.

**Why:** Scott said his wife would not agree to change the recipe for such a small saving (Claude estimated about $95 across 4 steers on bagged prices).

**Rejected alternatives:** A simpler 85% cracked corn / 15% soybean meal ration with free-choice hay (Claude's suggestion). Rejected because the savings were too small to justify the change.

**Would revisit if:** Real feed-store prices showed a bigger saving. Scott planned to get actual ingredient costs and rerun the numbers.

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** Beef finishing feed ratios, 2026-04-22
