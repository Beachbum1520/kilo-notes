# Poultry processing pad: level 10x12 slab with floor drain

**Decided:** If he builds, pour a level 10x12 concrete slab with a floor drain to a catch basin for poultry processing.

**Why:** "If I'm paying for a concrete slab I want it level."

**Rejected alternatives:** Processing on open pasture (doesn't meet drainable-floor rule); a sloped pad that sheds to gravel with no drain.

**Would revisit if:** GDA says a different setup is acceptable.

**Approx date:** September 2026

**Source:** Trump's executive order on farm meat processing, 2026-09-06
