# Breeding philosophy: F1 Berkshire × Duroc for market

They breed F1 Berkshire × Duroc crosses for market because they like the meat quality of the F1 cross.
Purebred litters are thrown when they are looking to expand or replace breeding stock, so purebred Berkshires may be a while.
