# Run the boar with all the sows, as they used to

**Decided:** Go back to running Chester with all the sows. They will watch for heat and mounting, record the dates, and plan from them. Pregnant sows go into farrowing pens once they are a week or two from farrowing, which is easy to tell by then. How AI fits into this is TBD.

**Why:** It's how they used to do it, before they used AI. Scott thinks it will give a better success rate than what they've seen from keeping animals separate and relying on AI plus bringing in the boar only at heat.

**Rejected alternatives:** Keeping sows separate and breeding only by AI, or putting the boar in only when a sow is in heat.

**Would revisit if:** unknown. How AI works alongside this is still undecided.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** Pig paddock moves and breeding activity, 2026-05-03
