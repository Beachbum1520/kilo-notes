# Georgia Dept. of Agriculture has been unclear on poultry exemption

Scott and Angie called GDA many times about processing up to 1,000 birds for resale and got no clear answer; everyone pointed to someone else.
A Georgia YouTuber was visited by GDA the previous summer and told to stop processing birds for resale.
GDA told them the house toilet and handwashing are not good enough; they have to be where the birds are processed.
Scott understood this to mean building a facility costing around $100,000 or more.
Scott believes Georgia has given in to corporate influence such as Tyson at small farmers' expense.

**Approx date:** September 2026

**Source:** Trump's executive order on farm meat processing, 2026-09-06
