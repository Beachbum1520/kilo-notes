# Cure/smoke allowance included in pork price

**Decided:** The pork price includes curing and smoking on up to 20 lb of ham and bacon per half (40 lb per whole); cure/smoke beyond that may carry an extra per-pound charge. Added to the standard pork pricing message.

**Why:** Scott wanted wording that processing is included but only covers a reasonable amount of cure and smoke, based on a normal hog.

**Rejected alternatives:** Vague "reasonable" wording; the brat-limit wording Claude drafted by misunderstanding the request.

**Would revisit if:** Unknown.

**Approx date:** September 2026

**Source:** Whole hog pricing and hanging weight details, 2026-09-16
