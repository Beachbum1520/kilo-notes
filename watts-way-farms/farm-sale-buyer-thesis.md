# Farm sale buyer thesis

Scott agrees that 85 head of Dexters on 90 acres would not support a $1 million note.
Scott believes he could easily double the herd on the farm.
Scott thinks a likely buyer is a local farmer expanding their operation. A move-cows-in-today setup is attractive to that buyer.
Scott's view: a barn, working facility, and well can't be moved onto a farm someone buys, so they keep their value to an expansion buyer.
Scott's view: whoever buys the farm would maintain the infrastructure whether or not a contract or clawback applies. [UNCERTAIN on the legal side. Claude read the O&M clause as binding only on the participant.]
