# Rotational grazing has been practiced since the beginning with hot wire

Scott has been doing rotational grazing since the beginning, using hot wire.
The paddocks are big multi-acre paddocks.
The plan is to rotate the herd within the paddocks using the virtual fence collars instead of electric fence.
Permanent cross fences serve a different purpose: weaning, separating heifers not ready to breed, feeding out steers, bulls — situations where electric fencing comes up short and Scott expects collars will too.
