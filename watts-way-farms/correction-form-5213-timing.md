# Form 5213 timing

**What was wrong:** Scott believed you file Form 5213 after the IRS questions your profitability on the 3-of-5-year test.

**Correct version:** Per Claude, the election is due within 3 years after the due date of the first year's return, or within 60 days of an IRS notice if that comes first. The notice route doesn't extend the window. Scott was told to confirm with his accountant.
