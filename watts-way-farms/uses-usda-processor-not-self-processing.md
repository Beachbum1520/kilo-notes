# Scott uses a USDA processor and does not process animals himself

Scott does not process his own pigs; he uses a USDA-inspected processor.
Because of this he cannot do on-farm checks like heating fat samples for boar taint at slaughter.
Feeding out an animal for months and then finding boar taint would be an expensive risk he is not willing to take on blindly.

**Source:** Piglet castration wound care products, 2026-06-13
