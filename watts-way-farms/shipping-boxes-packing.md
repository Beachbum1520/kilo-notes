# Shipping box sizes and packing practice

Two box sizes: 10x10 and 12x12.
Boxes should be moderately filled, leaving room for a couple of layers of ice packs.
Uses at least 3 or 4 ice packs, top and bottom, and stuffs them in to keep the box mostly full without overfilling.
Ships just about anywhere.
