# $100 poultry tier doesn't need a USDA-inspected plant

**What was wrong:** Scott said Claude had told him the $100 license tier requires an inspected, approved facility.

**Correct version:** The ≤1,000-bird $100 registration tier requires basic on-site sanitary facilities (toilet, handwash, drainable floor, overhead cover, potable water), not a federally inspected plant.

**Approx date:** September 2026

**Source:** Trump's executive order on farm meat processing, 2026-09-06
