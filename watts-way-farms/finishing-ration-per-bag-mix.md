# Steer finishing ration mixed per bag of corn

Scott's finishing ration is mixed per 50 lb bag of cracked corn: 1.5 scoops cottonseed hulls, 1 scoop beet pulp, 0.5 scoop soybean meal, 0.5 scoop BOSS. "That's how we mix it."

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** December processing batch steers, 2026-08-29
