# Hang sorting-lane rails directly on round posts, no flats

**Decided:** Hang the 2x6 rails straight onto the round posts without cutting chainsaw flats or shimming; if boards come loose, tighten or replace them.

**Why:** Scott wasn't willing to do the extra work of cutting flats on every post; it's a sorting lane he can maintain as needed.

**Rejected alternatives:** Chainsaw flats on each post where the rails land (too much work); shimming behind each board end.

**Would revisit if:** Boards repeatedly loosen or split.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Cattle sorting gate fastener specifications, 2026-08-16
