# Use an RV bathroom for poultry toilet and handwash

**Decided:** [UNCERTAIN] Scott proposed parking an RV with a bathroom (toilet, hot/cold water, sinks) next to the processing pad to meet the on-site toilet and handwash requirement, pending GDA confirmation.

**Why:** Avoids building a permanent restroom.

**Rejected alternatives:** Using the house bathroom (GDA said no); building a permanent facility.

**Would revisit if:** GDA rejects it.

**Approx date:** September 2026

**Source:** Trump's executive order on farm meat processing, 2026-09-06
