# Shipping vs. meetup policy

Whole and half hogs are not shipped; they are handled by meetup.
Retail cuts are sold and shipped through the website, wattswayfarms.com.
For roasters or piglets, Scott could probably meet a buyer halfway if they buy 5 or more.
