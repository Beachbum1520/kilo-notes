# No advance quotes for EQIP work

**Decided:** Scott will not get contractor quotes in advance for the EQIP practices.

**Why:** NRCS pays a fixed rate per practice no matter what the work actually costs. On past contracts some items cost more than the estimate and some cost less, and Scott kept the difference on the ones that came in under. He only has to prove the work meets standard to get paid.

**Rejected alternatives:** Claude's suggestion to get real bids on the well, pipeline, waterway, and fence before signing.

**Would revisit if:** Not stated.

**Approx date:** July 2026

**Source:** NRCS - EQIP contract 2026, 2026-07-31
