# Feed buying and mixing constraints

Scott can't buy half a bag of anything. Everything comes in 50 lb bags except black oil sunflower seeds, which come in 40 lb bags.
He doesn't want to buy a whole finish's worth of feed at once. For the first run he wanted enough to fill two or three 55-gallon barrels.
He bought 20 bags for the first batch.
He mixes in a 55-gallon barrel using a 3-quart scoop, not a scale.
