# No 7-of-10 farm hobby-loss form

**What was wrong:** Scott thought there was a farm-specific hobby-loss form that extends the test to 7 out of 10 years.

**Correct version:** Per Claude, the extended presumption is 2 profitable years out of 7, and it applies only to horse activities. Form 5213 is the only election form, and cattle and hogs use the standard 3-of-5.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Farm worker raise and tax deduction analysis, 2026-08-02
