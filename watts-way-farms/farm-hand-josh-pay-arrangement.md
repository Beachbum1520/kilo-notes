# Farm hand Josh pay arrangement

Josh is Scott's farm hand.
Josh has been paid $10/hour in cash, under the table, from Scott's personal account with post-tax dollars.
He works about 10–11 hours a week. He chooses his own hours and schedule but uses Scott's equipment.
Josh asked for a raise to $13.50/hour.
Scott told him a raise would mean at minimum a 1099 at year end, so Scott gets a tax write-off.
Scott believes Josh won't earn enough to owe income tax.
