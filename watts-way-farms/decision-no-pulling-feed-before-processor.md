# Don't pull feed on the farm before processing

**Decided:** Feed is not pulled on the farm before the cull cow goes to the processor.

**Why:** Scott said "the boss" (his wife) would not allow pulling feed. They drop at the processor on Monday and the processor doesn't dispatch until Tuesday, so the animal is off feed there anyway.

**Rejected alternatives:** Pulling feed 12-24 hours before transport (Claude's suggestion).
