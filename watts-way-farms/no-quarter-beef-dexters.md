# No quarter beef shares on Dexter cattle

**Decided:** Watts Way Farms does not sell quarter beef shares.

**Why:** Dexters are smaller than typical commercial cattle like Angus and Hereford, so a Dexter half is comparable to a commercial quarter.

**Rejected alternatives:** Offering quarters like commercial operations.

**Would revisit if:** unknown.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** Dexter cattle box sizes and pork availability, 2026-05-12
