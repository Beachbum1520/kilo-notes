# Heavy use pad rock on the current contract

The heavy use pads on the current EQIP contract (not the new one) use crusher run (GAB) over geotextile fabric, 6 inches deep.
Scott is building the pads at 34' x 33'.
The dump trucks dump rock onto the fabric and Scott spreads it with a tractor. He is not renting a compactor.
Scott will not place 7.5" of loose rock.
The fabric rolls are 12.5' wide, three widths per pad. Scott lays the two outside runs first, then the middle.
On the second waterer, one side coming off the 60" side of the concrete can only go 13' because of the cut-off valve.
The waterer concrete pad is 60" x 65".
