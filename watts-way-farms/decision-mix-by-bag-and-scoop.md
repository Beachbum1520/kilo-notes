# Mix finishing feed one bag of corn at a time, with scoops, and roll the barrel

**Decided:** Mix feed by dumping one bag of corn plus scoops of the other ingredients into a 55-gallon barrel, then putting the lid on and rolling the barrel to mix.

**Why:** Scott found this the easiest method. It's hard to measure 0.7 or 0.3 of a bag, and rolling the lidded barrel mixes well.

**Rejected alternatives:** Proportioning partial bags per barrel (too hard to measure). Mixing all 20 bags on a tarp and then splitting them into barrels (Claude's suggestion).
