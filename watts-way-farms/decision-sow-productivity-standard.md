# Sow standard: 2 litters a year of 10 weaned, or freezer camp

**Decided:** The goal is 2 litters per sow per year, with 10 piglets raised to weaning per litter. Sows that can't maintain that go to "freezer camp." Mabel gets one more chance to produce.

**Why:** Productivity per sow drives the economics. Mabel's small litter was her first, so she gets another try.

**Rejected alternatives:** Planning around 1 litter per year (Claude raised it as a scenario).

**Would revisit if:** unknown

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** Hog pricing and customer response, 2026-04-26
