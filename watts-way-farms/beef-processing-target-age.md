# Ideal beef processing age is 24–29 months

The ideal is to process steers between 24 and 29 months of age.
The 2025 steers are not a 2028 processing group; the farm simply fell behind.
At over 30 months, carcasses can't be cut into T-bones.

**Approx date:** July 2026

**Source:** Farm cattle inventory count, 2026-07-27
