# Waterer drain hole plug

The waterer drain hole measures 2" at the outside, 1 3/8" at the inside, and 2 5/16" deep.
Scott needed a plug that inserts and tightens with a center bolt.
Scott was right that a plug shorter than the bore depth would seat fine. The 2" tapered plug size was the right fit.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Rock size requirements for heavy use pads, 2026-08-05
