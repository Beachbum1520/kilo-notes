# Ginger/Nutmeg sire "Roll the Dice 19-4" was fabricated

**What was wrong:** From July 2026 on, records and customer messages said Ginger and Nutmeg were sired by Roll the Dice 19-4 (Vegas Odds and Grand Slam behind). Claude introduced this in a July 6, 2026 Facebook post draft with no source. It was repeated through August and September, including in messages Scott sent to buyers, and again on 9/17 after being flagged.

**Correct version:** The registration paper Scott photographed shows sire SDH3 Too Deep 262-1 and dam WHGD3 Meme 2 2-2. Roll the Dice 19-4 is the sire on the buyer's boar's papers. The shared link with the buyer's boar is the dam.

**Approx date:** September 2026
