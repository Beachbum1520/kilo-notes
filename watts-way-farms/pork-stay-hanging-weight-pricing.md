# Keep hanging-weight pricing for whole hogs

**Decided:** Keep pricing whole hogs per pound of hanging weight ($5.50/lb all-in), not a flat fee.

**Why:** After seeing actual hanging weights (230, 204, 241 lb) and costs per take-home pound ($6.26–$7.20), the weight spread made a flat fee a poor fit.

**Rejected alternatives:** Flat fee per whole pig (Scott raised it).

**Would revisit if:** Unknown.

**Approx date:** September 2026

**Source:** Whole hog pricing and hanging weight details, 2026-09-16
