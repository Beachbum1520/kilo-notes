# One piglet from Scarlet's litter had only one testicle found at castration

When Scott and Angie castrated Scarlet's piglets (litter born 5/15/26), only one testicle was found on one piglet; that one was removed.
Angie searched and Scott searched; no second testicle was found.
Angie had been checking him since birth and the second testicle never showed.
Scott refers to him as the "one nut wonder."
Scott expected he would quickly be too big for them to fix themselves.
