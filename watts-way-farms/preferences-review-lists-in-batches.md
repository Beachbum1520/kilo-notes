# Reviewing question lists with Scott

When reviewing a long list, show Scott 5 items at a time if he asks for that. Keep a running record of what he approved, cut, or changed.
Never carry forward or renumber items he hasn't reviewed as if they were approved. Don't invent items.
When he asks for the complete list, give the entire list, not just the approved subset.
Keep questions short. Don't pad them with detail he already knows (for example, "we all know what a spec sheet is").
Use his terms: say "cross fence, waterer, and well locations," not "practice locations."
When he asks for something copy-paste ready, give just the text in a plain block.

**Source:** NRCS - EQIP contract 2026, 2026-07-31
