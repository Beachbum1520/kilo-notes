# Customer sales message content

Don't include information buyers don't care about, such as whether the farm keeps a boar, unless Scott asks for it.
Don't ask buyers what they plan to do with the animal.
Don't add sales-pitch lines Scott didn't ask for (for example, the "full sisters make a matched pair" line).
Include the known details for every animal listed, including bred ones: DOB, bloodline, due date.
When the app's copy button fails, give the message as plain text with no extra punctuation or formatting.

**Source:** Selling Willow's piglet litter, 2026-08-11
