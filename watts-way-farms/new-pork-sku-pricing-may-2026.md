# Pricing for new brat and bacon SKUs

**Decided:** Bratwurst - Original $11.50/lb; Bratwurst - Cheddar $12.75/lb; Bratwurst - Jalapeño & Cheddar $12.75/lb; Bacon - Cured & Smoked $14.99/lb; Bacon Ends - Cured & Smoked $9.99/lb. All priced by weight, ~1 lb packages.

**Why:** Scott called the pricing "perfect" after seeing it sits near the pasture-raised market average, roughly double grocery-store brats.

**Rejected alternatives:** Pushing to the upper tier ($12.50/$13.50) at launch.

**Would revisit if:** unknown.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** Recent pork pickup inventory, 2026-05-13
