# How the pig breeding program is described to other breeders

The farm keeps purebred Berkshire and purebred Duroc as breeding stock and primarily does F1 crosses of the two.
They occasionally do purebred AI breedings to build breeding stock.
Scott stated that breeding is now exclusively AI with superior genetics from Shipley Swine.
