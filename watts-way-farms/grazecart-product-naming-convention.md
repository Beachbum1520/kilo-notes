# GrazeCart product naming: "Category - Variant"

**Decided:** New product titles follow the "Category - Variant" pattern, e.g. "Bratwurst - Original", "Bratwurst - Cheddar", "Bratwurst - Jalapeño & Cheddar", "Bacon - Cured & Smoked", "Bacon Ends - Cured & Smoked".

**Why:** Matches the existing Sausage naming (Sausage - Hot, Sausage - Mild, Sausage - Ground / Plain) so product families sort together. Scott asked which format to use and went with it.

**Rejected alternatives:** "Original Bratwurst" style.

**Would revisit if:** unknown.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** Recent pork pickup inventory, 2026-05-13
