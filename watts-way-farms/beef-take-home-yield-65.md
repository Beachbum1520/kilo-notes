# Beef consistently yields ~65% of hanging weight in take-home

The farm's beef consistently yields about 65% of hanging weight in packaged take-home meat.
Scott knows this because it became a debate: on a batch of about 4–6 head the processor said yield was 60% or below on beef, and that batch came back right at 65%. The processor said it must be because they were Dexters.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Farm cattle inventory count, 2026-07-27
