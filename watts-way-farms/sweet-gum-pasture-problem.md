# Sweet gum seedlings taking over pastures

The pastures are full of sweet gum seedlings and saplings. They used to be overrun.
Last year they sprayed, then kept the sweet gum cut with a brush hog. It is coming back with a vengeance.
They sprayed first and waited over 39 days before cutting.
Perilla mint (beefsteak plant) is also a target for the same spraying.

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** Beefsteak plant toxicity in pigs, 2026-04-11
