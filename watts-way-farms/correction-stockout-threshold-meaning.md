# Stock-out threshold is internal, not customer-facing

**What was wrong:** Scott wanted Stock-out Threshold at 0, believing a value of 3 would show "out of stock" to customers at 3 remaining.

**Correct version:** Stock-out Threshold only adds the product to the internal stock-out report; the separate Out Of Stock Threshold is the customer-facing one. Scott then set stock-out to 3.
