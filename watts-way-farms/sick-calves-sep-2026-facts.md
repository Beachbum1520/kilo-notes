# Sick penned calves — situation facts

The affected animals are from the 2025 calf crop; 23 are penned together in the cattle working system.
They're on the finishing ration with free-choice hay while penned.
Two calves showed drooling, foaming, and leg weakness a few weeks after working.
None of the affected calves were castrated or tagged at working.
All the calves got ivermectin pour-on (standard formulation), with doses based on guessed weights as they've done for years, all from the same gun.
Scott thought botulism or listeriosis would have affected more than 2 of 23.

**Approx date:** September 2026

**Source:** Tetanus in calves after working, 2026-09-15
