# Cash at pickup only

**Decided:** Cash at pickup only for livestock sales ("Cash is king at Watts Way Farms"). No Zelle, no payment apps, no paying upfront to hold an animal.

**Why:** Scott believed Zelle charges fees, and they got burned before, probably on Venmo. The policy also blocks scammers.

**Rejected alternatives:** Zelle, Venmo, upfront remote payment.

**Would revisit if:** Not stated.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Selling Willow's piglet litter, 2026-08-11
