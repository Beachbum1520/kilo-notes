# Farm network equipment

The farm network is a Ubiquiti UniFi Cloud Gateway, a USW Lite 8 PoE switch, and 2 U7 Outdoor APs.
They serve 7 Eufy WiFi solar cameras and a Eufy HomeBase.
Internet is from Spectrum.
The gateway and switch are in a sealed environmental enclosure on the barn wall, under the barn roof and out of the sun.
The setup worked fine for about a year before outages started.

**Approx date:** September 2026

**Source:** Ubiquiti network dropping offline intermittently, 2026-09-04
