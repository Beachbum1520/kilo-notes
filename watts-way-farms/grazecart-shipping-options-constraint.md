# GrazeCart shipping fee constraint

GrazeCart offers no tiered shipping options — only flat fee or per-pound. Scott has been down this path before.
At the time of the Memorial Day push GrazeCart was set to a flat shipping fee per order.
Minimum order settings in GrazeCart live under Delivery Zones (set per zone).
