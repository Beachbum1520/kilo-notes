# Breed purebreds and produce F1s; don't breed F1s

**Decided:** Scott prefers to breed purebreds only and produce F1 crosses from them, not to breed the F1s. Willow and Goldie are an exception: the plan is one litter each as a stop-gap, "but who knows."
