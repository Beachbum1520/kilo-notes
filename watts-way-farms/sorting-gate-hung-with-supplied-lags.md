# Sorting gate hung with the lags supplied with its hangers

**Decided:** The 6' sorting gate is hung with the hangers and lag screws that came with it; if/when the lags give out, replace them with through-bolts.

**Why:** The gate was already hung with the included hardware.

**Rejected alternatives:** Through-bolting the gate hinges now with 5/8"–3/4" HDG machine bolts (Claude's suggestion) — deferred until the lags fail.

**Would revisit if:** The lags loosen or the gate sags.
