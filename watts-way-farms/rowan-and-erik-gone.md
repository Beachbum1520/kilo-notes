# Rowan processed, Erik sold

Rowan and Erik are gone: Erik was sold and Rowan was processed.
Rowan was called "Red."
Rowan hung at 800 lb and was one of the best-tasting animals they've had to date; the fear that an older bull would be tougher beef did not pan out.
Rowan's processing invoice was dated Jan 24, 2025; processing was around then.
