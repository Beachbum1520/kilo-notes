# Fellow farmer's Berkshire sow dystocia

A fellow farmer called Scott about a Berkshire sow in labor since 8 AM with a stuck piglet; the vet couldn't be reached.
The piglet was head first. They got it out, but the sow died the next morning.
Litter was about 14; the farmer pulled the last 3, and the last one was huge — too big to pass.
The sow was AI'd with the same boar semen as Mabel (EL Macho). Scott asked whether he should be concerned for Mabel.
Scott asked whether Nuflor would work as the antibiotic.
Scott asked how hard it would be to get Scarlet to adopt some of the orphaned piglets; the farmer's piglets were doing well, and Scott assumed he was pan feeding them.
