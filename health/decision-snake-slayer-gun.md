# Snake gun: switched to the Bond Arms Snake Slayer

**Decided:** Scott ordered a Bond Arms .45 Colt/.410 derringer for killing snakes on the farm. He first ordered the Texas Defender, then switched to the Bond Arms Snake Slayer.

**Why:** Cottonmouths near the ponds and Zack's bite. The Snake Slayer's extended grip gives better control for a working farm gun.

**Rejected alternatives:** The Texas Defender (smaller carry grip); a long-handled brush axe (he couldn't get closer than about 15 ft before a snake darted into the water).

**Would revisit if:** unknown

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** Snake bite fatality risk from finger wound, 2026-04-12
