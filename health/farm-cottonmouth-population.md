# Cottonmouths on the farm

All the cottonmouths (water moccasins) Scott has seen on the farm are right around the ponds.
Last year they killed 3 large ones. One was nearly 5 ft long and as big around as a softball; the others were about 4 ft.
About 3 weeks before Zack's bite, the fence crew killed one in the same area where Zack was bitten.
A few days after the bite, Scott saw an 18–24 inch moccasin at a weed-covered rock at the water's edge during a herd check.
In late April 2026 he killed a cottonmouth with the Snake Slayer at that same spot. It was his second of the year. He buried it deep so the dogs and other animals wouldn't get to it.
The ponds hold frogs, small turtles, fish, and likely rodents.

**Approx date:** April 2026
