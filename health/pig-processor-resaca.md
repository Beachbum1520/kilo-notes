# Pig processing logistics

Watts Way Farms uses a USDA processor in Resaca, GA, about 3 hours from home. The processor opens at 8 AM.
In April 2026 the batch at the processor was split: 2 pigs to be delivered to customers who had already paid, and one for the family freezer.
Scott's home freezer needed a new control board. The repairman's second visit was set for Tuesday, so there was no freezer space for the incoming meat.
Other routine farm supply runs: pig feed pickup, and hauling loads of 15 round bales of hay with the big trailer (45-minute drive each way).
