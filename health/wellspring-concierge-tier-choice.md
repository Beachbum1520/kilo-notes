# Choose Wellspring MD Concierge tier, not Balance

**Decided:** Go with the base Concierge tier (~$2,800/yr) at Wellspring MD rather than Balance or higher tiers. [UNCERTAIN — Claude recommended it; Scott asked for confirmation and later signed up; the call debrief says Concierge was confirmed by both Scott and Dr. Bailey]

**Why:** The upper tiers mainly add bundled DEXA/VO2/RMR (which Scott gets at Life Fit) and functional-medicine testing he doesn't want. On the discovery call, TRT co-management and standard labs were confirmed as available at the base tier.

**Rejected alternatives:** Balance ($3,600) for "hormone testing"; Precision/Insight/Performance for extra scans and nutrition visits.

**Would revisit if:** Dr. Bailey gated TRT co-management behind a higher tier, or Scott wanted Bailey to cover more scans.
