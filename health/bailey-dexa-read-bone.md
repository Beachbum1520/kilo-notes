# Bailey's read of the DEXA scans

Bailey reviewed both DEXA scans. He called BMD normal with an excellent T-score, and put the changes down to weight loss and body composition shifts, not real bone loss. He noted that whole-body composition DEXAs aren't the right tool for fracture risk.
He said PTH with calcium and vitamin D was reasonable, but P1NP and CTX would likely be denied without an osteoporosis diagnosis.
