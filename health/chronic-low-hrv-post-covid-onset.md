# Chronic low HRV began after second COVID infection

Scott's chronic low HRV and higher-than-expected resting HR predate his GLP-1 (tirzepatide) use.
He believes it correlates with his second COVID infection; onset was definitely after that infection, around mid-to-late 2021.
At the time he used a Whoop band (no Oura yet); HRV was consistently in the single digits.
He discussed it many times with his doctor at the time.
This was before he moved to Georgia for the job he began in September 2022.
