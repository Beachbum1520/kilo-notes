# Zack's snakebite and hospitalization (April 2026)

Zack (11), one of Scott's three adopted children, was bitten on the index finger by a snake believed to be a baby, on Sunday, April 12, 2026, at the farm.
Scott was marking an area for the next clearing/cross-fencing phase when it happened; the boys were with him.
Zack reached the ER in LaGrange within an hour and was airlifted about 1.5 hours after the bite to Children's Healthcare of Atlanta. Swelling had not reached his wrist when he was loaded onto the helicopter.
The medical team decided not to give antivenom and consulted Poison Control.
The next day his fingertip turned dark, swelling spread up the forearm, and the team discussed operating (debridement). He was switched between NPO and cleared to eat several times in one day.
On Wednesday morning, April 15, he had a procedure where they laid back about an inch of skin on his index finger to relieve pressure from the swelling.
Zack was discharged on Thursday, April 16. He was on morphine during the stay.
