# Scott's corneal scratch during the snakebite rescue

Scott took a stick to the eye while getting Zack out of the woods after the bite.
The pain kept him from driving safely. Urgent care diagnosed a corneal scratch and prescribed ofloxacin eye drops and Refresh Tears lubricant.
He ended up parked in a grocery store parking lot with his 9-year-old son and decided to wait about 2 hours for his wife instead of calling 911 or taking an Uber/Lyft (the hospital was 68 miles away).
By the next day the eye was "a good bit better," though not 100%.

**Approx date:** April 2026

**Source:** Snake bite fatality risk from finger wound, 2026-04-12
