# Presbyopia and readers

Scott needs readers for close-up; he really can't see up close without them.
His distance vision is excellent; eye doctors have always told him he isn't a candidate for corrective surgery because any surgery or intervention (including contacts) would hurt his distance vision.
Vuity drops did nothing for him beyond making the world look slightly darker; he still needed readers.
His eye doctor tested him for monovision and found no apparent dominant eye; the doctor said he could try it but wasn't hopeful he'd like it.
He is left-handed but does many things right-handed.
Several years ago he tried progressive (multifocal) contacts in both eyes: struggled getting them in and out, got scared around day 3-4 when he couldn't get one out for a long time, and gave up; they also blurred distance things like street signs.
He buys readers from Look Optics (about $68/pair), has ordered from them several times, and keeps two pairs close at almost all times.
He hates having to wear readers.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** Correcting presbyopia without compromising distance vision, 2026-05-19
