# Using the Hatch for noise at night; his wife's view

Scott has a Hatch alarm that plays noise. He worried it would need to be so loud to work that it would disturb his wife on the other side of the bed.
His wife has really good hearing, and she asked, "What's it going to do for me?"
He already had the NIOSH Sound Level Meter app.
He picked white noise on the Hatch. It read about 57.5–57.9 dB, and the ringing was still very prominent over it.
