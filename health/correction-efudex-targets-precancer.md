# Efudex/calcipotriene reacts where precancer is, not where it isn't

**What was wrong:** Scott wrote that the doctor said Efudex/calcipotriene only hits "the spots with no pre-cancer" and there's no reaction otherwise.

**Correct version:** The reaction occurs at spots with precancer (including unseen ones); healthy skin is largely spared but can still get mild irritation.

**Approx date:** September 2026

**Source:** Skin Cancer, 2026-09-25
