# Religious views

Having traveled the world and seen many people, Scott can't believe in any version of the gods he has been exposed to.
He believes nothing waits after death (no heaven, virgins, or reincarnation).
Sometimes he is jealous of people with unwavering faith in an afterlife; he thinks it's not true but maybe ignorance is bliss.
Many people are jealous of the career that let him travel the world.
