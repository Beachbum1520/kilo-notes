# Chose topical over oral DHT blocker for hair loss

**Decided:** Scott wants a topical DHT blocker (topical dutasteride), not oral finasteride/dutasteride.

**Why:** After asking whether side-effect risk is the same for oral and topical, and hearing that topical carries lower systemic risk, he said "Then I want topical."

**Rejected alternatives:** Oral finasteride/dutasteride (higher systemic exposure/side-effect risk), even though oral is retail-fillable at CVS and Claude suggested starting with oral finasteride 1mg as the most-studied option.
