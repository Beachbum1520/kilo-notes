# TRT dosing history

Old protocol: one vial (1 mL of testosterone cypionate 200 mg/mL) per week for 3 weeks, then 1 week off.
On the old protocol Scott always did bloodwork at the end of the off week, knowing levels would be lower. He did this largely so insurance would keep funding the injections.
Current protocol: a daily micro-dose. He was at 10 units/day. When labs came in hot he dropped to 5, which made levels too low, then went up to 7. Since his last labs he has gone back to 10 units.

**Approx date:** June 2026

**Source:** blood work analysis, 2026-06-21
