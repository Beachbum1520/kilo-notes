# Keep DEXA / VO2max / RMR testing at Life Fit Labs rather than bundling with the concierge doctor

**Decided:** Scott does not need Wellspring MD for DEXA and VO2max testing; he keeps that testing with his existing provider, Life Fit Labs (Mike), and shares the reports with his PCP.

**Why:** Life Fit already holds his historical data and he believed it was cheaper. He briefly considered consolidating everything with the PCP ("maybe there is value in having all with the PCP") and offered to give Dr. Bailey copies of his historical tests. It later turned out that Dr. Bailey himself uses Mike at Life Fit for DEXA/VO2, so continuity is preserved either way.

**Rejected alternatives:** Moving DEXA/VO2/RMR into a higher Wellspring membership tier (Balance/Precision/Performance) that bundles those tests.

**Would revisit if:** Consolidating testing through the PCP turns out to be a better deal. [UNCERTAIN]
