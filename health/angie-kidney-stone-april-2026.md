# Angie's kidney stone and nephrostomy (April 2026)

Angie's symptoms began at 2 AM on Thursday, April 16, 2026, the day Zack was discharged. She had severe flank pain and vomiting.
Before this she had been to two ERs and an urgent care, and none of them diagnosed the kidney stones.
Scott took her to the LaGrange ER, the same one Zack started at. A CT showed a 12mm kidney stone "pretty high up." She also had a UTI and was treated with morphine and Zofran.
The urologist planned a PCNL, but the infection was too bad. They placed a nephrostomy tube instead and kept her until the infection came down, with the PCNL to be rescheduled.
She came home with the bag and tube still coming out of her back.
She may have had a stone 29 years ago while pregnant with their daughter; a stent was placed then, but stones were never confirmed.
