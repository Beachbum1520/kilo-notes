# Views on aging and longevity

Scott is frustrated that things keep breaking as he ages: eyes, year-over-year muscle and bone density loss, more gray and thinning hair.
He would love to live as long as he wants; dropping dead on the trail at 92 is his current best-case scenario, but it still "sucks."
He asked about the singularity and uploading consciousness into a VR world.
He agrees "a perfect copy of you is not you"; he always thought Star Trek transporters were murdering people week after week.
He describes his feeling as a form of FOMO: amazed at the change since he was born (cell phones, internet, self-driving cars, AI), and it's just getting really good while he is past the halfway mark.
He gets a dull pain in his chest whenever he thinks about his end, which is often.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** Correcting presbyopia without compromising distance vision, 2026-05-19
