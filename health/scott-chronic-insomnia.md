# Scott's chronic insomnia

Scott has been waking in the middle of the night (around 3–4 AM) for a year or more. It is not new or crisis-driven.
A sleep study ruled out sleep apnea.
He has had no alcohol in months; in the past year or more there was one night with two drinks.
He has had no medical workup for it, only his own investigation.
The wakes feel like a wired, alert physical state, not a racing mind. He thinks about work and other things but wouldn't call it racing.
He tracks sleep with an Oura ring, which he says tracks pretty well.
