# Longevity goal: don't die early

Scott's stated goal: "My goal is to not die." Everything else (training, job, wife, girlfriend, farm, adopted kids) "can be changed, or eliminated if necessary."
His dad died at 68 of a heart attack. His dad smoked, ate a bad diet, and was inactive. Scott doesn't expect to follow him for those reasons, but he worries about early death if he doesn't make changes.
He has lived his life for others for so long that he longs for the time he can live for himself, once the adoptees are gone. He doesn't want to reach that point and die a year or two later. He wants as many healthy, functional years as possible to enjoy life.
