# Widex Relax program and the tinnitus app

His Widex aids have a "Relax" setting for tinnitus that plays random fractal tones, and Widex has a tinnitus app that plays background noises.
Years ago, he used to play "birds by the pond" loud enough to mostly drown out the ringing.
