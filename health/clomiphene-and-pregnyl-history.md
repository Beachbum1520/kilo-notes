# Clomiphene and Pregnyl history

Scott takes clomiphene citrate 25mg (label: 25 days on / 5 off); he is on his 2nd bottle.
He had quit taking it for a while.
His doctor put him on Pregnyl for 60 days for testicular atrophy; it worked well.
Clomiphene was prescribed to maintain afterwards; Scott told his doctor it wasn't worth the effort and stopped.
His doctor advised him to continue it for LH and FSH.
His doctor thinks the suppressed LH/FSH on labs was because he quit taking it.
