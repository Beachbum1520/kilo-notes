# Watts Way Farms herd and land

The farm is 90 acres and raises Dexter cattle.
The herd was 77 head in April 2026: a herd sire, 8 steers from the 2023 calf crop, the full 2024 and 2025 calf crops, 15 calves so far in 2026, and the rest cows and heifers.
They process at about 29 months, have never sold live animals, and sell only as beef.
The land is not overstocked and could carry more. Except in drought, there is more grass than the cattle can eat.
From late March to about Oct/Nov, the only animals fed hay are the steers being finished.
