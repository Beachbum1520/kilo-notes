# Forgot the aids after a shower

On Aug 10, 2026, he took the aids out to shower, forgot to put them back in, and went out with his wife and kids. For the rest of the day his hearing was much worse and the ringing much louder.
His take: "Feels to me like my brain and ears is getting lazy then."
