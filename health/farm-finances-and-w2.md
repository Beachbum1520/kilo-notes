# Farm economics and income

The farm loses money every year. Scott said the profit shows up on his tax return, against about $300k in W-2 income, which is "jumping significantly" in 2026.
The farm gets NRCS EQIP contracts. The current one is about $70k, and they are looking at a possible ~$200k contract in 2026. Scott calls it "like free money." He sees it as adding significant value for the farm's eventual resale.
