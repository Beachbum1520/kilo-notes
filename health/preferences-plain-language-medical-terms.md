# Use plain language for medical terms he'll say out loud

When drafting things Scott will say to a doctor, use plain everyday wording he can say naturally (e.g., "thick blood" instead of "erythrocytosis"; "How many patients do you take on total, and how close to full are you?" instead of "How many patients in the panel"). Explain unfamiliar lab abbreviations (SHBG, LH, CBC, PSA, etc.) rather than assuming he knows them.

**Source:** Finding the right concierge doctor service, 2026-06-23
