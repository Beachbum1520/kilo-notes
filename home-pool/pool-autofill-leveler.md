# Installed an above-deck pool auto-fill float leveler

**Decided:** Get a pool-filling float valve and leave the hose running to it; installed a deck-mount auto water leveler.

**Why:** Keeps the pool at a constant level with a continuous trickle instead of periodic multi-inch fills.

**Rejected alternatives:** Skimmer-mounted float valve — would require busting up some of the concrete pad around the pool.

**Would revisit if:** unknown

**Approx date:** July–August 2026
