# Pool skimmer and circulation layout

The skimmer box has one hole at the bottom; water enters there, runs under the concrete pool deck and the gazebo to the single 1.5" PVC that goes into the pump.
The skimmer is plumbed from the bottom and the skimmer box is surrounded by the concrete deck.
After the pump the water returns to the pool via two return jets.
The skimmer has no weir flap.
The filter sits just behind the gazebo, as seen from the skimmer box.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** Diagnosing suction-side air leak in pump, 2026-05-12
