# Late-July 2.5-inch slow fill

Measured with a tape: planned to add 2.5 inches.
Started around 7:49 PM; by 9:38 AM next morning about 1 inch still to go.
He had been gone for two weeks; water was at the bottom of the skimmer opening, so he had no choice but to fill.
Pool developed a green tint with about 1/4 inch left to go.
He dosed 2 quarts of Purple Stuff after the tint appeared (more than the 1.5 Claude suggested); 1 quart had been dosed pre-fill.
