# Cold plunge hoses use PVC true union ball valves

**Decided:** Replace the quick connects with 1/2" Sch 80 PVC true union ball valves (threaded end connectors, which came in the kit) screwed onto the tub stubs, plus stainless MAACFLOW 1/2" NPT male × 3/4" hose barbs (2-pack under $10), ear clamps, PTFE tape.

**Why:** Scott liked the idea of a union with an on/off valve to hold water in the tub while servicing the chiller; stainless barb chosen because the PVC option he found was ambiguously sized and the stainless 2-pack was cheap.

**Rejected alternatives:** Keeping the quick connects; direct GHT threading (hard to twist the stiff hose); camlocks; PVC barb (listing size ambiguous).

**Would revisit if:** unknown.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Cold plunge quick connect leaking and losing suction, 2026-08-02
