# Live with the leak and cycle the pump via timer

**Decided:** Scott chose to live with the suction leak and use the manual timer with multiple on/off clips to turn the pump off and back on several times a day.

**Why:** Turning it off and back on fixes it for a few hours; it's cheap and requires no demolition.

**Rejected alternatives:** Dropping a flexible suction line down through the top of the skimmer ("would look like shit"); tearing out gazebo/concrete to bypass or repair the buried line.

**Would revisit if:** unknown (not stated by Scott).

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** Diagnosing suction-side air leak in pump, 2026-05-12
