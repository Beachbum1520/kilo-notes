# Pool product verdicts

Skim-A-Round: scam, avoid.
WaterGuru Sense 2: legit but $400+ subscription, redundant with Taylor kit.
Bettabot Betta SE Plus (~$400): recommended for tree-covered pool, post-Philippines purchase.
Pool Day "Simple Scoop": avoid (copper sulfate stains vinyl).
Champion 12.5%: legit but $12.82/gal Amazon vs $7.13/gal Leslie's.
BioGuard Burn Out, Smart Shock, Banish, Back Up: unnecessary upsells.
Clear Aid (SMARTZyme enzyme): needs FC <5 ppm + 48 hr no-shock; save for post-SLAM.
