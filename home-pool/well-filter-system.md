# Well water treatment setup

Scott's well setup: a large black tank (Fusion 2 by Vitasalus) that removes the iron, plus smaller cartridge filters and a UV unit.
The smaller filters are not cheap; the dealer who sold him the system says they only need replacing once a year.
The big black tank is what removes iron, not the smaller filters.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** OLD - Opening Pool for Summer, 2026-05-10
