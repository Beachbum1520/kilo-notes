# Pool pump suction plumbing layout

The pump suction entry is 1.5" PVC glued to a 1.5" PVC threaded adapter that screws into the pump; the PVC is glued to the adapter and won't twist off, so removing it means cutting.
Going backwards from the pump intake: the PVC runs straight a few inches, then a 90 into the ground, then another 90 below ground to point toward the pool.
Scott already replaced the above-ground 90 closest to the pump himself.
