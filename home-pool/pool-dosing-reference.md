# Dosing reference for this pool (26,659 gal)

1 lb cal-hypo (73%) raises FC ~3 ppm.
1 gal 12.5% liquid chlorine raises FC ~6 ppm; 1 gal 10% raises ~5 ppm.
1 cup muriatic acid drops pH ~0.4; 1.5 cups ~0.8.
1 lb soda ash (~2 cups) raises pH meaningfully; 5 lbs at once overshoots.
1 lb CYA raises CYA ~3.6 ppm.
1.5 lbs baking soda raises TA ~10 ppm.
