# Cold plunge hose quick connects leaking air

Scott had put garden-hose quick connects (M MINGLE GHT) on the plunge hoses to make removal for cleaning easier. They worked for a while, then dripped and let in air, leaving the filter canister running nearly empty.
The tape on the hose end only holds the pipe insulation — not a seal.
Tub port is a 1/2" male NPT stub; existing adapter was 3/4" GHT female × 1/2" NPT female; hoses have 3/4" barbs.
Removing the quick connect and swivel made the hose hard to twist on and off.
