# Well water is very high in iron

Scott's well water has very, very heavy iron.
He has a high-end filter system at the well, but when filling the pool the water moves faster than the filter can handle.
When he tops off the pool, chlorine oxidizes the iron and the water turns super cloudy.
In past summers, when topping off, he had to add Jack's Magic The Pink Stuff to the water.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** OLD - Opening Pool for Summer, 2026-05-10
