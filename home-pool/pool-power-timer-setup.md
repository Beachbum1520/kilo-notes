# Pool pump power and timer setup

Power runs to a timer/switch at the pad, then to the pool motor through flex conduit with weatherproof joints at each end.
The pump runs on a manual (mechanical) timer with on/off clips; Scott currently has two sets of on/off clips.

**Approx date:** May 2026
