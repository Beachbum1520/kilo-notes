# Chlorinator cell iron deposits and acid soak

**Decided:** Acid-soak the CircuPool cell after hose spray didn't remove the orange-brown deposits; soak the whole cell standing in a 5-gallon bucket since he had no cap, using ~3 gallons of 4:1 water:muriatic acid.

**Why:** Scott figured it probably needed descaling anyway and wanted to do it all at once; hose spraying produced no change.

**Rejected alternatives:** Hose-only / plastic shim cleaning (tried hose, no change); capped cell soak (no cap).
