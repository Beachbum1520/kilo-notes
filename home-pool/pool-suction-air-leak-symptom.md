# Pool pump sucking air: symptom pattern

The pool pump has an air leak somewhere on the suction side; bubbles visible at the SWG cell. Nothing obvious.
If the pump is turned off for a few minutes and back on, it works pretty well for an extended period (not perfect), then progressively sucks more air and less water until it's barely moving any water.
Water level at the skimmer is fine (not dropping below the skimmer opening).

**Approx date:** May 2026
