# First pool store purchase

Scott described the pool store bill as almost $600 in chemicals based on their test of his water.
The receipt he supplied totaled $506.13.
Items included 12 x 1 lb Power Powder Plus (cal-hypo), Green to Clean, Clear Aid, two No Phos, two 5 lb soda ash, Hardness Plus 25 lb, 2 gal muriatic acid, Cal-Chlor flakes 50 lb, and 40 lb pool salt bags.
A small empty bottle was included for Scott to bring a water sample next time.
The staff member told him the powder (cal-hypo) was better than liquid chlorine because it is more concentrated, sold him a product to clear the water after the cal-hypo clouds it, and said Hardness Plus would keep his steps and handrail from tarnishing, which they have done in the past.
