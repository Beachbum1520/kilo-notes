# Pump wouldn't prime — suction union O-ring missing

After the repair, the pump ran 4 minutes without moving water; Scott poured ~14 gallons into the pump and suction line over two fills.
Pump lid O-ring was fine; gauge read 0; no air from the gauge port on Filter; impeller turned with the shaft; motor sounded normal; suction line held water.
Cause found by Scott: the union on the front (suction side) of the pump wasn't tight — the entire O-ring had fallen out. After fixing, water flowed.
The discharge union (the one broken for the test) was leaking a little afterward.
Scott's filter setup has no dedicated air relief valve as far as he can tell.

**Approx date:** July 2026

**Source:** Removing a broken PVC adapter from valve, 2026-07-31
