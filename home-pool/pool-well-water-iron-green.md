# Well water iron turns pool green when added

Last summer, whenever he added well water to the pool it turned green; he added a couple of bottles of Pink Stuff and in a day or two the water was crystal clear.
He attributed the May 18 green tint after a gallon of chlorine to iron from the well water.
He attributed the July 3 olive-green event to iron oxidation from adding well water.

**Approx date:** May–July 2026

**Source:** OLD  - Opening pool for summer, 2026-05-17
