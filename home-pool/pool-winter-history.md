# Pool winter-closing history

Last winter Scott turned the pump and filter off in October, covered the pool, and waited until May; the pool opened green.
The winter before last he did not cover the pool and kept it running all winter; it was a real pain because of leaves and pine straw getting in the pool.
Scott believes there is a better approach than turning everything off in October, covering, and waiting until May.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** OLD - Opening Pool for Summer, 2026-05-10
