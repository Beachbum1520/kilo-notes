# Pool and plunge test kit

Scott uses a Leslie's Total Poolcare DPD test kit (18-576), a comparator-block kit — not a K-2005/K-2006 FAS-DPD drop-count kit and not OTO.
For high FC readings he does a 50/50 dilution and doubles the reading.
He tests both the pool and the cold plunge with this kit.

**Approx date:** August 2026

**Source:** Cold plunge chlorine and borax dosing, 2026-08-12
