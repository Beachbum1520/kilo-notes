# Daughter's house had a cracked sewer line repaired with a pipe liner

Scott's daughter had a cracked sewage line in the foundation of her house.
Instead of ripping up the house, the repair company blew a pipe liner through the entire line that unfolded as it went — a pipe within the pipe.
