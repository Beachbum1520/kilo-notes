# Pool cover brass anchor screws fused

Last year some of the brass screw-in plugs the cover hooks to fused in place; Scott had a hard time getting them off and had to replace at least one.
Scott bought and applied anti-seize (from Home Depot) to the brass screws before screwing them down for the summer.
