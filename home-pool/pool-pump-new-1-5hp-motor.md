# New 1.5HP motor installed before pool opening

Before the pool "opening ceremonies" in spring 2026, Scott installed a new 1.5HP motor on the wet end of his pump.
The same week he replumbed the pump suction with 1.5" flex PVC.
The threaded adapter at the pump suction was installed when he installed the pump (about 24 hours before this conversation).
He sealed the threaded adapter with more than enough Teflon tape, pipe dope, and silicone sealant on the outside.
