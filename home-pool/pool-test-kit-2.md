# Pool test kit and testing method

Scott's kit is the Leslie's Total Poolcare DPD test kit (comparator-block style; Taylor K-2005 equivalent).
For FC he follows the kit's yellow-section steps: 5 drops R-0001 and 5 drops R-0002, then match the color — on a 50/50 dilution during SLAM and doubling the result.
He tests pH on straight pool water.
He reads the comparator colors himself and reports his own read.
