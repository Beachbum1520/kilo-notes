# Home pool basic setup

Scott has an in-ground saltwater pool at home.
Scott initially estimated the pool at about 25,500 gallons.
The pool store (Leslie's) water analysis put the pool at 26,659 gallons.
The pool has a vinyl liner, which Scott says is about 4 years old.
The pool has no main drain; it has a pool light.
The filter is a sand filter with a multiport valve (backwash/rinse).
The salt water generator is a CircuPool unit; it has a Super Chlorinate feature and a Cell Maint indicator.
The cleaner is a robotic Pool Shark; it is the only vacuum Scott has.
The house is on well water.
The pool has a mesh safety cover that hooks to small brass screw-in anchor plugs in the deck.

**Approx date:** May 2026

**Source:** OLD - Opening Pool for Summer, 2026-05-10
