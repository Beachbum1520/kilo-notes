# Fill pool slowly so the well filter can work

**Decided:** Go "stupid slow" out of the faucet when topping off, using the hose iron pre-filter; let the late-July fill run low and slow rather than speeding it up.

**Why:** The slower the fill, the better chance the filter on the well does its job. Also wanted to avoid the iron storm and overuse of expensive chemicals.

**Rejected alternatives:** Increasing flow to finish faster; stopping at 2 inches.

**Would revisit if:** unknown

**Approx date:** July 2026

**Source:** OLD  - Opening pool for summer, 2026-05-17
